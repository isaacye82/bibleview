创世记 40章 Genesis Chapter 40

本章核心主题 Core Theme

被遗忘的监牢与神隐藏的预备

A Forgotten Prison and God’s Hidden Preparation

创世记40章延续约瑟在监中的故事。酒政和膳长的梦把约瑟带到法老宫廷的边缘，但他仍没有立刻脱离困境。本章的重点不是约瑟技巧性地预测未来，而是他在受限环境中继续关心人、承认解释属乎神，并在被人遗忘后仍被神保守在预备的道路上。

Genesis 40 continues Joseph’s life in prison. The cupbearer’s and baker’s dreams move him near Pharaoh’s court, yet his release does not come immediately. The chapter is not mainly about Joseph’s skill in prediction; it shows him caring for others, confessing that interpretation belongs to God, and being kept by God even after human memory fails.

酒政与膳长

Cupbearer and Baker

mashqeh / opheh

两位近臣被下在约瑟所在的监里，显示宫廷权力也会突然失稳。神把约瑟放在人的失落与惧怕旁边，使他学习在低处服事。 Two royal servants fall from favor, and Joseph learns to serve beside anxious people in a low place.

解梦属乎神

Interpretations Belong to God

pithronim l’Elohim

约瑟没有把洞察归给自己，而把解释的权柄归给神。他的谦卑使恩赐不成为自我抬高，而成为安慰与警醒人的服事。 Joseph treats insight as God’s gift, not personal leverage, so wisdom becomes service.

三日的记号

The Sign of Three Days

sheloshet yamim

两个梦都以“三日”为时间框架，却通向不同结局。经文提醒人生命在神手中，消息可能甜美也可能严肃。 The same three-day frame leads to opposite outcomes, placing human destiny before God.

记念与忘记

Remembering and Forgetting

zakar / shakach

约瑟请求酒政记念他，却被遗忘。人的忘记没有取消神的护理，只把读者带入更深的等候。 Human forgetfulness does not cancel God’s providence; it deepens the waiting.

创世记40章逐段双轨研读

A bilingual two-track study through Genesis Chapter 40

本章以两个梦推动约瑟故事，却把焦点放在神的解释、人的记念与忘记、以及等待中的忠心。研读轨道观察叙事结构和关键词，灵修轨道把“在被遗忘中仍服事”连接到今日的工作、关系与信心操练。 This chapter moves Joseph’s story through two dreams, focusing on God’s interpretation, human remembering and forgetting, and faithfulness in waiting. The study track observes structure and key words; the devotional track connects forgotten service to work, relationships, and faith today.

40:1-4

法老的酒政和膳长得罪了埃及王，被关在护卫长府内、约瑟所在的监里。护卫长把他们交给约瑟，约瑟便服事他们；他们在监里过了一段日子。

Pharaoh’s cupbearer and baker offend the king of Egypt and are confined in the same prison where Joseph is held. The captain of the guard assigns them to Joseph, and he serves them for some time.

经文含义 Meaning

宫廷危机进入监牢

经文把两个宫廷人物带进约瑟的空间，显示神的预备常借普通安排推进。约瑟仍是囚犯，却被托付照管别人；他没有因自己的冤屈停止服事。

The narrative brings two royal officials into Joseph’s confined world. God’s preparation advances through ordinary assignments: Joseph remains a prisoner, yet he is entrusted with care for others.

灵修应用 Devotional

在受限处境中仍然服事

现代人也会在不理想的岗位、家庭压力或等待期中感到被困。桥梁原则是：神常在我们尚未脱困时训练我们的忠心。今天可以先照顾眼前被托付的人，而不是等环境完美才开始顺服。

We may feel confined by work, family pressure, or prolonged waiting. The bridge principle is that God often trains faithfulness before release comes. Care for the people entrusted to you now, not only after circumstances improve.

40:5-8

酒政和膳长同夜各作一梦，各梦都有不同含义。早晨约瑟见他们面带愁容，就询问原因。他们说无人能解梦；约瑟回答说，解梦不是出于神吗？请把梦告诉我。

Both officials dream on the same night, each dream carrying its own meaning. Joseph notices their troubled faces and asks why. When they say there is no interpreter, Joseph replies that interpretations belong to God and invites them to tell him.

经文含义 Meaning

从观察愁容到承认神

约瑟先注意人的脸色，再谈属神的解释。这里的属灵成熟不是冷漠地说正确答案，而是在怜悯中把人的困惑带到神面前。

Joseph notices their distress before speaking of God’s interpretation. Mature faith is not detached correctness; it brings human confusion before God with compassion.

灵修应用 Devotional

看见别人脸上的重担

职场和教会中，人常把焦虑藏在表情和沉默里。桥梁原则是：敬畏神的人不只追求完成任务，也学习看见人。一个简单的询问，可能成为别人重新面对神的入口。

In workplaces and churches, anxiety often appears in silence or expression. The bridge principle: people who fear God do more than finish tasks; they learn to notice people. A simple question may open space for someone to face God again.

40:9-13

酒政述说梦见葡萄树有三根枝子，发芽、开花、结出熟葡萄；他把葡萄挤在法老杯里，递在法老手中。约瑟解释三枝就是三天，三天内法老必抬举他，使他官复原职。

The cupbearer dreams of a vine with three branches that bud, blossom, and bear grapes. He presses the grapes into Pharaoh’s cup and places the cup in Pharaoh’s hand. Joseph explains that the three branches mean three days, after which Pharaoh will lift up his head and restore him.

经文含义 Meaning

恢复原职的梦

葡萄树从发芽到结果，画出生命恢复的速度与完整性。酒政再次把杯递给法老，表示他将回到原来的职分。约瑟清楚传达好消息，却仍把解释归于神。

The vine’s quick movement from budding to fruitfulness pictures restoration. The cupbearer serving Pharaoh again signals return to office. Joseph delivers good news while keeping interpretation under God’s authority.

灵修应用 Devotional

收到好消息时仍谦卑

当机会重新打开，容易只看见自己的翻身。桥梁原则是：恢复也是托付。若神使某个关系、职位或机会重新开始，我们应以感恩和忠心承接，而不是把恩典变成自我夸耀。

When opportunity reopens, it is easy to see only personal comeback. The bridge principle: restoration is stewardship. If God restores a role, relationship, or opportunity, receive it with gratitude and faithfulness, not self-display.

40:14-15

约瑟请求酒政得好处时记念他，在法老面前提说他，救他出这监牢；因为他是从希伯来人之地被拐来的，在这里也没有做该下监的事。

Joseph asks the cupbearer to remember him when things go well, to mention him to Pharaoh and help bring him out of prison. He explains that he was taken from the land of the Hebrews and has done nothing deserving confinement.

经文含义 Meaning

清白者的正当请求

约瑟表达自己的冤屈，却没有扭曲事实或报复他人。他的请求说明信靠神不排除正当地寻求公义；等候神也可以包含合宜地开口。

Joseph states his injustice without distorting facts or seeking revenge. Trusting God does not exclude appropriate appeals for justice; waiting can include honest speech.

灵修应用 Devotional

诚实表达需要

有些人把属灵忍耐误解为永远沉默。桥梁原则是：信心可以诚实说出伤害、需要和合理请求，同时把结果交给神。今天面对不公时，可以清楚表达事实，而不让苦毒控制语气。

Some mistake spiritual patience for permanent silence. The bridge principle: faith can name hurt, need, and a reasonable request while entrusting outcomes to God. In injustice, speak facts clearly without letting bitterness govern your tone.

40:16-19

膳长见解释吉利，也述说自己梦见头上有三筐白饼，最上筐有为法老烤的食物，却有飞鸟来吃。约瑟解释三筐就是三天，三天内法老要处决他，飞鸟要吃他的肉。

Seeing the favorable interpretation, the baker tells his dream: three baskets of baked goods are on his head, and birds eat from the top basket. Joseph explains that the three baskets mean three days, after which Pharaoh will execute him and birds will eat his flesh.

经文含义 Meaning

严肃信息也要忠实传达

两个梦结构相似，结局却相反。约瑟没有为了讨好而改轻信息；真解释必须按神所显明的传达。经文让读者感受生命结局的重量。

The two dreams have similar structures but opposite outcomes. Joseph does not soften the message to please the hearer; faithful interpretation says what God has made clear. The text presses the seriousness of human destiny.

灵修应用 Devotional

不把真理改成讨喜的话

在关系和牧养场景中，我们容易只说对方想听的内容。桥梁原则是：爱不是回避严肃真相，而是用敬畏神的心诚实说话。真诚、温柔和清楚可以同时存在。

In relationships and pastoral care, we may say only what others want to hear. The bridge principle: love does not avoid serious truth; it speaks honestly before God. Sincerity, gentleness, and clarity can belong together.

40:20-23

第三天是法老的生日，他为臣仆设宴，把酒政和膳长带出来。酒政官复原职，膳长被处死，正如约瑟所解释的。然而酒政不记念约瑟，竟忘了他。

On the third day, Pharaoh’s birthday, he holds a feast and brings out the cupbearer and baker. The cupbearer is restored, and the baker is executed, just as Joseph interpreted. Yet the cupbearer does not remember Joseph; he forgets him.

经文含义 Meaning

应验之后仍要等候

梦的应验证明解释确实来自神，但约瑟的处境没有立即改变。结尾的“忘了他”制造张力：人的记忆失败，神的故事却还没有结束。

The fulfillment confirms that the interpretation came from God, yet Joseph’s circumstances do not change at once. The final note of forgetfulness creates tension: human memory fails, but God’s story is not finished.

灵修应用 Devotional

被遗忘时仍在神眼前

许多服事、善行和忠心不会马上被人看见，甚至被受益者忘记。桥梁原则是：人的遗忘不是神的遗忘。今天可以把失望带到神面前，继续在小事上忠心，因为神的时间表比人的感谢更可靠。

Much service, goodness, and faithfulness may go unnoticed or even be forgotten by those helped. The bridge principle: human forgetfulness is not divine forgetfulness. Bring disappointment to God and remain faithful in small things, because God’s timing is more reliable than human gratitude.

方法论 Methodology

从经文原意走向今日应用

Moving from the text’s original meaning to present-day application

正统解经路径 Exegetical Path

先观察叙事位置：创世记40章在约瑟被卖、被囚之后，在被高举之前。再观察重复与对比：两个官员、两个梦、两个“三日”、两个结局，以及“记念/忘记”的张力。最后把约瑟的解释放在“解梦属乎神”的宣告之下，避免把本章读成个人成功学。

Begin with narrative location: Genesis 40 stands after Joseph’s enslavement and imprisonment and before his elevation. Then observe repetition and contrast: two officials, two dreams, two three-day periods, two outcomes, and the tension between remembering and forgetting. Read Joseph’s interpretations under his confession that interpretation belongs to God, not as a manual for personal success.

灵修应用路径 Devotional Path

应用时先找桥梁原则：神看见被囚的人，使用人在低处的服事，也容许等候延长。再落到具体生活：在职场被忽略时仍忠心，在别人愁苦时主动关心，在不公中诚实表达请求，在被忘记时把盼望放回神的时间。

For application, identify bridge principles: God sees the confined, uses service in low places, and may allow waiting to continue. Then bring them into concrete life: remain faithful when overlooked at work, notice the troubled, speak honestly in injustice, and place hope back in God’s timing when forgotten.

实际操练 Practice

今天可以用三个问题默想本章：我身边是否有人“面带愁容”等待被看见？我是否把自己的恩赐归给神并用来服事人？当人忘记我的付出时，我是否仍相信神没有忘记？

Pray through three questions: Who near me looks troubled and needs to be noticed? Am I receiving my gifts as God’s and using them to serve? When people forget my service, do I still trust that God remembers?
