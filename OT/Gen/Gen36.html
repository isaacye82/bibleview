创世记 36章 Genesis Chapter 36

本章核心主题 Core Theme

以东的家谱：世代展开与应许主线的分辨

Edom’s Genealogy: Generations Unfolding and the Covenant Line Discerned

创世记36章集中记录以扫及其后裔，从迦南迁往西珥，到族长、诸王和首领的出现。本章不是无关紧要的名单，而是在雅各叙事之后提醒读者：神也看见以扫家族的扩展；同时，外在繁荣、地土拥有和政治组织并不自动等同于承受盟约应许。

Genesis 36 records Esau and his descendants, from the move out of Canaan to Seir to the rise of chiefs, kings, and clan leaders. The chapter is not a disposable list; it shows that God sees Esau’s expanding household while also distinguishing visible prosperity from the covenant promise carried forward through Jacob.

以扫就是以东

Esau, That Is Edom

edom

本章把以扫的别名“以东”和西珥山地连在一起，说明雅各故事之外也有真实民族历史。神的护理并不只记录盟约主线，也照见旁支家族的兴起。 The chapter links Esau, Edom, and Seir, showing that God’s providence also observes the side branches of family history.

分别中的和平

Peace Through Separation

shalom

以扫因财物甚多离开雅各，呼应亚伯拉罕与罗得的分开。分离在这里不是单纯破裂，而是为避免冲突、让各自道路显明。 Separation is not merely rupture; it can restrain rivalry and reveal each household’s path.

族长与君王

Chiefs and Kings

alluph

以东很早出现族长和诸王，外在组织看似快速成熟；但创世记让读者分辨，政治强盛不等于承受应许。 Edom’s early chiefs and kings display public strength, yet visible power is not the same as covenant inheritance.

家谱中的记念

Memory in Genealogy

toledot

长串名字提醒我们，神看见每个家族与世代。即使这些名字不处在叙事中心，它们仍被纳入神掌管历史的见证。 Even names outside the narrative center are held within God’s governance of history.

创世记36章逐段双轨研读

A bilingual two-track study through Genesis Chapter 36

本章主要是家谱，却不是信息贫乏的附录。研读时要同时观察名字、地名、族长和君王的排列，也要分辨创世记如何把以东的真实兴起放在雅各盟约主线旁边。 This chapter is genealogy, but not an empty appendix. We observe names, places, chiefs, and kings while discerning how Genesis places Edom’s real rise beside Jacob’s covenant line.

36:1-8

这是以扫的后代，以扫就是以东。以扫带着妻子、儿女和一切财物离开雅各，往别处去，因为二人的财物群畜甚多，寄居之地容不下他们。于是以扫住在西珥山里。

These are the generations of Esau, that is, Edom. Esau took his wives, children, household, livestock, and possessions away from Jacob, because their goods were too great for them to dwell together. So Esau lived in Mount Seir.

经文含义 Meaning

从同住到分开

开头用“后代”标记一个新的家族段落，并反复说明以扫就是以东。分开的直接原因是财物和牲畜太多，土地承载不了两个家族。这与亚伯拉罕、罗得分开相呼应，显示神允许家族各走其路，同时保留雅各承受迦南应许的主线。

The opening formula marks a new family record and identifies Esau with Edom. The separation is caused by abundance and limited space, echoing Abraham and Lot. God permits distinct paths while preserving Jacob’s line in relation to the promise of Canaan.

灵修应用 Devotional

不把分开等同失败

有些关系的健康需要清楚边界。职场、家庭或服事中，当资源、方向和责任持续冲突时，敬虔的处理不一定是勉强同住，而是在诚实、和平和守约中给彼此空间。关键不是赢过对方，而是在自己的道路上继续敬畏神。

Some relationships need clear boundaries to remain healthy. In work, family, or ministry, faithful action may not mean forcing closeness, but creating honest and peaceful space. The goal is not to defeat the other person, but to walk your own path before God.

36:9-14

以扫住在西珥山里；这是以东人始祖以扫的后代。经文列出以扫众子的名字，也记下亚大、巴实抹和阿何利巴玛所生的后裔。

These are the generations of Esau, the father of the Edomites in Mount Seir. The text names the sons and descendants born through Adah, Basemath, and Oholibamah.

经文含义 Meaning

家谱确认真实历史

这些名字把以扫从个人故事推进到民族源头。“以东人始祖”说明一个人的选择会在后代中展开。经文没有把旁支历史抹去，而是让读者看见以色列周边民族也在神掌管的历史中形成。

The names move Esau from individual narrative to national origin. Calling him the father of the Edomites shows how one life unfolds into generations. Scripture does not erase the side branch; it locates neighboring peoples within God’s governed history.

灵修应用 Devotional

看重不在中心的人

我们常只关注“主角”，却忽略办公室、家庭和教会里那些不在聚光灯下的人。神记念名字，提醒我们不要用影响力大小决定人的价值。今天可以主动看见一个平常被忽略的人，用尊重和具体关心回应他。

We often notice only the apparent main characters and miss people outside the spotlight. God’s naming of generations warns us not to measure worth by visibility. Today, choose to notice someone easily overlooked and respond with concrete respect.

36:15-19

以扫子孙中作族长的记在这里：从以利法、流珥、耶乌施、雅兰、可拉等支派出来的族长，都是以东地以扫子孙中的首领。

These are the chiefs among Esau’s descendants: leaders from the lines of Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah, chiefs in the land of Edom.

经文含义 Meaning

族长制度的兴起

“族长”强调部族组织和社会秩序。以东在外在结构上发展迅速，有支派、有首领、有地界。创世记没有否认这种兴盛，却把它放在盟约故事旁边，让人分辨组织成熟与属灵承受之间的差别。

The title “chiefs” points to tribal organization and social order. Edom develops visible structure with clans, leaders, and land. Genesis does not deny this success, but places it beside the covenant story so readers can distinguish social maturity from covenant inheritance.

灵修应用 Devotional

能力不是身份的根基

职位、资源和组织能力都是真实恩赐，但不能成为身份根基。现代人很容易把头衔当成安全感，把团队规模当成价值。经文邀请我们善用责任，却不让责任取代与神的关系。

Roles, resources, and organizational skill can be real gifts, but they cannot become the foundation of identity. It is easy to treat titles as security and scale as worth. This passage invites responsible leadership without letting responsibility replace relationship with God.

36:20-30

经文又记下那地原有的何利人西珥子孙，包括罗坍、朔巴、祭便、亚拿、底顺、以察、底珊等族长。

The text also records the sons of Seir the Horite, the earlier inhabitants of the land: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, Dishan, and their chiefs.

经文含义 Meaning

进入已有历史的土地

以东的地并非空白地带，那里已有何利人西珥的家族与族长。经文把原住族群列出，说明民族形成常包含迁徙、融合与取代。圣经的历史观具体而诚实，不把地土简化成抽象地图。

Edom’s land was not an empty stage; Seir’s Horite families were already there. By naming them, the text shows that nations form through migration, contact, and replacement. Biblical history is concrete and honest, not an abstract map.

灵修应用 Devotional

尊重别人走过的路

我们进入新岗位、新城市或新群体时，那里早有故事、劳苦和伤痕。属灵成熟的人不会把一切当作自己从零开始的舞台，而会先倾听、学习、尊重前人的界线，再承担自己的责任。

When we enter a new role, city, or community, stories and wounds already exist there. Spiritual maturity does not treat every place as a blank stage. It listens, learns, respects prior boundaries, and then carries responsibility wisely.

36:31-39

以色列人未有君王治理以先，在以东地作王的记在下面：比拉、约巴、户珊、哈达、桑拉、扫罗、巴勒哈南、哈达等相继作王。

Before any king reigned over the children of Israel, kings reigned in the land of Edom: Bela, Jobab, Husham, Hadad, Samlah, Shaul, Baal-hanan, and Hadar in succession.

经文含义 Meaning

以东先有君王

经文特别说明，以色列未有君王以前，以东已经有王。这句话让读者看见外邦制度可能比应许子民更早成熟。神的应许不总按人的速度显现；雅各后裔仍在等待中，但等待不等于神失信。

The text notes that Edom had kings before Israel did. A neighboring nation may develop institutions earlier than the people of promise. God’s promise does not always appear on human timelines; waiting does not mean God has failed.

灵修应用 Devotional

不被别人的进度搅动

看见别人更快升迁、成家、买房或建立成果时，心容易焦躁。创世记36章帮助我们把“更早”与“更蒙福”分开。信心是在自己的时钟里忠心，不让比较偷走顺服。

When others advance faster in career, family, finances, or public achievement, anxiety rises. Genesis 36 separates “earlier” from “more blessed.” Faithfulness means obeying within your own timeline without letting comparison steal obedience.

36:40-43

按着宗族、住处、名字，以扫所出的族长记在这里：亭纳、亚勒瓦、耶帖、阿何利巴玛、以拉、比嫩、基纳斯、提幔、米比萨、玛基叠、以兰；这是以东人在所得为业之地的族长。以扫就是以东人的始祖。

By their families, places, and names, the chiefs descended from Esau are listed: Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, Magdiel, and Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom in their inherited land; Esau is the father of the Edomites.

经文含义 Meaning

以“所得为业之地”收束

结尾以宗族、住处和名字总结，强调以东确实得着地业和社会身份。但创世记随即转回约瑟故事，显示以东虽有自己的地，盟约叙事仍沿雅各家展开。旁支的完整记录反而更清楚衬托主线。

The closing summary by families, places, and names stresses Edom’s real land and identity. Yet Genesis will turn next to Joseph, showing that the covenant narrative continues through Jacob’s household. The side branch is honored while the main line is clarified.

灵修应用 Devotional

接受神给自己的边界

以东有以东的地，雅各家有雅各家的呼召。我们也需要接受神给自己的位置，不羡慕别人的边界，也不轻看自己的托付。成熟的灵修不是幻想别人的人生，而是在被交付之地忠心。

Edom has Edom’s land, and Jacob’s house has Jacob’s calling. We also need to receive the place God gives us, neither envying another boundary nor despising our own trust. Mature devotion is faithfulness in the field assigned to us.

方法论 Methodology

如何读创世记36章的家谱

How to read the genealogy of Genesis 36

正统解经路径

The exegetical path

先看上下文：第35章重申雅各的名与应许，第37章转入约瑟，因此第36章是雅各线继续展开前对以扫线的完整交代。再看重复词：以扫、以东、西珥、族长、王、住处，显示本章关心身份、地土与社会结构。最后看神学分辨：神允许以东兴盛，却没有把盟约应许转移给以东。

Begin with context: Genesis 35 renews Jacob’s name and promise, while Genesis 37 turns to Joseph, so chapter 36 completes Esau’s line before the Jacob line continues. Repeated terms such as Esau, Edom, Seir, chiefs, kings, and dwellings highlight identity, land, and structure. The key distinction is that Edom prospers, yet the covenant promise is not transferred to Edom.

灵修应用路径

The devotional path

应用时不要把以东简单当成负面标签，也不要把外在强盛直接等同神的认可。本章帮助我们面对比较、边界、家族历史和身份焦虑：别人可能更早拥有可见成果，但神呼召人忠于自己被交付的位置。家谱也训练我们尊重名字背后的生命，不以是否处在中心来衡量人的价值。

Devotional reading should not reduce Edom to a flat negative label, nor equate visible strength with divine approval. The chapter addresses comparison, boundaries, family history, and identity anxiety. Others may gain visible outcomes earlier, yet God calls each person to faithfulness in an assigned place. Genealogy also trains us to honor lives behind names, not only those at the center.

落地操练 Practice

今天默想时，可以写下一个让你产生比较的人或群体，再写下神实际交给你的责任、边界和可顺服的一步。把眼光从“谁更快”转向“我是否忠心”。

For practice, name one person or group that triggers comparison, then write the responsibility, boundary, and next obedient step God has actually entrusted to you. Move the question from “Who is faster?” to “Am I faithful?”
