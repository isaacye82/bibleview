创世记 25章 Genesis Chapter 25

本章核心主题 · Chapter Theme

应许的转承与长子名分的轻看

The Promise Transferred and the Birthright Despised

创世记25章像一座桥：亚伯拉罕安然离世，以实玛利的谱系被交代，叙事焦点转向以撒与利百加的家。神听以撒祷告，使不孕的利百加怀孕，又在双子相争中宣告“大的要服事小的”。本章最后以扫为一碗红汤卖了长子名分，显出人可能因眼前饥饿轻看神所赐的长期祝福。

Genesis 25 functions as a bridge: Abraham dies in peace, Ishmael's line is acknowledged, and the narrative center moves to Isaac and Rebekah. God answers Isaac's prayer, gives children to Rebekah, and announces that “the elder shall serve the younger.” The chapter ends with Esau trading his birthright for red stew, exposing how immediate appetite can despise God's long-range gift.

列祖的交接

Patriarchal Transition

toledot / generations

亚伯拉罕的死亡不是应许的终点，而是叙事交给以撒的门槛。经文以家谱和安葬说明：神的计划穿过人的有限生命继续前行。 / Abraham's death is not the end of promise but the doorway into Isaac's generation. Genealogy and burial show that God's purpose moves forward through mortal lives.

祷告中的出生

Birth through Prayer

atar / plead, entreat

以撒为利百加祈求，神应允，使不孕成为生命的入口。应许不是机械传递，而是在等候、祷告和神主动赐下中显明。 / Isaac pleads for Rebekah, and God answers, turning barrenness into the doorway of life. Promise is not mechanical succession; it is received through waiting, prayer, and divine gift.

大的服事小的

The Elder Serves the Younger

rav yaavod tsair

神在双子出生前宣告颠倒常规的拣选。经文重点不是鼓励偏心或争夺，而是说明祝福根源在神自由而信实的旨意。 / Before the twins are born, God announces a reversal of custom. The point is not favoritism or rivalry, but that blessing rests in God's free and faithful purpose.

长子名分

The Birthright

bekorah / birthright

长子名分承载产业、家族责任和应许线索。以扫因一时饥饿轻看它，显明欲望能使人把未来的祝福换成眼前满足。 / The birthright carries inheritance, family responsibility, and the promise line. Esau despises it under hunger, showing how appetite can trade future blessing for immediate relief.

阅读导向：生命有限，应许延续，欲望受审

Reading lens: mortality, continuing promise, and tested desire.

本章不要只读成家族故事，也不要只读成雅各聪明、以扫粗心。经文把死亡、家谱、祷告、出生、神谕和一碗红汤放在一起，要求读者问：我怎样看待神所赐却暂时看不见的祝福？当饥饿、疲惫和冲动来临时，我是否仍按应许而活？

This chapter should not be reduced to family drama, nor to Jacob's cleverness and Esau's carelessness. Death, genealogy, prayer, birth, oracle, and a bowl of stew are placed together so readers must ask: How do I value God's unseen gift? When hunger, fatigue, and impulse arrive, will I still live by promise?

亚伯拉罕余年的家族安排 (25:1-6)

Abraham's Later Family Arrangements (25:1-6)

25:1-6

亚伯拉罕又娶一妻，名叫基土拉。她给他生了心兰、约珊、米但、米甸、伊施巴和书亚。亚伯拉罕将一切所有的都给了以撒；亚伯拉罕把财物分给他庶出的众子，趁着自己还在世的时候，打发他们离开以撒，往东方去。

Then again Abraham took a wife, and her name was Keturah. And she bare him Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. And Abraham gave all that he had unto Isaac. But unto the sons of the concubines Abraham gave gifts, and sent them away from Isaac his son, while he yet lived, eastward, unto the east country.

经文含义 Meaning

广泛后裔与应许主线 / Many Descendants and One Promise Line

基土拉所生的众子说明神确实使亚伯拉罕成为多族之父；但“将一切所有的都给了以撒”明确区分普遍繁衍与应许承继。送往东方不是轻视其他儿子，而是保护以撒承接神所立的约线。

Keturah's sons show that God truly made Abraham the father of many peoples. Yet “Abraham gave all that he had unto Isaac” distinguishes broad fruitfulness from covenant succession. Sending the other sons east protects Isaac's role in the promise line.

灵修应用 Devotional

祝福需要清晰交托 / Blessing Needs Clear Stewardship

人生进入交接季节时，爱心不等于边界模糊。桥梁原则是：神赐下的托付需要有秩序地安排，使下一代不在混乱中承受呼召。今天可以检视自己的时间、资源和责任，哪些需要清楚交托，哪些需要温柔但坚定地分界？

In seasons of transition, love is not the same as blurred boundaries. The bridge principle: God's entrusted gifts need ordered stewardship so the next generation can receive its calling without confusion. Review your time, resources, and responsibilities: what needs clear transfer, and what needs gracious boundaries?

亚伯拉罕安然归到列祖 (25:7-11)

Abraham Is Gathered to His People (25:7-11)

25:7-11

亚伯拉罕一生的年日是一百七十五岁。他寿高年迈，气绝而死，归到他列祖那里。他两个儿子以撒、以实玛利把他埋葬在麦比拉洞里。亚伯拉罕死了以后，神赐福给他的儿子以撒；以撒靠近庇耳拉海莱居住。

And these are the days of the years of Abraham's life which he lived, an hundred threescore and fifteen years. Then Abraham gave up the ghost, and died in a good old age, an old man, and full of years; and was gathered to his people. And his sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah. And it came to pass after the death of Abraham, that God blessed his son Isaac.

经文含义 Meaning

死亡没有截断神的祝福 / Death Does Not Cut Off God's Blessing

“寿高年迈、满了年日”强调亚伯拉罕在神信实中走完旅程。以撒和以实玛利同葬父亲，短暂呈现家族关系的修复；随后经文立刻说神赐福给以撒，说明应许从亚伯拉罕身上继续延伸。

The phrase “old man, and full of years” portrays Abraham's journey as completed under God's faithfulness. Isaac and Ishmael bury him together, briefly displaying family repair. Then the text immediately says God blessed Isaac, showing the promise continuing beyond Abraham's death.

灵修应用 Devotional

把有限生命交在信实神手中 / Entrust Mortal Life to the Faithful God

死亡提醒我们没有人能亲手完成神一切的计划。桥梁原则是：信心包括忠心走完自己的段落，也相信神会继续祂的工作。面对家庭传承、事奉交接或职业阶段变化时，可以少一点控制，多一点交托。

Death reminds us that no one personally completes all of God's purposes. The bridge principle: faith means finishing our portion faithfully and trusting God to continue his work. In family legacy, ministry handoff, or career transition, practice less control and deeper entrustment.

以实玛利的后裔与边界 (25:12-18)

Ishmael's Descendants and Borders (25:12-18)

25:12-18

撒拉的使女埃及人夏甲给亚伯拉罕所生的儿子以实玛利，他的后代记在下面。以实玛利的众子按着名字和村庄、营寨，作了十二族的族长。以实玛利享寿一百三十七岁，气绝而死，归到他列祖那里；他的子孙住在哈腓拉直到书珥，正在埃及前面，往亚述的路上。

Now these are the generations of Ishmael, Abraham's son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah's handmaid, bare unto Abraham. These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names, by their towns, and by their castles; twelve princes according to their nations. And these are the years of the life of Ishmael, an hundred and thirty and seven years: and he gave up the ghost and died; and they dwelt from Havilah unto Shur.

经文含义 Meaning

神也记念旁支的生命 / God Also Remembers the Side Line

以实玛利不是承受以撒之约的人，却不是被神遗忘的人。十二族长回应了神曾给夏甲和亚伯拉罕的应许。经文尊重他的后裔，同时把叙事边界整理清楚，好让焦点转入以撒家。

Ishmael does not carry Isaac's covenant line, but he is not forgotten by God. The twelve princes fulfill earlier promises given to Hagar and Abraham. The text honors Ishmael's descendants while setting narrative boundaries before turning to Isaac's household.

灵修应用 Devotional

不在中心，也在神眼中 / Not Central, Yet Seen by God

我们常把“不是主线”误解为“没有价值”。桥梁原则是：神的特殊呼召有清楚焦点，但祂的眷顾比我们的焦点更宽。今天在团队、家庭或教会中，不要用可见位置衡量人的尊严；也不要因自己不在中心就以为神不看见。

We often confuse “not central” with “not valuable.” The bridge principle: God's special calling has a clear focus, but his care is wider than our focus. In teams, families, or church, do not measure dignity by visible position; and do not assume God does not see you when you are not center stage.

以撒祈求与利百加怀孕 (25:19-21)

Isaac Pleads and Rebekah Conceives (25:19-21)

25:19-21

亚伯拉罕的儿子以撒的后代记在下面。亚伯拉罕生以撒；以撒娶利百加为妻的时候正四十岁。以撒因他妻子不生育，就为她祈求耶和华；耶和华应允他的祈求，他的妻子利百加就怀了孕。

And these are the generations of Isaac, Abraham's son: Abraham begat Isaac. And Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah to wife. And Isaac intreated the LORD for his wife, because she was barren: and the LORD was intreated of him, and Rebekah his wife conceived.

经文含义 Meaning

应许经过祷告与等候 / Promise Moves through Prayer and Waiting

利百加不孕让读者想起撒拉，说明应许后裔仍然不是靠天然能力自动出现。以撒祈求，神应允，叙事把生命的源头放在神面前，也显出夫妻共同承受应许的张力。

Rebekah's barrenness recalls Sarah's, showing again that the promised offspring does not arise automatically by natural ability. Isaac pleads, God answers, and the narrative locates life before God while revealing the couple's shared tension in receiving promise.

灵修应用 Devotional

把无能为力处带到神前 / Bring Helpless Places before God

等候最容易使人沉默、埋怨或自己硬撑。桥梁原则是：祷告不是控制结果，而是承认生命来自神，并在无能为力处与神保持关系。今天把一个你无法制造结果的处境具体说给神听，也学习为身边承压的人代求。

Waiting can make us silent, bitter, or self-reliant. The bridge principle: prayer does not control outcomes; it acknowledges that life comes from God and keeps us in relationship with him amid helplessness. Name one situation you cannot produce by force, and intercede for someone else under pressure.

腹中相争与神的宣告 (25:22-26)

Struggle in the Womb and God's Oracle (25:22-26)

25:22-26

孩子们在她腹中彼此相争，她就说：“若是这样，我为什么活着呢？”她就去求问耶和华。耶和华对她说：“两国在你腹内；两族要从你身上出来。这族必强于那族；将来大的要服事小的。”生产的时候，果然是双子；先产的身体发红，浑身有毛，如同皮衣，他们给他起名叫以扫。随后又生了以扫的兄弟，手抓住以扫的脚跟，因此给他起名叫雅各。

And the children struggled together within her; and she said, If it be so, why am I thus? And she went to enquire of the LORD. And the LORD said unto her, Two nations are in thy womb, and two manner of people shall be separated from thy bowels; and the one people shall be stronger than the other people; and the elder shall serve the younger. And the first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name Esau. And after that came his brother out, and his hand took hold on Esau's heel; and his name was called Jacob.

经文含义 Meaning

神解释隐藏的冲突 / God Interprets the Hidden Struggle

利百加没有把痛苦只当作身体问题，而是去求问耶和华。神的回答把腹中相争解释为两族未来的张力，并宣告“大的要服事小的”，预先表明应许的承继不按人间长幼秩序自动决定。

Rebekah does not treat her pain as merely physical; she seeks the LORD. God's answer interprets the womb struggle as the future tension of two peoples and announces that “the elder shall serve the younger,” showing that promise is not governed automatically by human birth order.

灵修应用 Devotional

困惑时先求问，不急着定论 / In Confusion, Ask before Concluding

当内在冲突、家庭张力或职场压力强烈到让人问“为什么这样”时，我们容易急着贴标签。桥梁原则是：信心把困惑带到神面前，等候祂给出更大的解释。今天在反应之前，先用祷告把自己的混乱命名出来，再按神的话校准下一步。

When inner conflict, family tension, or workplace pressure makes us ask, “Why is this happening?” we rush to label things. The bridge principle: faith brings confusion before God and waits for a larger interpretation. Before reacting today, name your turmoil in prayer and let God's word calibrate your next step.

兄弟性情与父母偏爱 (25:27-28)

The Brothers' Temperaments and Parental Preference (25:27-28)

25:27-28

两个孩子渐渐长大，以扫善于打猎，常在田野；雅各为人安静，常住在帐棚里。以撒爱以扫，因为常吃他的野味；利百加却爱雅各。

And the boys grew: and Esau was a cunning hunter, a man of the field; and Jacob was a plain man, dwelling in tents. And Isaac loved Esau, because he did eat of his venison: but Rebekah loved Jacob.

经文含义 Meaning

家庭偏爱预备后续冲突 / Preference Sets Up Later Conflict

经文简洁描写两兄弟不同性情，也直接指出父母各有所爱。以撒的偏爱与口腹有关，利百加的偏爱与雅各相连；这些家庭倾向将成为后面欺骗与祝福争夺的土壤。

The text briefly sketches the brothers' different temperaments and plainly notes divided parental affection. Isaac's preference is linked to appetite, while Rebekah's is attached to Jacob. These family inclinations will become the soil for later deception and struggle over blessing.

灵修应用 Devotional

偏爱会塑造关系气候 / Preference Shapes the Relational Climate

关系里的偏爱常以“我只是更欣赏他”出现，却可能让家庭、团队或教会形成竞争。桥梁原则是：真实的爱要承认差异，却不把人变成满足自己口味的工具。今天检视自己是否因能力、性格或利益而偏待某人，并主动补上一份公义的关注。

Preference often sounds harmless: “I simply appreciate this person more.” Yet it can create competition in families, teams, or churches. The bridge principle: real love recognizes difference without turning people into tools for our tastes. Examine whether ability, personality, or benefit has led you to partiality, and offer just attention where needed.

以扫卖了长子名分 (25:29-34)

Esau Sells His Birthright (25:29-34)

25:29-34

有一天，雅各熬汤，以扫从田野回来累昏了。以扫对雅各说：“我累昏了，求你把这红汤给我喝。”雅各说：“你今日把长子的名分卖给我吧。”以扫说：“我将要死，这长子的名分于我有什么益处呢？”雅各叫他起誓，以扫就起了誓，把长子的名分卖给雅各。于是雅各将饼和红豆汤给了以扫；以扫吃了喝了，便起来走了。这就是以扫轻看了他长子的名分。

And Jacob sod pottage: and Esau came from the field, and he was faint. And Esau said to Jacob, Feed me, I pray thee, with that same red pottage; for I am faint. And Jacob said, Sell me this day thy birthright. And Esau said, Behold, I am at the point to die: and what profit shall this birthright do to me? And he sware unto him: and he sold his birthright unto Jacob. Then Jacob gave Esau bread and pottage of lentiles; and he did eat and drink, and rose up, and went his way: thus Esau despised his birthright.

经文含义 Meaning

眼前饥饿暴露价值秩序 / Present Hunger Reveals the Order of Values

雅各的交易显出精明甚至利用机会；以扫的回应则显出更深问题：他把长子名分看作无益，宁愿用它换取一餐。结尾“轻看”给出道德评价，说明真正危险不是饥饿，而是把神赐的产业视为可随手交换的东西。

Jacob's bargain shows shrewdness and opportunism, but Esau's response reveals the deeper issue: he treats the birthright as useless and trades it for one meal. The closing word “despised” gives the moral verdict. The danger is not hunger itself, but treating God's inheritance as something easily exchanged.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要用一碗红汤换掉呼召 / Do Not Trade Calling for a Bowl of Stew

疲惫、孤独和压力会放大眼前需要，让人以为“现在舒服”比“长期忠心”更真实。桥梁原则是：属灵成熟包括在身体和情绪饥饿时仍保护神所托付的价值。今天在购物、情欲、怒气、职业捷径或关系妥协前，先问：这碗汤要我付出什么未来代价？

Fatigue, loneliness, and pressure magnify immediate need until comfort feels more real than long-term faithfulness. The bridge principle: spiritual maturity protects God's entrusted values even when body and emotions are hungry. Before spending, lust, anger, career shortcuts, or relational compromise, ask: What future cost is this bowl of stew demanding?

方法论 · Methodology

从正统解经到日常灵修

From sound exegesis to daily devotion.

正统解经原则 · Sound Exegesis

先按创世记叙事脉络观察：第24章为以撒预备妻子，第25章完成亚伯拉罕时代的收束，并开启以撒、雅各线索。解释时要尊重“家谱过渡”“不孕与祷告”“神谕反转”“长子名分”这些叙事标记，避免把本章简化成家庭伦理故事或个人成功策略。

Begin with the narrative flow of Genesis: chapter 24 prepares Isaac's wife, while chapter 25 closes Abraham's era and opens the Isaac-Jacob line. Interpretation should respect the narrative markers of genealogy, barrenness and prayer, divine reversal, and birthright, without reducing the chapter to family ethics or personal success tactics.

灵修应用路径 · Devotional Application

应用不是随意把自己代入雅各或以扫，而是先抽出桥梁原则：神的应许越过人的死亡继续；祷告把无能为力带到神前；偏爱会伤害关系；欲望会检验我们是否轻看属灵产业。

Devotional application does not casually identify oneself with Jacob or Esau. It first draws bridge principles: God's promise continues beyond human death; prayer brings helplessness before God; partiality damages relationships; and desire tests whether we despise spiritual inheritance.
