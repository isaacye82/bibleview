创世记 29章 Genesis Chapter 29

Genesis 29 Overview · 创世记二十九章全景

井旁相遇：爱、服事与被看见的人

At the Well: Love, Service, and the One Who Is Seen

创世记29章把雅各带到东方人的地。他在井旁遇见拉结，亲手移开石头，进入拉班家中；然而曾用计得福的雅各，如今在婚约中被拉班欺骗。雅各为拉结服事十四年，利亚在不被爱的处境中生下儿子，并用孩子的名字表达她渴望被丈夫接纳，也逐渐转向称颂耶和华。经文让人看见，人间的爱常夹杂偏爱、交易与伤害，但神仍在复杂家庭中顾念软弱者，推进祂对亚伯拉罕家的应许。

Genesis 29 brings Jacob to the land of the eastern peoples. At the well he meets Rachel, rolls away the stone, and enters Laban’s household; yet the man who once used deception to gain blessing is now deceived in marriage. Jacob serves fourteen years for Rachel, while Leah, unloved and overlooked, bears sons whose names voice her longing for acceptance and her gradual turn toward praise. The chapter shows that human love is often mixed with favoritism, bargaining, and pain, while God still sees the vulnerable and carries forward His promise to Abraham’s family.

动态意象：井水微光、石头移开、羊群缓慢移动，一条玫瑰色线从相遇延伸到赞美，表达本章从爱情追寻到神看见被冷落者的转向。 The glowing well, rolling stone, moving flocks, and rose thread trace the chapter’s movement from romantic pursuit to God seeing the overlooked.

本章核心主题

Core themes in Genesis 29

井旁的引导

Guidance at the Well

be’er / well

井是旅人得水、牧人聚集、婚姻故事展开之处。雅各看似偶然遇见拉结，叙事却延续神在旅途保守他的应许。EN: The well gathers travelers, shepherds, and marriage negotiations; Jacob’s meeting with Rachel appears ordinary, yet it continues God’s promised guidance on the road.

爱的服事

Service of Love

avad / to serve

雅各因爱拉结而服事七年，并觉得日子短少。经文承认爱情的力量，也让读者看见爱若被家庭权力利用，会变成漫长代价。EN: Jacob serves seven years for Rachel, and love makes them seem few; the text honors love’s strength while exposing how family power can exploit it.

欺骗的回声

Echoes of Deception

mirmah / deceit

拉班用利亚替代拉结，使雅各经历自己曾加给父亲和哥哥的欺骗回声。经文不是简单报复论，而是显明罪会在关系中留下真实后果。EN: Laban substitutes Leah for Rachel, and Jacob tastes the echo of his own deception; this is not a crude revenge tale but a sober picture of sin’s relational consequences.

被冷落者蒙看见

The Unloved Is Seen

ra’ah / to see

耶和华看见利亚失宠，就使她生育。神的看见临到家庭系统中最容易被忽略的人，并在她的痛苦中开启赞美之路。EN: The Lord sees Leah’s unloved condition and gives her children; divine sight reaches the overlooked and opens a path from pain toward praise.

创世记29章逐段双轨研读

Bilingual study path through Genesis Chapter 29

以下按叙事推进拆分本章：井旁相遇、为爱服事、婚约受骗、偏爱造成家庭张力，以及神看见利亚的苦情。 The study follows the narrative through the well encounter, loving service, marital deception, family favoritism, and the Lord’s seeing of Leah’s affliction.

雅各来到井旁遇见拉结 (29:1-14)

Jacob Meets Rachel at the Well

29:1-8

雅各起行，到了东方人之地，看见田间有一口井，旁边卧着三群羊；因为人饮羊群都是用那井里的水。井口上的石头是大的。牧人说：“我们认识拉班；他的女儿拉结领着羊来了。”雅各劝他们饮羊，但他们说必须等众群聚齐，才可把石头转离井口。

Then Jacob went on his journey and came into the land of the people of the east. He looked and saw a well in the field, with three flocks of sheep lying beside it, for out of that well the flocks were watered. The stone on the well’s mouth was large. The shepherds said they knew Laban, and that Rachel his daughter was coming with the sheep. Jacob urged them to water the sheep, but they answered that all the flocks must first be gathered before the stone could be rolled away.

经文含义 Meaning

从伯特利应许到普通田野

经文没有用奇异景象开始，而是用旅程、井、羊群、牧人的问答展开。伯特利的应许进入日常场景，显明神的引导常隐藏在普通道路与普通人际接触中。井口的大石头也预备后文雅各主动行动，表达他从孤单逃亡者进入家族网络的第一步。

The chapter opens not with a vision but with travel, a well, flocks, and ordinary conversation. Bethel’s promise now moves into daily circumstances, showing that God’s guidance often works through normal roads and relationships. The large stone prepares for Jacob’s decisive action and his entrance into a new family network.

灵修应用 Devotional

在平常场景中辨认引导

我们常期待神用显眼方式指路，却忽略日常中的问题、对话和机会。职场里一次普通沟通，家庭中一次耐心倾听，可能就是下一步顺服的入口。灵修不是逃离平凡，而是在平凡中学习警醒、谦卑和主动。

We often expect guidance to arrive dramatically and miss the questions, conversations, and opportunities of ordinary life. A routine workplace exchange or a patient family conversation may become the doorway to obedience. Devotion does not escape the ordinary; it learns attentiveness, humility, and faithful initiative within it.

29:9-14

雅各正说话的时候，拉结领着她父亲的羊来了。雅各看见拉结，就上前把石头转离井口，饮他舅舅拉班的羊群，并与拉结亲嘴，放声而哭。拉结跑去告诉父亲；拉班迎接雅各，抱着他，与他亲嘴，领他到家。雅各将一切的情由告诉拉班，拉班说：“你实在是我的骨肉。”

While Jacob was still speaking, Rachel came with her father’s sheep. When Jacob saw Rachel, he rolled the stone from the well’s mouth and watered Laban’s flock. Jacob kissed Rachel and lifted up his voice and wept. Rachel ran and told her father, and Laban came to meet Jacob, embraced him, kissed him, and brought him to his house. Jacob told Laban all these things, and Laban said, “Surely you are my bone and my flesh.”

经文含义 Meaning

热情、眼泪与亲族接纳

雅各移石饮羊，显出力量与急切；他放声而哭，说明旅途压力、亲族相认和神应许的延续同时涌上心头。拉班的“骨肉”之言有亲情意味，也为后文的利益计算埋下伏笔，因为这家并非只有温暖，也有操控。

Jacob’s rolling of the stone shows strength and urgency; his loud weeping reveals the emotional weight of travel, kinship, and promise converging. Laban’s “bone and flesh” language sounds affectionate, yet it also prepares the reader for a household where welcome and manipulation will stand close together.

灵修应用 Devotional

欢迎也需要分辨

人际关系中的热情值得感恩，但成熟的信心不会把所有亲近都等同于安全。进入新团队、新家庭或新合作时，我们既可真诚投入，也要保守诚实边界。神带领我们进入关系，也教我们在关系中学习智慧。

Warm welcome is a gift, but mature faith does not confuse every form of closeness with safety. In a new team, family setting, or partnership, we can engage sincerely while keeping honest boundaries. God leads us into relationships and teaches wisdom within them.

雅各为拉结服事，拉班以利亚欺骗 (29:15-30)

Jacob Serves for Rachel and Is Deceived with Leah

29:15-20

拉班对雅各说：“你虽是我的骨肉，岂可白白地服事我？请告诉我，你要什么为工价？”拉班有两个女儿，大的名叫利亚，小的名叫拉结。利亚的眼睛没有神气，拉结却生得美貌俊秀。雅各爱拉结，就说：“我愿为你小女儿拉结服事你七年。”雅各就为拉结服事了七年；他因为深爱拉结，就看这七年如同几天。

Laban said to Jacob, “Because you are my relative, should you therefore serve me for nothing? Tell me, what shall your wages be?” Laban had two daughters: the older was Leah, and the younger was Rachel. Leah’s eyes were weak, but Rachel was beautiful in form and appearance. Jacob loved Rachel and said, “I will serve you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.” So Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they seemed to him but a few days because of the love he had for her.

经文含义 Meaning

爱进入工价语言

“服事”和“工价”把亲族关系转入劳动契约。雅各的爱真实而深，却必须通过拉班设定的交易结构表达。经文对拉结与利亚的描写也揭示家庭中的比较眼光：一个被称为美，一个被放在不足的叙述里，后续痛苦由此展开。

The words “serve” and “wages” move kinship into the language of labor contract. Jacob’s love is real, yet it must be expressed within Laban’s transactional structure. The comparison between Rachel and Leah also exposes a family culture of evaluation, where one daughter is celebrated and the other framed by lack.

灵修应用 Devotional

爱不可被交易逻辑吞没

爱会付代价，但爱不该被操控者当作筹码。今天在婚恋、职场或家庭中，我们需要分辨：我的服事是出于自由的爱，还是被别人的期待和不透明规则挟制？健康的爱愿意给予，也愿意说清楚界限。

Love is willing to pay a cost, but it should not become a lever for manipulation. In dating, work, or family, we need to ask whether our service flows from free love or from hidden rules and pressured expectations. Healthy love gives generously and speaks boundaries clearly.

29:21-27

七年满了，雅各对拉班说：“日期已经满了，求你把我的妻子给我，我好与她同房。”拉班摆设筵席，到了晚上却将女儿利亚送来给雅各，雅各就与她同房。早晨雅各发现是利亚，便对拉班说：“你为什么欺哄我？”拉班回答说：“大女儿还没有给人，先把小女儿给人，在我们这地方没有这规矩。你为这个满了七日，我就把那个也给你，你再为她服事我七年。”

When the seven years were completed, Jacob said to Laban, “Give me my wife, that I may go in to her, for my time is completed.” Laban gathered the people and made a feast, but in the evening he took Leah his daughter and brought her to Jacob. In the morning, behold, it was Leah. Jacob said to Laban, “What is this you have done to me? Why then have you deceived me?” Laban replied that it was not done in that place to give the younger before the firstborn, and told Jacob to complete the week, then Rachel would also be given for another seven years of service.

经文含义 Meaning

欺骗者被欺骗

“早晨，看哪，是利亚”形成强烈揭露。雅各曾利用父亲眼目昏花与长幼次序，如今拉班用夜晚和地方规矩反制他。经文显明欺骗会制造循环，也让读者看见神的应许并不取消人行为的后果。

“In the morning, behold, it was Leah” lands as a sharp unveiling. Jacob once exploited blindness and birth order; now Laban uses darkness and local custom against him. The text shows deception creating cycles, while God’s promise does not erase the consequences of human actions.

灵修应用 Devotional

面对后果时仍可回转

当过去的模式在今天关系里回头伤害我们，最重要的不是只控诉别人，而是让神照亮自己的生命轨迹。诚实承认、停止复制操控、学习透明沟通，是从旧循环出来的开始。神能在后果中管教，也能在管教中带来更新。

When old patterns return to wound our present relationships, the needed response is not only to accuse others but to let God illuminate our own history. Honest confession, refusing manipulation, and practicing transparent speech begin the way out of old cycles. God can discipline through consequences and renew us within that discipline.

29:28-30

雅各就如此行。满了利亚的七日，拉班便将女儿拉结给雅各为妻，又将婢女辟拉给拉结作使女。雅各也与拉结同房，并且爱拉结胜似爱利亚，于是又服事了拉班七年。

Jacob did so, and completed Leah’s week. Then Laban gave him his daughter Rachel to be his wife, and gave Bilhah his female servant to Rachel. Jacob went in to Rachel also, and he loved Rachel more than Leah. So he served Laban for another seven years.

经文含义 Meaning

偏爱的家庭结构成形

雅各得到拉结，却不是以简单圆满结束；经文立刻说明他爱拉结胜过利亚。两个妻子、两个使女和不均衡的爱，成为后续十二支派故事的家庭背景。神会使用这个家庭，却不粉饰其中的偏爱与伤害。

Jacob receives Rachel, but the story does not end in simple fulfillment. The text immediately says he loved Rachel more than Leah. Two wives, two servants, and unequal affection form the family setting for the later tribes of Israel. God will work through this household without sanitizing its favoritism and pain.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要把得着当作医治

雅各得到所爱的人，却没有因此解决家庭破裂。今天我们也可能以为升职、结婚、买房或达成目标就会自动医治内心；经文提醒我们，外在得着若没有被神更新的爱和公义，仍会制造新的伤口。

Jacob receives the woman he loves, yet the household is not healed. We may also assume that promotion, marriage, a home, or a milestone will automatically mend the heart. The text warns that outward gain without renewed love and righteousness can still create new wounds.

神看见利亚，使她生育 (29:31-35)

The Lord Sees Leah and Gives Children

29:31-32

耶和华见利亚失宠，就使她生育，拉结却不生育。利亚怀孕生子，给他起名叫流便，因为她说：“耶和华看见我的苦情，如今我的丈夫必爱我。”

When the LORD saw that Leah was unloved, he opened her womb, but Rachel was barren. Leah conceived and bore a son, and she called his name Reuben, for she said, “Because the LORD has looked upon my affliction; for now my husband will love me.”

经文含义 Meaning

神看见失宠者的苦情

叙事第一次明确说“耶和华见”。在人只按美貌和偏爱排序的家中，神的目光转向被冷落的利亚。流便之名与“看见”相关，显示利亚把生产理解为神对她苦情的回应，但她仍盼望丈夫的爱来确认自己。

The narrative now explicitly says that the LORD saw. In a household ordered by beauty and preference, God’s gaze turns toward overlooked Leah. Reuben’s name is connected to seeing, showing that Leah understands the birth as God’s response to her affliction, even while she still longs for her husband’s love to confirm her worth.

灵修应用 Devotional

被神看见先于被人接纳

当我们在家庭、团队或朋友圈中长期觉得自己排在后面，心会渴望某个人终于证明“我值得”。利亚的故事先把我们带到神的目光下：人的接纳宝贵，却不能成为身份根基。被神看见，是重新站稳的开始。

When we feel pushed to the margins in family, work, or friendships, the heart longs for someone to prove, “I matter.” Leah’s story first brings us under God’s gaze. Human acceptance is precious, but it cannot become the foundation of identity. Being seen by God is where steadiness begins.

29:33-34

利亚又怀孕生子，就说：“耶和华因为听见我失宠，所以又赐给我这个儿子。”于是给他起名叫西缅。她又怀孕生子，说：“我给丈夫生了三个儿子，他必与我联合。”因此起名叫利未。

She conceived again and bore a son, and said, “Because the LORD has heard that I am unloved, he has given me this son also.” So she called his name Simeon. Again she conceived and bore a son, and said, “Now this time my husband will be attached to me, because I have borne him three sons.” Therefore his name was called Levi.

经文含义 Meaning

从被听见到仍求联合

西缅之名与“听见”相连，利亚承认神听见她失宠的处境；利未之名与“联合”相连，显出她仍把盼望投向丈夫的依附。经文温柔而真实地记录信心成长中的混杂：她认识神的怜悯，却仍在人的爱里寻找稳定。

Simeon’s name is linked to hearing, and Leah confesses that God has heard her unloved condition. Levi’s name is linked to attachment, revealing her continuing desire for her husband’s bond. The text records the mixed process of growth: Leah recognizes God’s mercy while still seeking stability in human affection.

灵修应用 Devotional

让渴望被神整理

我们对亲密、认可和归属的渴望并不羞耻，但若不交给神，就容易把别人压成满足自己的工具。祷告可以很诚实：主啊，我渴望被爱，也求你整理我的渴望，使我不再用成就、付出或控制来换取安全感。

The desire for intimacy, recognition, and belonging is not shameful, but without surrender it can turn others into tools for our security. Prayer can be honest: Lord, I long to be loved; order my desires so I no longer trade achievement, sacrifice, or control for safety.

29:35

利亚又怀孕生子，说：“这回我要赞美耶和华。”因此给他起名叫犹大。这才停了生育。

And she conceived again and bore a son, and said, “This time I will praise the LORD.” Therefore she called his name Judah. Then she ceased bearing.

经文含义 Meaning

赞美成为新的转向

犹大之名与“赞美”有关。利亚的语言从“丈夫必爱我”“必与我联合”，转到“我要赞美耶和华”。这不是说痛苦立刻消失，而是焦点发生转向。更深地看，君王谱系后来出自犹大，神把未来盼望放在被冷落者的赞美里。

Judah’s name is associated with praise. Leah’s speech moves from “my husband will love me” and “he will be attached to me” to “I will praise the LORD.” This does not mean her pain vanishes, but her focus turns. In the larger story, the royal line will come through Judah, placing future hope within the praise of the overlooked woman.

灵修应用 Devotional

赞美不是否认伤口

真正的赞美不是假装不痛，而是在伤口还未完全改变时，把最终重量交还给神。今天你也许仍未得到某人的肯定，但可以先在神面前说：这回我要赞美耶和华。这样的赞美会慢慢把人从索取认可的焦虑中释放出来。

True praise does not pretend the wound is gone; it gives final weight back to God while the situation is still unfinished. You may still lack someone’s affirmation, yet you can say before God, “This time I will praise the LORD.” Such praise slowly frees the heart from anxious dependence on approval.

Methodology · 方法论

两条路径：先理解经文，再进入生活

Two paths: understand the text first, then carry it into life.

正统解经原则

Sound exegesis

本章先按叙事脉络阅读：雅各从伯特利应许进入哈兰现实；井旁相遇连接神的引导；拉班欺骗呼应雅各过去的欺骗；利亚生产则显示神看见被冷落者。观察关键词如“服事”“欺哄”“看见”“听见”“赞美”，能帮助我们避免只把本章读成浪漫故事。

Read the chapter in narrative sequence: Jacob moves from Bethel’s promise into Haran’s reality; the well encounter shows guidance; Laban’s deceit echoes Jacob’s past; Leah’s children reveal God seeing the unloved. Key words such as serve, deceive, see, hear, and praise keep the chapter from becoming merely a romance story.

灵修应用路径

Devotional application

应用时不把自己简单代入“雅各成功追爱”或“利亚终于翻身”，而是问：我是否在关系中复制操控？我是否把爱变成交易？我是否把人的认可当作身份根基？桥梁原则是：神在复杂关系中仍看见人、管教人、更新人，并呼召我们用诚实、界限和赞美回应。

Application should not flatten the story into “Jacob wins love” or “Leah finally triumphs.” Instead ask: Am I repeating manipulation? Have I turned love into transaction? Have I made human approval the basis of identity? The bridge principle is that God sees, disciplines, and renews people within complex relationships, calling us to respond with honesty, boundaries, and praise.

实际落地指南

Practical guide

读创世记29章时，可以先标出每个角色的行动和动机，再写下神主动“看见”的地方。随后把焦点带到今天：在家庭、婚恋、团队和职场里，哪些关系需要停止欺骗或含糊？哪些渴望需要交给神整理？哪些被忽略的人需要被公义地看见和尊重？

When reading Genesis 29, first mark each character’s actions and motives, then note where God actively “sees.” Bring the focus into today: Which relationships in family, romance, teams, or workplaces need an end to deceit or vagueness? Which desires need to be ordered before God? Which overlooked people need to be seen and honored with justice?
