创世记 43章 Genesis Chapter 43

本章核心主题 Core Theme

惧怕走向筵席，担保预备复和

Fear Moves Toward a Feast; Pledge Prepares Reconciliation

创世记43章承接饥荒压力，把雅各一家带到必须面对便雅悯、银子和西缅的时刻。犹大成为转折人物，他愿为便雅悯担保，显示过去破碎的兄弟关系开始出现新的责任。兄弟们进入约瑟家时充满恐惧，却先听见“平安，不要害怕”；约瑟看见便雅悯而哭，又用一场有秩序、有恩待的筵席预备下一步试验。本章的中心不是悬疑本身，而是神怎样在惧怕、责任、恩典和桌席中推进复和。

Genesis 43 continues the pressure of famine and brings Jacob’s family to the unavoidable issues of Benjamin, the silver, and Simeon. Judah becomes the turning figure: he offers himself as surety for Benjamin, showing that broken brotherhood is beginning to bear new responsibility. The brothers enter Joseph’s house in fear, yet first hear, “Peace to you; do not be afraid.” Joseph weeps when he sees Benjamin and then prepares an ordered, gracious meal that sets up the next test. The chapter is not merely suspense; it shows God advancing reconciliation through fear, responsibility, grace, and the table.

创世记43章逐段双轨研读

A bilingual two-track study through Genesis Chapter 43

本章围绕“担保、交托、平安、眼泪、筵席”展开。研读轨道观察家庭责任如何开始转向，约瑟怎样以隐藏身份继续试验；灵修轨道把这些主题连接到现代关系修复、压力决策、诚实沟通与为他人的恩典欢喜。 This chapter unfolds around pledge, surrender, peace, tears, and the meal. The study track observes how family responsibility begins to turn and how Joseph continues testing from hiddenness; the devotional track connects these themes to modern relational repair, pressured decisions, honest communication, and rejoicing in grace given to others.

雅各同意便雅悯同行 (43:1-14)

Jacob Allows Benjamin to Go

43:1-10

迦南地的饥荒甚大，粮吃尽后，雅各要儿子们再去埃及买粮。犹大提醒父亲，埃及那人严严告诫他们，若不带便雅悯就不得见他的面。雅各埋怨他们为何透露还有兄弟，众子说明只是照实回答。犹大请求父亲把童子交给他，并愿亲自担保，若不带回便雅悯，就永远担罪。

The famine remains severe, and when the grain is gone Jacob tells his sons to return to Egypt. Judah reminds him that the Egyptian ruler solemnly warned them not to appear without Benjamin. Jacob laments that they revealed another brother, but they explain that they only answered the man’s direct questions. Judah asks Jacob to entrust the boy to him and pledges to bear the blame forever if Benjamin is not brought back.

经文含义 Meaning

危机中的新责任

经文把家庭张力推进到关键处：雅各仍被失去约瑟的痛苦捆住，儿子们却不能再用沉默逃避。犹大的话与过去形成对比，他不再把弟弟当作可牺牲的人，而是愿意承担后果。经文含义显示，真实悔改常先表现为新的责任感，而不是漂亮解释。

The narrative presses the family tension to a turning point: Jacob is still bound by the pain of losing Joseph, while the sons can no longer hide behind silence. Judah’s words contrast with his earlier failure; he now treats a brother not as expendable but as one for whom he will bear consequence. Genuine repentance often appears first as new responsibility, not polished explanation.

灵修应用 Devotional

用承担代替逃避

现代生活中，我们也可能像雅各一样被旧创伤支配，或像儿子们一样习惯拖延困难对话。桥梁原则是：神的更新会把人从自保带向承担。今天可以在家庭、团队或教会关系中问：我需要为谁站出来？哪一项责任不能再推给环境或别人？

In modern life we may be ruled by old wounds like Jacob or delay hard conversations like his sons. The bridge principle is that God’s renewing work moves people from self-protection toward responsibility. We can ask: for whom must I stand up, and what responsibility can no longer be blamed on circumstances or others?

43:11-14

雅各终于吩咐他们带上本地最好的出产作礼物，又带双倍银子，把口袋里归还的银子一并送回，或许那是错放的。他也同意带便雅悯去，并祈求全能的神使他们在那人面前蒙怜悯，释放西缅和便雅悯；若他必须丧子，就丧吧。

Jacob finally tells them to take some of the best products of the land as a gift, along with double money and the returned silver, perhaps placed in their sacks by mistake. He allows Benjamin to go and prays that God Almighty may grant them mercy before the man, so that Simeon and Benjamin may be released; if he is bereaved, he is bereaved.

经文含义 Meaning

尽责与交托并行

雅各不是消极放手，他准备礼物、归还银子、承认可能的错误，同时向“全能的神”求怜悯。这里的信心不是否认风险，而是在风险中做当做的事，并把结果交给神。

Jacob does not surrender passively. He prepares a gift, returns the silver, acknowledges a possible mistake, and asks God Almighty for mercy. Faith here does not deny risk; it does what is right within risk and entrusts the outcome to God.

灵修应用 Devotional

把可控的做好，把不可控的交托

在职场决策、医疗等待、关系修复中，信靠神并不等于什么都不做。桥梁原则是：敬虔的交托包含诚实预备。我们可以整理该归还的、说明该说明的、道歉该道歉的，然后承认结果不在自己手里。

In workplace decisions, medical waiting, or relational repair, trusting God does not mean doing nothing. The bridge principle is that faithful surrender includes honest preparation. We return what should be returned, explain what should be explained, apologize where needed, and then admit that the outcome is not in our hands.

兄弟们入约瑟家中 (43:15-25)

The Brothers Enter Joseph's House

43:15-18

众人带着礼物、双倍银子和便雅悯下到埃及，站在约瑟面前。约瑟看见便雅悯，就吩咐家宰把这些人带到屋里，预备筵席，因为中午要同他们吃饭。兄弟们被带进约瑟家，心里惧怕，以为这是因先前口袋里的银子，要找机会攻击他们，夺取他们和牲畜作奴仆。

The men take the gift, double money, and Benjamin, go down to Egypt, and stand before Joseph. When Joseph sees Benjamin, he orders his steward to bring them into the house and prepare a meal, for they will dine with him at noon. The brothers are afraid when brought into Joseph’s house, assuming it is because of the silver in their sacks and fearing an accusation that will enslave them and seize their animals.

经文含义 Meaning

恩待被误读为威胁

约瑟安排筵席，本意是接待；兄弟们因未解决的罪疚，把好事解读成陷阱。叙事让读者看见，良心不安会扭曲判断，使人把恩典看成审判的前奏。

Joseph arranges a meal as hospitality, but the brothers’ unresolved guilt interprets kindness as a trap. The narrative shows how an unsettled conscience distorts perception, making grace look like the beginning of judgment.

灵修应用 Devotional

罪疚会改变我们听见的话

当人长期逃避责任时，别人的普通询问也可能听起来像指控，新的机会也可能被看成危险。桥梁原则是，神常先处理我们的心，再处理外面的局面。我们需要把隐藏的惧怕带到神面前，也向可信任的人说出真相。

When we have long avoided responsibility, ordinary questions can sound like accusations and new opportunities can feel dangerous. The bridge principle is that God often addresses the heart before the outer situation changes. We need to bring hidden fear before God and speak truthfully with trustworthy people.

43:19-25

兄弟们在门口向家宰说明先前买粮回家时发现银子仍在口袋里，如今带回来，也另带银子买粮。家宰回答：“你们平安，不要害怕，是你们的神和你们父亲的神赐给你们财宝；你们的银子我早已收了。”随后他把西缅带出来，给他们水洗脚，也喂养驴。他们便预备礼物，等候约瑟中午来到。

At the doorway the brothers explain to the steward that they found the silver in their sacks after the first journey and have brought it back, along with more money to buy grain. The steward replies, “Peace to you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has given you treasure; I received your money.” He brings Simeon out, gives them water to wash their feet, feeds their donkeys, and they prepare the gift for Joseph’s arrival at noon.

经文含义 Meaning

平安先临到惧怕的人

家宰的话把焦点从自我辩护转向神的供应：“你们平安，不要害怕。”西缅被释放、水和饲料被供应，显示他们所怕的审判并未发生。经文含义不是鼓励逃避责任，因为他们确实带回银子；而是显明神的恩典超过他们的恐惧。

The steward’s words move the focus from self-defense to God’s provision: “Peace to you; do not be afraid.” Simeon is released, water is provided, and the animals are fed, showing that the judgment they feared does not occur. The text does not excuse irresponsibility, since they did bring the silver back; it reveals grace larger than their fear.

灵修应用 Devotional

听见“平安”的操练

许多人在认错前最怕的是失控的后果。桥梁原则是，神的平安不是让我们否认错误，而是给我们勇气诚实面对。今天若要修复一段关系，可以先停止编造最坏剧本，按真相开口，并相信神能在对话中预备出路。

Many people fear uncontrolled consequences before admitting wrong. The bridge principle is that God’s peace does not make us deny fault; it gives courage to face it honestly. When repairing a relationship, we can stop rehearsing the worst-case script, speak truthfully, and trust that God can prepare a way through the conversation.

约瑟见便雅悯并设席 (43:26-34)

Joseph Sees Benjamin and Hosts the Meal

43:26-30

约瑟回家时，兄弟们把礼物拿进屋里给他，又俯伏在地。约瑟问他们安，并问他们年老的父亲是否还在。他们回答父亲仍在，并低头下拜。约瑟举目看见同母弟便雅悯，就问这是否是他们所说的小兄弟，并说：“小儿啊，愿神赐恩给你。”约瑟因爱弟之情发动，急忙寻找地方哭泣，就进内室哭了一场。

When Joseph comes home, the brothers bring the gift into the house and bow to the ground. Joseph asks about their welfare and about their aged father, and they answer that he is alive, bowing again. Joseph looks up and sees Benjamin, his mother’s son, asks whether this is the younger brother they mentioned, and says, “God be gracious to you, my son.” Deeply moved, Joseph hurries out to find a place to weep and enters a private room to cry.

经文含义 Meaning

梦应验中有眼泪

兄弟们再次下拜，使约瑟的梦更清楚地应验；但经文没有把约瑟写成冷酷胜利者。他的祝福和眼泪说明，神的计划成就时，并不抹去人的深情和伤痛。真正的复和往往需要真相、时间、试验，也需要怜悯。

The brothers bow again, clarifying the fulfillment of Joseph’s dreams, yet the text does not portray Joseph as a cold victor. His blessing and tears show that the fulfillment of God’s plan does not erase human affection and pain. Real reconciliation often requires truth, time, testing, and mercy.

灵修应用 Devotional

在成就中仍可哭泣

有时我们以为“神掌权”就意味着自己不该难过。桥梁原则是，信心不是麻木；神允许人在顺服中流泪。面对家庭创伤或漫长等待时，可以承认情绪，同时拒绝让情绪掌权。

Sometimes we assume that if God is sovereign, we should not grieve. The bridge principle is that faith is not numbness; God permits tears within obedience. When facing family wounds or long waiting, we may acknowledge emotion while refusing to let emotion rule us.

43:31-34

约瑟洗脸出来，勉强控制自己，吩咐摆饭。众人分别摆席：约瑟一席，弟兄们一席，埃及人另席，因为埃及人不与希伯来人同吃。约瑟按长幼次序安排兄弟们坐席，他们彼此惊奇。约瑟把自己面前的食物分给他们，给便雅悯的比别人多五倍；他们就饮酒，与约瑟一同宴乐。

Joseph washes his face, returns, controls himself, and orders the meal served. They are seated separately: Joseph by himself, the brothers by themselves, and the Egyptians by themselves, for Egyptians do not eat with Hebrews. Joseph seats the brothers in birth order, and they look at one another in astonishment. Portions are served from Joseph’s table, and Benjamin’s portion is five times as much as the others; they drink and are merry with him.

经文含义 Meaning

桌席成为试验与恩典的场所

分席反映埃及社会界限，长幼排序显出约瑟对他们的认识，便雅悯五倍分量则预备下一章的试验：哥哥们会否再次嫉妒受宠的弟弟？然而整场饭也充满恩待，他们从惧怕被奴役进入同席欢宴。

The separate tables reflect Egyptian social boundaries, the birth-order seating reveals Joseph’s knowledge of them, and Benjamin’s fivefold portion prepares the next test: will the brothers again envy a favored younger brother? Yet the meal is also full of kindness; they move from fearing enslavement to sharing a feast.

灵修应用 Devotional

恩典也会显明我们的心

我们常以为试验只来自困难，但恩典也会测试我们：别人被看重时，我是否嫉妒？同事被提拔时，我是否失衡？桥梁原则是，神在餐桌般的日常场景中显明人的心，使我们学习为别人的恩典欢喜，而不是重演旧罪。

We often think testing comes only through hardship, but grace can test us too: do I envy another person’s honor, or lose balance when a colleague is promoted? The bridge principle is that God reveals the heart in ordinary table-like settings, teaching us to rejoice in grace given to others rather than reenact old sin.

方法论 Methodology

从经文原意到今日操练

From textual meaning to present practice

正统解经路径

Responsible exegesis

先观察创世记43章在约瑟叙事中的位置：42章显出哥哥们的罪疚，43章则让犹大担保、便雅悯同行、约瑟设席，为44章最终试验铺路。解释时注意叙事重复、人物转变、关键话语“平安，不要害怕”，避免把约瑟每个动作简化成道德口号。

Begin with Genesis 43 in the Joseph narrative: chapter 42 exposes guilt, chapter 43 brings Judah’s pledge, Benjamin’s presence, and Joseph’s feast, preparing the decisive test of chapter 44. Attend to repetition, character change, and the key word, “Peace; do not fear,” instead of reducing every action to a simple moral slogan.

灵修应用路径

Devotional application

再把经文原则带入今日处境：旧创伤可能制造控制，真实悔改会产生承担，神的平安使人诚实面对，恩典也会试验我们是否嫉妒。应用不越过经文原意，而是从家庭复和、职场责任、关系沟通和内心惧怕中寻找顺服的下一步。

Then bring the passage’s principles into present settings: old wounds may create control, real repentance produces responsibility, God’s peace enables honest facing of truth, and grace can test whether we envy. Application does not bypass textual meaning; it seeks the next obedient step in family repair, workplace responsibility, relational speech, and inner fear.

实际落地：先问“这段经文在故事中推进了什么？”再问“这项真理今天要求我承担、归还、说明、等候或祝福谁？”

Practice: first ask, “What does this passage advance in the story?” Then ask, “Whom does this truth call me to bear with, repay, explain to, wait for, or bless today?”
