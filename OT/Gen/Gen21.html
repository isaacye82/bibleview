创世记 21章 Genesis Chapter 21

本章核心主题 · Chapter Theme

应许成就中的欢笑与旷野眷顾

Laughter Fulfilled and Mercy in the Wilderness

创世记21章把多年等待推向可触摸的成就：以撒照神所定的时候出生，撒拉的笑从怀疑转为敬拜的喜乐。同时，夏甲和以实玛利被送入旷野，神却听见孩子的声音，开她的眼睛看见水井。本章同时显明应许的独特路线与神对边缘者的怜悯。

Genesis 21 brings years of waiting into visible fulfillment: Isaac is born at God's appointed time, and Sarah's laughter turns from uncertainty into worshipful joy. Yet Hagar and Ishmael are sent into the wilderness, where God hears the boy and opens Hagar's eyes to a well. The chapter holds together the unique line of promise and God's mercy toward the vulnerable.

阅读导向：应许的专一与怜悯的宽广

Reading lens: the particular promise and the wideness of mercy.

本章不能只读成家庭冲突，也不能只读成成功故事。经文一方面清楚说明应许之线要从以撒承接，另一方面又显明神没有丢弃夏甲与以实玛利。神既按所说的话成就，也在人的眼泪中开出水井。

This chapter should not be reduced to family conflict or triumphal success. Scripture makes clear that the line of promise continues through Isaac, while also showing that God does not abandon Hagar and Ishmael. God fulfills what he has spoken and opens a well in the place of tears.

逐段研读 · Passage Study

Five movements trace promise fulfilled, pain faced, mercy heard, covenant peace, and worship under the everlasting God.

21:1-7

耶和华按着先前的话眷顾撒拉，便照他所说的给撒拉成就。撒拉就给亚伯拉罕生了一个儿子，正当神所说的日期。亚伯拉罕给撒拉所生的儿子起名叫以撒。撒拉说：“神使我喜笑，凡听见的必与我一同喜笑。”

The LORD visited Sarah as he had said, and the LORD did unto Sarah as he had spoken. Sarah bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him. Abraham called the name of his son Isaac. Sarah said, "God hath made me to laugh, so that all that hear will laugh with me."

经文含义 Meaning

按所说的话成就

经文反复使用“按着先前的话”“照他所说的”“正当神所说的日期”，把焦点放在神话语的可靠性上。以撒的出生不是偶然的迟来祝福，而是神在不可能处境中精准守约。撒拉的笑也被更新：先前的疑惑与惊讶，如今成为可分享的喜乐见证。

The repeated phrases "as he had said," "as he had spoken," and "at the set time" place the emphasis on the reliability of God's word. Isaac's birth is not accidental relief but precise covenant fulfillment. Sarah's laughter is transformed from uncertainty into public joy.

灵修应用 Devotional

等待不等于被忘记

当生活长期没有动静时，人容易把沉默解释成神忘记了自己。本段给我们的桥梁原则是：神的时间并不被人的焦虑推迟或催熟。今天面对家庭压力、身体软弱或职业停滞时，可以把“神所说的日期”转化为祷告中的安稳：我仍负责任行动，但不把结果当成自己必须掌控的证明。

Long seasons of silence can make us feel forgotten. This passage teaches that God's timing is not delayed or ripened by human anxiety. In family pressure, bodily weakness, or career stagnation, we can act responsibly while refusing to make outcomes the proof that we are in control.

21:8-13

孩子渐长，就断了奶。亚伯拉罕在以撒断奶的日子设摆丰盛的筵席。撒拉看见埃及人夏甲给亚伯拉罕所生的儿子戏笑，就对亚伯拉罕说：“你把这使女和她儿子赶出去。”神对亚伯拉罕说：“凡撒拉对你说的话，你都该听从；因为从以撒生的，才要称为你的后裔。至于使女的儿子，我也必使他的后裔成立一国，因为他是你所生的。”

The child grew, and was weaned: and Abraham made a great feast the same day that Isaac was weaned. Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian mocking, and said unto Abraham, "Cast out this bondwoman and her son." God said, "In Isaac shall thy seed be called. And also of the son of the bondwoman will I make a nation, because he is thy seed."

经文含义 Meaning

应许路线与家庭痛楚

以撒断奶的筵席象征应许孩子公开进入家庭未来，但以实玛利的“戏笑”引发深层张力。神确认应许之线要从以撒承接，同时没有否定以实玛利的价值，仍因他是亚伯拉罕的儿子而给他民族性的应许。本段既严肃处理界限，也保留怜悯。

Isaac's weaning feast publicly marks the promised child as part of the family's future, yet Ishmael's mocking exposes deep conflict. God confirms that the promise will be reckoned through Isaac, while still promising a nation to Ishmael because he is Abraham's son. Boundaries and mercy stand together.

灵修应用 Devotional

清楚界限中的温柔信靠

现代关系中，界限常被误解为冷漠，怜悯也常被误解为没有原则。本段提醒我们：忠于神给出的道路，有时需要面对痛苦决定；但我们不能因此把他人贬低为无关紧要。处理家庭冲突、同事竞争或旧有关系时，可以学习既诚实设限，又把对方最终交托给神的看顾。

In modern relationships, boundaries are often mistaken for coldness, and mercy for lack of principle. This passage teaches that faithfulness may require painful clarity without treating others as disposable. In family conflict or workplace tension, we can set honest limits while entrusting people to God's care.

21:14-21

亚伯拉罕清早起来，拿饼和一皮袋水给了夏甲，搭在她的肩上，又把孩子交给她，打发她走。水用尽了，夏甲把孩子撇在小树底下，自己相对而坐，说：“我不忍见孩子死。”神听见童子的声音；神的使者从天上呼叫夏甲说：“夏甲，你为何这样呢？不要害怕，神已经听见童子的声音了。”神使夏甲的眼睛明亮，她就看见一口水井。

Abraham rose up early, took bread and a bottle of water, and gave it unto Hagar, sending her away with the child. When the water was spent, she cast the child under one of the shrubs and said, "Let me not see the death of the child." God heard the voice of the lad; the angel of God called to Hagar, "Fear not; for God hath heard the voice of the lad where he is." God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water.

经文含义 Meaning

旷野中被听见的哭声

旷野场景把人的无助推到极限：水尽、孩子被放在树下、母亲不忍观看死亡。然而经文说神听见“童子的声音”，并直接对夏甲说“不要害怕”。神不是只眷顾应许主线中的人物；他也在边缘与绝境中施行保全，使夏甲看见已经在那里预备的水井。

The wilderness brings human helplessness to the edge: water is gone, the child lies beneath a shrub, and the mother cannot bear to watch death. Yet God hears the boy and speaks to Hagar, "Fear not." God cares not only for the central promise line but also preserves the vulnerable at the margins.

灵修应用 Devotional

开眼看见近处的井

有些时刻，人的问题不只是没有资源，而是绝望遮住了眼睛，看不见神已经摆在近处的供应。职场失利、亲子焦虑或被关系排挤时，我们可以把夏甲的经历化为祷告：主啊，求你先听见我，也开我的眼睛，使我看见下一步可以领受的水井、帮助与路。

Sometimes the issue is not only lack of resources but despair that blinds us to provision already near. In career setbacks, parenting anxiety, or relational exclusion, Hagar's story becomes a prayer: Lord, hear me and open my eyes to the well, the help, and the next step you provide.

21:22-34

那时，亚比米勒同他军长非各对亚伯拉罕说：“凡你所行的事都有神的保佑。”亚伯拉罕因亚比米勒的仆人霸占了一口水井，就责备亚比米勒。二人彼此立约，亚伯拉罕把七只母羊羔另放在一处，作为他挖这口井的证据。所以那地方叫作别是巴，因为他们二人在那里起了誓。亚伯拉罕在别是巴栽上一棵垂丝柳树，又在那里求告耶和华永生神的名。

Abimelech and Phichol said unto Abraham, "God is with thee in all that thou doest." Abraham reproved Abimelech because of a well of water, which Abimelech's servants had violently taken away. They made a covenant, and Abraham set seven ewe lambs apart as a witness that he had digged the well. Wherefore he called that place Beersheba. Abraham planted a grove in Beersheba, and called there on the name of the LORD, the everlasting God.

经文含义 Meaning

和平、证据与敬拜

亚比米勒承认神与亚伯拉罕同在，但亚伯拉罕仍指出水井被霸占的问题。立约并非逃避现实冲突，而是用明确证据、公开誓言和彼此承认来建立和平。别是巴的水井与垂丝柳树相连：一个关乎生存权利，一个指向长期居留与敬拜永生神。

Abimelech recognizes that God is with Abraham, yet Abraham still addresses the seized well. Covenant peace does not avoid real conflict; it uses evidence, public oath, and mutual acknowledgment to restore order. Beersheba joins a well of survival with a tamarisk of long-term worship before the everlasting God.

灵修应用 Devotional

用诚实建立可居住的和平

信靠神并不意味着避免谈判、证据和边界。亚伯拉罕在别是巴的行动鼓励我们：在合同、团队职责、邻里关系或教会服事中，和平需要真实沟通和清楚记号。更深的根基则是敬拜“永生神”，知道人的协议有限，但神的信实长存。

Trusting God does not mean avoiding negotiation, evidence, or boundaries. Abraham's actions at Beersheba encourage honest communication in contracts, team duties, neighborly life, or church service. Deeper still, worship of the everlasting God steadies us when human agreements remain limited.

方法论总结 · Methodology

从经文原意到今日生命

From textual meaning to present-day life, the chapter is read through two coordinated paths.

正统解经路径 · Exegetical Path

先观察叙事顺序、重复词语与上下文承接：创世记21章接续20章对撒拉的保护，并兑现17至18章关于以撒出生的应许。读者要区分“应许从以撒承接”与“神仍眷顾以实玛利”这两条同时存在的线索。

The exegetical path observes narrative order, repeated wording, and context: Genesis 21 follows the protection of Sarah in chapter 20 and fulfills the promises of chapters 17-18. Readers distinguish the promise through Isaac from God's continuing care for Ishmael.

灵修应用路径 · Devotional Path

再把经文原则转化为生活桥梁：等待中的信靠、关系中的界限、绝望中的祷告、和平中的诚实。应用不是把自己随意代入每个角色，而是从神如何信实、听见、供应和引导人的行动中学习回应。

The devotional path builds bridges into life: trust in waiting, boundaries in relationships, prayer in despair, and honesty in peacemaking. Application does not force us into every role; it learns response from the way God fulfills, hears, provides, and guides.

实际落地指南 · Practical Guide

阅读本章时，可先问：“神在本段中说过什么、成就什么、听见什么？”再问：“这如何塑造我今天的等待、界限、眼光和敬拜？”这样既避免脱离上下文的主观发挥，也避免只停留在知识层面的旁观。

When reading this chapter, first ask, "What has God spoken, fulfilled, and heard in this passage?" Then ask, "How does this shape my waiting, boundaries, perception, and worship today?" This guards against detached subjectivity and against knowledge without response.
