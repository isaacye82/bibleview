创世记 46章 Genesis Chapter 46

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

下埃及：应许中的迁徙与同在

Going Down to Egypt: Migration Held by Promise and Presence

创世记第 46 章把雅各全家从迦南带到埃及。表面上，这是饥荒中的迁徙；在更深层面，这是神按祂对亚伯拉罕所说的话，把一家人带进将要成为大族的历史阶段。雅各先在别是巴献祭，神在夜间异象中应许：“不要害怕下埃及去，因为我必使你在那里成为大族；我要和你同下埃及去，也必定带你上来。”随后经文列出随行家谱，显示神不只看见一个抽象民族，也记念每一个名字。最后，约瑟与父亲重逢，并安排家人住在歌珊，保存他们的身份和生活方式。

Genesis 46 moves Jacob’s household from Canaan to Egypt. On the surface it is a famine migration; at a deeper level it is the next stage of God’s promise to Abraham, where one family will become a great nation. Jacob worships at Beersheba, and God speaks in a night vision: do not fear going down to Egypt, for He will go down with Jacob and bring him up again. The genealogy shows that God remembers names, not merely an abstract people. The chapter closes with Joseph’s reunion with his father and the family’s settlement strategy in Goshen, preserving their identity within Egypt.

研读视图 / Study View

创世记 46 章经文双轨研读

本章按叙事推进分为别是巴异象、家谱数算、父子重逢与歌珊安排。每个单元都从上下文经文含义走向今日的迁徙、家庭、身份与信心应用。

The chapter unfolds through the Beersheba vision, the counted household, the father-son reunion, and the Goshen arrangement. Each unit moves from contextual meaning to present application in migration, family, identity, and faith.

God Comforts Jacob at Beersheba

神在别是巴安慰雅各 (46:1-7)

46:1-4

以色列带着一切所有的起行，来到别是巴，就献祭给他父亲以撒的神。夜间，神在异象中呼叫雅各；神说：“不要害怕下埃及去，因为我必使你在那里成为大族。我要和你同下埃及去，也必定带你上来；约瑟必给你送终。”

Israel set out with all that he had and came to Beersheba, where he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. In a night vision God called Jacob and said, “Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, for I will make you into a great nation there. I will go down with you, and I will surely bring you up again; Joseph’s hand will close your eyes.”

经文含义 Meaning

应许没有被地点限制

别是巴靠近迦南南界，雅各在这里停下献祭，说明他知道离开应许之地不是小事。神的回答直接触及他的恐惧：埃及不是应许的终点，却是成就应许的阶段。神重复“我”所要做的事，把主动权从饥荒和政治势力中取回，放在祂自己的信实里。

Beersheba stands near the southern edge of Canaan, so Jacob pauses in worship before crossing a major boundary. God answers the fear beneath the journey: Egypt is not the goal of the promise, but it is a stage in its fulfillment. The repeated divine “I” grounds the move in God’s faithful initiative rather than in famine or empire.

灵修应用 Devotional

带着敬拜进入未知

当生活把我们推向陌生环境时，信心不是假装没有不安，而是先把决定带到神面前。职场转换、城市迁移、家庭责任变化，都可能像“下埃及”一样让人害怕。本段邀请我们问：我是在被压力驱赶，还是在神面前寻求确认？神未必把路变短，却应许祂的同在先于我们的到达。

When life moves us into unfamiliar places, faith does not pretend there is no anxiety; it brings the decision before God. A job change, relocation, or family responsibility can feel like going down to Egypt. This passage invites us to ask whether we are merely driven by pressure or seeking God’s confirmation. The road may not become shorter, but His presence goes with His people.

46:5-7

雅各从别是巴起行；以色列的儿子们使他们的父亲雅各和他们的妻子、儿女，都坐在法老为雅各送来的车上。他们带着牲畜和所得的财物，来到埃及。雅各和他的一切后裔都一同来了。

Jacob departed from Beersheba. Israel’s sons carried their father, their children, and their wives in the wagons Pharaoh had sent. They brought their livestock and possessions acquired in Canaan, and Jacob came to Egypt with all his offspring.

经文含义 Meaning

全家进入应许的下一幕

经文强调“所有”“一切后裔”，不是为了旅行细节，而是要说明神的计划正在从个人族长转向整个家族共同进入历史。法老的车辆成为神护理的一部分，外邦资源也被用来扶持应许之家。

The stress on “all” Jacob’s offspring is not a travel note only; it marks the movement from an individual patriarch to a whole household entering the next stage of sacred history. Even Pharaoh’s wagons become part of providence, as foreign resources serve the family of promise.

灵修应用 Devotional

信心也照顾同行的人

雅各不是单独冒险，而是带着老人、妻子、孩子和财物一起前行。今天许多决定也会影响家人、团队或教会群体。属灵应用不是只问“我有没有勇气”，也要问“我有没有负责任地照顾同行者”。信心可以大胆，同时也可以细致、周到、有秩序。

Jacob does not move alone; the elderly, wives, children, and possessions are all carried along. Many modern decisions affect families, teams, or churches. Spiritual application asks not only whether I have courage, but whether I am caring responsibly for those who travel with me. Faith can be bold and also careful, practical, and ordered.

The Genealogy Shows God Numbering His People

家谱见证神数算祂的百姓 (46:8-27)

46:8-15

来到埃及的以色列人名字记在下面：雅各和他的儿孙。利亚给雅各所生的儿孙，连同女儿底拿，共三十三人。

These are the names of the Israelites who came to Egypt: Jacob and his descendants. The sons and descendants through Leah, together with Dinah, numbered thirty-three persons.

经文含义 Meaning

名字承载记忆

家谱在叙事中放慢速度，使读者看见迁徙不是无名人潮，而是一个个被神记念的生命。利亚支派的名单也提醒人，神的应许常通过复杂家庭史继续推进；人的偏爱、伤痕和竞争没有取消神的计划。

The genealogy slows the narrative so that the migration is not a nameless crowd but a collection of lives remembered by God. The Leah list also reminds readers that God’s promise moves through complicated family histories; human favoritism, wounds, and rivalry do not cancel His purposes.

灵修应用 Devotional

在名单中看见人的重量

现代生活容易把人变成数据、工号、绩效或群聊头像。创世记用名字提醒我们，每个人在神面前都有重量。无论是在家庭照顾、职场管理还是教会服事中，灵修的回应是重新学习称呼、聆听和记念具体的人，而不只处理“事情”。

Modern life easily turns people into data, employee numbers, metrics, or profile icons. Genesis answers with names: every person carries weight before God. In family care, workplace leadership, or church service, a devotional response is to relearn naming, listening, and remembering concrete people, not merely managing tasks.

46:16-22

迦得、亚设以及拉结所生约瑟和便雅悯的后代也列在其中。约瑟在埃及已经有两个儿子：玛拿西和以法莲，是安城祭司波提非拉的女儿亚西纳给他生的。

The descendants of Gad and Asher are listed, along with the line of Rachel through Joseph and Benjamin. Joseph already had two sons in Egypt, Manasseh and Ephraim, born to him by Asenath, daughter of Potiphera, priest of On.

经文含义 Meaning

埃及中的后代也被纳入

约瑟的两个儿子出生在埃及，名字和环境都带着异乡印记，但他们仍被列入雅各家。经文预备后面以法莲、玛拿西成为以色列支派的事实，显明神能在离散和异文化处境中保存并扩展祂的百姓。

Joseph’s sons were born in Egypt and carry the marks of a foreign setting, yet they are included in Jacob’s household. This prepares for Ephraim and Manasseh becoming tribes in Israel and shows that God can preserve and enlarge His people in diaspora and cross-cultural settings.

灵修应用 Devotional

异乡不必等于失根

许多人在海外、外地城市或跨文化职场中生活，担心下一代失去属灵身份。本段不鼓励封闭恐惧，却提醒我们主动传递信仰记忆：讲述神的作为，建立敬拜节奏，给孩子和新人一个可归属的故事。环境会塑造人，但神的恩典也能在环境中扎根。

Many live overseas, in distant cities, or in cross-cultural workplaces and worry that the next generation may lose spiritual identity. This passage does not encourage fearful isolation, but calls us to pass on faith memory: telling God’s works, shaping rhythms of worship, and giving children or newcomers a story of belonging. Environment shapes people, but grace can take root within it.

46:23-27

但、拿弗他利和雅各众子的后代也在名单中。凡从雅各而生、来到埃及的，不包括他儿妇，共六十六人；再加上约瑟在埃及所生的两个儿子，雅各家来到埃及的共有七十人。

The descendants of Dan, Naphtali, and the rest of Jacob’s sons are counted. Those born to Jacob who came into Egypt numbered sixty-six, not counting the sons’ wives; with Joseph’s two sons in Egypt, Jacob’s household in Egypt totaled seventy persons.

经文含义 Meaning

七十人的种子

“七十”在经文中呈现完整家族进入埃及的画面。人数不大，却是后来民族扩展的种子。创世记把这小小群体放在神宏大的应许中阅读，提醒读者不要用眼前规模判断神计划的真实。

The number seventy presents a complete household entering Egypt. It is small, yet it is the seed of later national growth. Genesis places this modest group within God’s vast promise, warning readers not to measure God’s plan only by present size.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要轻看小开始

神常把未来放进看似微小的起点里：一个家庭祭坛、一次诚实道歉、一小群稳定查经、一个愿意忠心的人。我们容易因规模小而灰心，但本章提醒我们，神看见种子里的未来。今天要忠于已经托付给你的“小数目”。

God often places the future inside small beginnings: a family altar, one honest apology, a small steady Bible study, one faithful person. We may become discouraged by small scale, but this chapter teaches us that God sees the future within the seed. Be faithful today with the “small number” entrusted to you.

Joseph Reunites with Jacob and Prepares Goshen

约瑟与雅各重逢，并预备住在歌珊 (46:28-34)

46:28-30

雅各打发犹大先去见约瑟，请他引路往歌珊去。他们来到歌珊地。约瑟套车往歌珊去迎接父亲以色列，见了面，就伏在父亲颈项上哭了许久。以色列说：“我既得见你的面，知道你还活着，就是死我也甘心。”

Jacob sent Judah ahead to Joseph to show the way to Goshen. Joseph prepared his chariot and went to meet his father Israel in Goshen. When he appeared before him, he fell on his father’s neck and wept a long time. Israel said, “Now I can die, since I have seen your face and know that you are still alive.”

经文含义 Meaning

哀伤被重逢医治

犹大先行，显示这个曾经导致约瑟被卖的家庭正在被重组；约瑟与雅各拥抱痛哭，则让多年失丧进入真实安慰。经文没有把信心写成冷静无泪，而是让应许在人的眼泪中成形。

Judah going ahead signals a family once fractured by Joseph’s sale being reordered. Joseph and Jacob’s tearful embrace brings years of loss into real consolation. Scripture does not portray faith as tearless composure; promise takes shape in human grief and comfort.

灵修应用 Devotional

让和好有真实情感

有些关系的修复需要时间，也需要允许眼泪出现。属灵成熟不是把情绪压扁，而是在神的恩典中诚实面对失去、亏欠、等待和重逢。今天若你正在修复家庭或朋友关系，可以从一个真实、谦卑、不过度控制的相见开始。

Some reconciliations require time and room for tears. Spiritual maturity does not flatten emotion; it faces loss, guilt, waiting, and reunion honestly before God. If you are repairing a family or friendship, begin with a truthful, humble encounter that does not try to control everything.

46:31-34

约瑟告诉弟兄和父家，他要上去禀告法老，说他们从迦南地来了，素来以牧养牲畜为业，并带着羊群牛群和一切所有。约瑟指示他们若法老问起职业，就说仆人从幼年直到如今都以牧养牲畜为业，好叫他们可以住在歌珊地，因为埃及人厌恶一切牧羊的人。

Joseph told his brothers and his father’s household that he would report to Pharaoh that they had come from Canaan with their flocks, herds, and possessions, and that they had always been keepers of livestock. If Pharaoh asked their occupation, they should say the same, so that they might live in Goshen, because shepherds were detestable to the Egyptians.

经文含义 Meaning

智慧地保持身份边界

约瑟没有让家人隐藏身份，也没有使他们完全融入埃及宫廷。他用埃及人的社会偏见反而为家族争取歌珊，使他们有空间继续牧养生活。经文展现一种务实智慧：在外邦体系中诚实表达身份，并安排能保存使命的环境。

Joseph does not ask his family to hide their identity or dissolve into Egyptian court life. He uses Egyptian social assumptions to secure Goshen, where the family can continue its pastoral way of life. The passage shows practical wisdom: speak truthfully about identity within a foreign system and arrange an environment that preserves calling.

灵修应用 Devotional

在融入与分别之间有智慧

基督徒生活在职场、学校和城市文化中，既不能轻率逃离，也不能失去身份。约瑟的安排提醒我们：可以尊重制度、使用合宜语言、做专业沟通，同时为敬拜、家庭、伦理和群体生活留下边界。真正的智慧不是讨好所有人，而是让神托付的身份能长期存活。

Christians live in workplaces, schools, and urban cultures; we should neither flee carelessly nor lose our identity. Joseph’s plan reminds us that we can respect systems, use appropriate language, and communicate professionally while keeping boundaries for worship, family, ethics, and community. True wisdom is not pleasing everyone; it helps God-given identity endure.

方法论 / Methodology

从经文原意走向今日应用

创世记 46 章不能只读成温情团圆故事，也不能只读成迁徙管理案例。正统解经先看它在创世记大叙事中的位置：神对亚伯拉罕的应许正在通过雅各家进入埃及而推进。灵修应用随后才问：当神带我们进入未知、跨文化环境或家庭重组时，我们如何在敬拜、同在应许和身份边界中回应。

Genesis 46 should not be reduced either to a sentimental reunion or to a migration-management case study. Sound interpretation first locates it within Genesis: God’s promise to Abraham is advancing as Jacob’s household enters Egypt. Devotional application then asks how we respond when God leads us into uncertainty, cross-cultural settings, and family reordering through worship, promised presence, and identity boundaries.

正统解经原则

Principles for contextual interpretation

  • 观察上下文：本章承接约瑟救全家的叙事，也预备出埃及记中以色列成为大族。Read the chapter between Joseph’s preservation of the family and Exodus’s national growth.
  • 尊重经文结构：异象、名单、重逢、安排四部分共同说明神既掌管大历史，也记念具体家庭。Vision, list, reunion, and settlement together show providence over history and households.
  • 避免抽离应许：雅各下埃及不是离弃迦南，而是神按约推进救赎历史。Going to Egypt is not abandonment of Canaan but covenant history moving forward.

灵修应用路径

A bridge from ancient truth to present life

  • 从敬拜开始：重大转向前，先像雅各在别是巴一样，把恐惧和计划带到神面前。Begin major transitions with worship, bringing fear and plans before God.
  • 在同在中前行：神可能呼召人进入陌生处境，但祂的同在使未知不再等于被弃。God may lead into unfamiliar settings, but His presence means uncertainty is not abandonment.
  • 智慧持守身份：约瑟安排歌珊，提醒我们在制度内沟通，也为信仰、家庭和群体保留边界。Joseph’s Goshen strategy teaches communication within systems and boundaries for faith and community.
