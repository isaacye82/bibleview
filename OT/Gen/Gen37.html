创世记 37章 Genesis Chapter 37

本章核心主题 Core Theme

梦、嫉妒与深坑：神在破碎家庭中的隐藏引导

Dreams, Envy, and the Pit: God’s Hidden Guidance in a Fractured Family

创世记37章开启约瑟叙事。父亲偏爱、兄弟嫉妒、梦的显明、彩衣被剥夺、约瑟被卖到埃及，这一连串破裂事件并没有直接提到神的名字，却在叙事结构中预备后来的拯救。经文让读者看见：人的罪会制造真实伤害，但神能在看似失控的道路中推进更深的旨意。

Genesis 37 begins the Joseph narrative. Parental favoritism, brotherly envy, revealed dreams, the stripped robe, and Joseph’s sale into Egypt unfold without an explicit mention of God’s name, yet the story quietly prepares later deliverance. Human sin causes real harm, but God can advance a deeper purpose through roads that appear out of control.

Favoritism and Fracture

The Disclosure of Dreams

Hidden Providence

本章没有明说神行动，却让读者在后文回望时看见祂掌管路径。沉默不等于缺席。 The chapter does not explicitly name God’s action, yet later hindsight shows His rule; silence is not absence.

创世记37章逐段双轨研读

A bilingual two-track study through Genesis Chapter 37

本章需要同时读家庭层面的罪、梦的叙事伏笔，以及神隐藏护理的长线推进。每一段都把人的责任与神的掌管并列呈现。 This chapter should be read through family sin, narrative foreshadowing, and God’s long hidden providence. Each unit holds human responsibility and divine rule together.

37:1-4

雅各住在迦南地。约瑟十七岁，与哥哥们一同牧羊，并把他们的恶行报给父亲。以色列爱约瑟过于众子，因为他是年老所生的儿子，就给他做了一件彩衣。哥哥们见父亲爱约瑟过于爱他们，就恨约瑟，不能与他说和睦的话。

Jacob lived in Canaan. Joseph, seventeen years old, shepherded with his brothers and brought a bad report about them to his father. Israel loved Joseph more than all his sons and made him a richly ornamented robe. His brothers saw the favoritism, hated him, and could not speak peaceably to him.

经文含义 Meaning

偏爱的外衣成为裂痕的标志

叙事一开始把雅各家放在应许之地，却立刻显出家庭内部的失序。约瑟的报告、雅各的偏爱、哥哥们的仇恨彼此交织。彩衣不是单纯美丽衣物，而是公开的偏爱记号，使原本隐藏的竞争变成明面冲突。

The setting is the land of promise, but the household is disordered. Joseph’s report, Jacob’s favoritism, and the brothers’ hatred converge. The robe publicly marks preference and turns private rivalry into open hostility.

灵修应用 Devotional

爱需要公义的形状

在家庭、团队或教会中，偏袒会让被忽略者把痛苦转成敌意。桥梁原则是：真实的爱不只表达亲近，也要维护公平、聆听和透明。今天我们可以检视自己是否用资源、称赞或机会制造了不必要的比较。

In families, teams, or churches, favoritism can turn neglected pain into hostility. True love must take the shape of fairness, listening, and transparency. We can examine whether our praise, resources, or opportunities create needless comparison.

37:5-11

约瑟做了梦，告诉哥哥们，他们就越发恨他。他梦见自己的禾捆起来站着，哥哥们的禾捆围着下拜；又梦见太阳、月亮与十一个星向他下拜。哥哥们嫉妒他，父亲却把这话存在心里。

Joseph dreamed and told his brothers, and they hated him even more. He saw his sheaf standing upright while theirs bowed down; he also saw the sun, moon, and eleven stars bowing to him. His brothers envied him, but his father kept the matter in mind.

经文含义 Meaning

梦是伏笔，也是试验

两个梦都指向未来的俯伏，但现在只激化关系。经文没有把约瑟塑造成完美少年，也没有否定梦的意义；它让读者同时看见神启示的真实性，以及承受启示者仍需学习谦卑和等候。

Both dreams point to future bowing, yet in the present they intensify conflict. The text neither presents Joseph as flawless nor dismisses the dreams. Revelation is real, but the one who receives it must still learn humility and patience.

灵修应用 Devotional

异象要经过品格承载

我们可能很快说出自己的梦想、呼召或规划，却忽略听者的处境。桥梁原则是：神赐的方向需要用谦卑表达，并用长期忠心验证。职场和服事中，越重要的异象，越需要成熟的语言、时机和可受教的心。

We may quickly announce dreams, calling, or plans without considering those who hear us. God-given direction should be spoken humbly and tested by long faithfulness. The greater the vision, the more it needs mature words, timing, and teachability.

37:12-17

约瑟的哥哥们往示剑放羊。以色列吩咐约瑟去看哥哥们和羊群平安不平安。约瑟到了示剑找不到他们，有人告诉他他们往多坍去了，于是约瑟追上哥哥们。

Joseph’s brothers went to pasture the flock near Shechem. Israel sent Joseph to see whether his brothers and the flock were well. Joseph did not find them at Shechem; a man told him they had gone to Dothan, so Joseph followed and found them there.

经文含义 Meaning

顺服走进未知路径

雅各差遣约瑟查看平安，形成强烈反差：他寻找的是平安，却走向背叛。示剑曾是暴力记忆之地，多坍成为转折地点。叙事用普通差遣把约瑟带入神长远计划的门口。

Jacob sends Joseph to seek peace, yet the journey leads toward betrayal. Shechem carries memories of violence, and Dothan becomes the turning point. An ordinary errand brings Joseph to the threshold of a long providential plan.

灵修应用 Devotional

平凡差遣也在神手中

许多人生转折不是从宏大选择开始，而是从一次普通责任开始。桥梁原则是：我们不能控制每个结果，却能在眼前托付上忠心。面对看似平凡的任务，信心不是预知结局，而是在神面前正直行走。

Many turning points begin not with dramatic choices but with ordinary responsibility. We cannot control every outcome, but we can be faithful in the task before us. Faith is not knowing the ending; it is walking uprightly before God.

37:18-24

哥哥们远远看见约瑟，就同谋要害死他，说：“那做梦的来了。”流便劝他们不可流血，只把他丢在坑里，想要救他回到父亲那里。他们剥了约瑟的彩衣，把他丢在坑里；那坑是空的，里面没有水。

His brothers saw Joseph from afar and plotted to kill him, saying, “Here comes this dreamer.” Reuben urged them not to shed blood but to put him in a pit, intending to rescue him later. They stripped Joseph of his robe and threw him into an empty pit with no water.

经文含义 Meaning

嫉妒从言语走向暴力

“那做梦的”带着讥讽，显示哥哥们攻击的不只是约瑟本人，也是在抵挡梦所预示的未来。流便有拦阻杀害的意图，却没有公开承担足够责任。彩衣被剥和空坑描绘约瑟身份、保护与生命安全被一步步夺去。

“This dreamer” is contemptuous; the brothers resist not only Joseph but the future suggested by the dreams. Reuben intends rescue but does not shoulder responsibility openly. The stripped robe and empty pit show identity, protection, and safety being removed.

灵修应用 Devotional

半心的拦阻仍不够

当群体走向伤害时，沉默或含糊的善意常常挡不住罪。桥梁原则是：公义需要清楚的行动，不只是内心不同意。在职场霸凌、家庭冲突或群体排斥中，信心会推动我们承担代价去保护弱者。

When a group moves toward harm, silent or vague goodwill is often insufficient. Justice needs clear action, not merely private disagreement. In workplace bullying, family conflict, or social exclusion, faith calls us to protect the vulnerable at real cost.

37:25-28

哥哥们坐下吃饭，举目看见一伙以实玛利人从基列来，骆驼驮着香料、乳香、没药，要带下埃及。犹大提议不要杀约瑟，把他卖给商人。于是他们把约瑟从坑里拉上来，以二十舍客勒银子卖给以实玛利人，他们就把约瑟带到埃及。

The brothers sat down to eat and saw a caravan of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead with spices, balm, and myrrh bound for Egypt. Judah proposed selling Joseph instead of killing him. They lifted Joseph from the pit and sold him for twenty shekels of silver, and he was taken to Egypt.

经文含义 Meaning

罪把兄弟变成交易

他们在坑旁吃饭，显出良心麻木。犹大的建议避免流血，却把约瑟变成可出售的物品。去埃及的商队看似偶然出现，实则成为后文饥荒拯救叙事的必要通道。

Eating beside the pit displays a dulled conscience. Judah’s proposal avoids bloodshed but turns Joseph into merchandise. The caravan to Egypt appears incidental, yet it becomes the necessary route for the later story of deliverance in famine.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要用较小的恶包装自己

我们也可能用“至少我没有更坏”来合理化伤害。桥梁原则是：神看重的不只是避免最坏结果，也包括是否按真理对待人。工作决策、家庭谈判和利益分配中，不能把人当工具来换取自己的安全。

We may justify harm by saying, “At least I did not do worse.” God cares not only that we avoid the worst outcome, but that we treat people truthfully. In work decisions, family negotiations, and shared resources, people must not become tools for our security.

37:29-35

流便回来见约瑟不在坑里，就撕裂衣服。兄弟们宰了一只公山羊，把约瑟的彩衣染了血，送给父亲辨认。雅各认出彩衣，以为恶兽吞吃了约瑟，便撕裂衣服，为儿子悲哀多日，不肯受安慰。

Reuben returned and found Joseph gone from the pit, so he tore his clothes. The brothers slaughtered a goat, dipped Joseph’s robe in blood, and sent it to their father for recognition. Jacob recognized it, believed a wild beast had devoured Joseph, tore his garments, and mourned many days, refusing comfort.

经文含义 Meaning

欺骗回到欺骗者家中

染血彩衣让读者想起雅各曾用山羊和衣服欺骗父亲以撒。创世记不是简单说报应，而是显明罪会在家庭中留下回声。兄弟们没有亲口说约瑟死了，却设计证据让父亲自己得出结论，欺骗因此更冷酷。

The bloodied robe recalls Jacob’s earlier deception of Isaac through goats and garments. Genesis shows how sin echoes through a family. The brothers do not explicitly say Joseph is dead; they stage evidence so their father reaches the conclusion himself.

灵修应用 Devotional

未承认的罪会扩大哀伤

隐瞒常常让伤害持续发酵，使受害者活在错误信息中。桥梁原则是：悔改不只是停止作恶，也包括停止操控事实。若我们曾用沉默、剪裁信息或暗示伤害别人，今天就需要在神面前选择诚实。

Concealment lets harm keep growing and leaves others living under false information. Repentance is not only ceasing evil; it is also refusing to manipulate facts. If silence, edited information, or insinuation has harmed others, honesty before God is required.

37:36

米甸人把约瑟卖到埃及，给法老的内臣、护卫长波提乏。

The Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard.

经文含义 Meaning

结尾不是终局，而是门槛

本章最后把约瑟定位在埃及权力体系附近。对雅各而言，约瑟像是失去；对读者而言，故事被推进到神将要使用的地点。经文以悬念结束，邀请人等候神在下一幕中显明护理。

The final verse places Joseph near Egypt’s power structure. To Jacob, Joseph appears lost; to the reader, he has been moved to the place God will use. The chapter ends with tension and invites patient trust in the next movement of providence.

灵修应用 Devotional

神能把门槛藏在损失里

人生中有些转场看起来只是被动承受：调动、失去、误解或离开熟悉环境。桥梁原则是：我们不把恶称为善，却相信神能在恶之后仍开路。今天可以把无法解释的处境交给神，并在新位置上保持忠心。

Some transitions feel purely passive: relocation, loss, misunderstanding, or leaving what is familiar. We do not call evil good, yet we trust God can open a road after evil. We can entrust unexplained circumstances to Him and remain faithful in the new place.

方法论 Methodology

如何读创世记37章

How to read Genesis 37 responsibly and devotionally

正统解经原则

Contextual exegesis

先观察叙事结构：雅各家的偏爱、约瑟的梦、哥哥们的嫉妒、深坑和埃及路线。再注意创世记前后呼应，例如衣服与山羊的欺骗主题，以及后来约瑟在埃及被高举的伏笔。不要把每个细节任意寓意化，而要让上下文决定重点。

Begin with narrative structure: favoritism, dreams, envy, the pit, and the road to Egypt. Then notice canonical echoes such as garments, goats, deception, and Joseph’s later elevation. Avoid arbitrary allegory; let context govern emphasis.

灵修应用路径

Devotional application

应用不是把自己直接等同约瑟，而是从经文原则进入现实：偏爱会伤害关系，嫉妒会扭曲判断，沉默的善意需要变成公义行动，神的护理常在尚未看见时运行。把这些原则带入家庭沟通、职场竞争和受伤后的信靠。

Application does not simply identify us with Joseph. It draws principles into life: favoritism wounds relationships, envy distorts judgment, private goodwill must become just action, and God’s providence often works before it is visible.

落地指南 Practical Guide

研读时先问：“这段在约瑟故事中推动了什么？”再问：“这段显明人的哪些责任与神怎样隐藏掌管？”最后问：“我今天在哪个关系或处境中需要停止嫉妒、承认事实、保护受伤者，或在看不见结果时继续忠心？”

Ask first, “What does this unit advance in Joseph’s story?” Then ask, “What human responsibility and hidden divine rule are revealed?” Finally ask, “Where must I stop envy, tell the truth, protect the wounded, or remain faithful without seeing the outcome?”
