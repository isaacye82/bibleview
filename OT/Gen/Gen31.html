创世记 31章 Genesis Chapter 31

Genesis 31 Overview · 创世记三十一章全景

出走与立约：神在不公中守护应许

Departure and Covenant: God Guards the Promise amid Injustice

创世记31章描写雅各在拉班家二十年后终于离开。拉班的脸色改变，儿子们议论雅各夺取家产，神却吩咐雅各回到本地本族。拉结和利亚承认父家对她们并无公义，雅各于是带着家眷和牲畜出走。拉班追赶而来，神在梦中拦阻他的恶意；最后双方在基列山立石为证，划定界限，承认神监察人与人之间看不见的关系。

Genesis 31 tells how Jacob finally leaves Laban after twenty years. Laban’s attitude changes, his sons accuse Jacob of taking their wealth, and God commands Jacob to return to his land and kindred. Rachel and Leah acknowledge the injustice of their father’s house, so Jacob departs with his family and flocks. Laban pursues, but God restrains him in a dream; at Gilead the two men raise a witness heap, set a boundary, and confess that God watches hidden relationships.

动态意象：虚线道路象征雅各出走与拉班追赶；中央石柱和石堆轻微浮动，表示界限被立起，也被神鉴察。 The moving road portrays departure and pursuit; the pillar and stones mark a boundary watched by God.

创世记31章逐段双轨研读

Bilingual study path through Genesis Chapter 31

以下按叙事推进拆分本章：神呼召雅各离开、拉结偷取家神、拉班追赶、雅各申诉，以及基列山立约设界。 The study follows God’s call to leave, Rachel’s stolen household gods, Laban’s pursuit, Jacob’s protest, and the covenant boundary at Gilead.

神呼召雅各离开拉班 (31:1-16)

God Calls Jacob Away from Laban

31:1-3

雅各听见拉班的儿子们说，雅各把他们父亲所有的都夺去了；他也看见拉班的脸色不如从前。耶和华对雅各说：“你要回你祖、你父之地，到你亲族那里去，我必与你同在。”

Jacob heard Laban’s sons claim that he had taken their father’s wealth, and he saw that Laban’s attitude was no longer favorable. The LORD said to Jacob, “Return to the land of your fathers and to your kindred, and I will be with you.”

经文含义 Meaning

环境改变与神清楚的命令

经文把外在压力和神的话放在一起。拉班家的气氛已经改变，但雅各离开的根基不是单纯的危机判断，而是神再次确认“我必与你同在”。这回应伯特利的应许，使雅各的路不是无根漂流，而是回到神所指定的方向。

The text places external pressure beside God’s word. Laban’s household has changed, but Jacob’s departure rests not merely on crisis management; it rests on God’s renewed promise, “I will be with you.” This echoes Bethel and turns Jacob’s movement into a return toward God’s appointed direction.

灵修应用 Devotional

在压力中辨认呼召

现代人也会遇到环境变冷、关系变僵、职场不再公平的时候。桥梁原则是：压力可以提醒我们留意神的带领，却不能代替祷告和清楚的顺服。今天面对转向时，可以问：我是在逃避不舒服，还是在回应神已经显明的责任与方向？

We also face chilled relationships, strained workplaces, and unfair systems. The bridge principle is that pressure may alert us to God’s leading, but it cannot replace prayerful obedience. When considering a change, ask whether you are escaping discomfort or responding to a direction and responsibility God has made clear.

31:4-13

雅各召拉结和利亚到田野羊群那里，说明她们父亲的脸色改变，十次改了他的工价；但神没有容许拉班害他。神在梦中提醒他，祂看见拉班所做的一切，并吩咐他离开这地，回到本族本地。

Jacob called Rachel and Leah to the field and explained that their father’s attitude had changed and that he had changed Jacob’s wages many times. Yet God had not allowed Laban to harm him. In a dream God reminded Jacob that He had seen all Laban had done and commanded him to leave and return home.

经文含义 Meaning

神看见长期的不公

“十次改了工价”表达拉班反复压榨雅各。雅各并非无罪完人，但这一章强调神看见不公，且能在复杂劳动关系中保守人。神对“有纹、有点、有斑”的牲畜主权安排，也说明祝福最终不由拉班的合约控制。

The repeated changing of wages shows Laban’s sustained exploitation. Jacob is not portrayed as flawless, yet this chapter stresses that God sees injustice and preserves His servant within a complicated labor relationship. The patterned animals also show that blessing is not finally controlled by Laban’s contracts.

灵修应用 Devotional

被误待时仍可诚实作工

职场或家庭中长期被占便宜，会让人只剩怨气。桥梁原则是：神看见隐藏的不公，也看见我们如何回应不公。诚实不是纵容压迫；它让我们在寻求合宜边界与出路时，仍不把自己的灵魂交给苦毒。

Being used in work or family can leave us full of resentment. The bridge principle is that God sees hidden injustice and also sees how we respond. Integrity does not excuse oppression; it keeps our souls from bitterness while we seek proper boundaries and a faithful way forward.

31:14-16

拉结和利亚回答雅各，说在父家已经没有她们的分了；父亲把她们当外人，又吞了她们的价银。她们承认神从父亲夺回的财物属于她们和孩子，于是劝雅各照神所吩咐的去行。

Rachel and Leah answered that they no longer had a share in their father’s house; he had treated them as outsiders and consumed their bride price. They recognized that what God took from their father belonged to them and their children, and urged Jacob to do what God had said.

经文含义 Meaning

妻子们确认离开的必要

拉结和利亚的回应揭开拉班家内部的不义：父亲把女儿当交易对象，没有按应有方式保障她们。她们不是被动附属品，而是在叙事中共同确认神的命令。离开拉班家，是雅各家庭从旧控制中被分别出来的重要一步。

Rachel and Leah expose injustice inside Laban’s house: their father has treated them transactionally and failed to protect their share. They are not passive accessories; they jointly confirm God’s command. Leaving Laban becomes a decisive step in separating Jacob’s household from old control.

灵修应用 Devotional

顺服也需要家庭中的诚实沟通

重大决定若只靠单方推动，容易留下裂痕。雅各把处境、亏损和神的话向妻子说明，使家庭在痛苦中形成共同方向。今天的应用是：属灵决定不应包装成强迫别人配合，而要在真相、祷告和彼此尊重中一起承担。

Major decisions pushed by one person often leave wounds. Jacob explains the situation, losses, and God’s word to his wives, and the family forms a shared direction amid pain. Spiritual decisions should not be disguised coercion; they should be carried through truth, prayer, and mutual respect.

秘密出走与拉班追赶 (31:17-35)

Secret Departure and Laban’s Pursuit

31:17-21

雅各起身，使儿女和妻子骑上骆驼，带着牲畜和所得财物，要往迦南地父亲以撒那里去。拉班剪羊毛去了，拉结偷了父亲家中的神像；雅各瞒着拉班逃走，过了大河，面向基列山行去。

Jacob rose, set his children and wives on camels, and took his livestock and possessions to go to his father Isaac in Canaan. While Laban was away shearing sheep, Rachel stole her father’s household gods. Jacob left secretly, crossed the river, and headed toward the hill country of Gilead.

经文含义 Meaning

离开旧家，也暴露旧依靠

叙事同时呈现顺服与混杂：雅各回应神的命令离开，拉结却偷走家神。家神可能与家庭保护、继承权或身份安全有关；无论具体功能如何，它显示拉结仍抓住旧家的象征。经文诚实说明，神带领人出发时，人心常还带着未处理的依靠。

The narrative presents obedience and mixture together: Jacob responds to God’s command, while Rachel steals the household gods. These objects may relate to protection, inheritance, or household identity; in any case, Rachel clings to an old symbol. The text honestly shows that people may begin following God while still carrying unresolved dependencies.

灵修应用 Devotional

出发之前检查手里抓着什么

人可以离开一个环境，却把旧恐惧、旧控制、旧安全感带到新路上。桥梁原则是：神呼召我们前行，也邀请我们松开暗中倚靠的替代品。今天可安静省察：我口里说信靠神，手里却还藏着什么让我觉得“没有它就不安全”？

We may leave an environment yet carry old fear, control, and false security into the new path. The bridge principle is that God calls us forward and invites us to release hidden substitutes. Ask quietly: while I say I trust God, what am I still holding because I feel unsafe without it?

31:22-29

第三日有人告诉拉班雅各逃走了，拉班带人追赶七日，在基列山追上他。夜间神在梦中对拉班说：“你要小心，不可与雅各说好说歹。”拉班质问雅各为何暗中逃走，又承认自己有能力害他，只是雅各父亲的神昨夜警告了他。

On the third day Laban was told that Jacob had fled. He pursued for seven days and overtook him in Gilead. God came to Laban in a dream and warned him not to speak either good or bad to Jacob. Laban questioned Jacob’s secret flight and admitted that he had power to harm him, but the God of Jacob’s father had warned him.

经文含义 Meaning

神在追赶者之前说话

拉班的追赶充满威胁，但神先一步介入。 “不可说好说歹”不是禁止普通谈话，而是限制拉班用威胁、诱骗或强制扭转雅各的路。经文让读者看见，神不只在雅各心里引导，也在危险者身上设限。

Laban’s pursuit carries threat, but God intervenes first. The warning not to speak “good or bad” limits Laban from using threat, persuasion, or force to reverse Jacob’s path. God guides Jacob inwardly and also restrains the dangerous pursuer outwardly.

灵修应用 Devotional

不是每个冲突都由我们掌控

当我们面对强势的人，常以为安全完全取决于自己解释得够不够好。桥梁原则是：我们要诚实负责地说话，同时承认神能在别人心里和局势中设边界。信靠神不等于消极，而是在行动时不把最终保护归给自己的口才。

When facing forceful people, we may think safety depends entirely on how well we explain ourselves. The bridge principle is that we speak honestly and responsibly while trusting God to set boundaries in people and circumstances. Trust is not passivity; it means we do not credit our eloquence with ultimate protection.

31:30-35

拉班说雅各思家可以理解，却质问为何偷他的神像。雅各不知道拉结所做的事，就说在谁那里搜出来，谁就不得存活。拉班搜查雅各、利亚和两个使女的帐棚，又进拉结帐棚；拉结把神像藏在骆驼驮篓里，坐在上面，借口身体不便没有起来，拉班没有搜出来。

Laban said he could understand Jacob’s homesickness but asked why his gods had been stolen. Jacob, unaware of Rachel’s act, declared that whoever had them should not live. Laban searched the tents of Jacob, Leah, and the servants, then Rachel’s tent. Rachel had hidden the idols in a camel saddle and sat on them, excusing herself because of her condition, so Laban did not find them.

经文含义 Meaning

隐藏的罪带来危险话语

雅各因不知道真相而发出严厉誓言，显明隐藏行为会把无辜关系推入危险。拉结的欺瞒短期保护了自己，却没有解决偶像问题。经文没有赞美她的策略，而是让读者感到一种不安：应许之家已经上路，但旧家的阴影仍在帐棚里。

Jacob’s ignorant oath shows how hidden actions endanger innocent relationships. Rachel’s deception protects her in the short term but does not resolve the idol problem. The text does not celebrate her tactic; it leaves unease: the promise household is moving, yet the shadow of the old house remains in the tent.

灵修应用 Devotional

隐藏之物会牵连别人

秘密的财务、成瘾、谎言或偶像式依靠，往往不只伤害自己，也让身边人承担风险。桥梁原则是：真正的自由不只是没有被发现，而是在神面前愿意被光照。今天可以选择向神承认一个隐藏角落，并寻找可信、合宜的帮助。

Secret finances, addictions, lies, or idol-like dependencies rarely harm only ourselves; they put others at risk. The bridge principle is that real freedom is not merely avoiding exposure but becoming willing to be seen by God. Today, name one hidden place before Him and seek trustworthy, appropriate help.

雅各申诉与双方立约 (31:36-55)

Jacob’s Protest and the Covenant Boundary

31:36-42

雅各发怒责问拉班：他犯了什么罪，以致拉班追赶搜查？二十年来，他牧养羊群，承担损失，白日受尽干热，黑夜受尽寒霜，睡眠不得安宁。若不是他父亲的神、亚伯拉罕的神、以撒所敬畏的神与他同在，拉班必定叫他空手而去；但神看见他的苦情和劳碌，昨夜责备了拉班。

Jacob became angry and asked what offense justified Laban’s pursuit and search. For twenty years he had shepherded the flocks, borne losses, endured heat by day and frost by night, and lost sleep. If the God of his father, the God of Abraham and the Fear of Isaac, had not been with him, Laban would have sent him away empty-handed; but God saw his affliction and labor and rebuked Laban.

经文含义 Meaning

苦情与劳碌在神面前不消失

雅各的申诉是本章情感高点。他回顾二十年的劳苦，指出自己承担牧人的责任，也指出拉班原本会让他空手离开。这里的重点不是雅各自我称义，而是神看见“苦情与劳碌”。经文安慰受亏负者：真实的损失不会因强者否认而从神面前消失。

Jacob’s protest is the emotional peak of the chapter. He recalls twenty years of labor, his responsibility as shepherd, and Laban’s willingness to send him away empty-handed. The point is not Jacob’s self-righteousness but that God saw his affliction and labor. Real losses do not disappear before God because the powerful deny them.

灵修应用 Devotional

让神听见你的真实申诉

信仰不是要求人永远压下受伤感。桥梁原则是：申诉可以成为诚实，而不是报复；它把事实带到神面前，也让边界被说清楚。面对长期不公平时，可以用不夸大、不羞辱的方式陈明事实，并把最终审断交给神。

Faith does not require wounded people to suppress pain forever. The bridge principle is that protest can be honest rather than vengeful; it brings facts before God and clarifies boundaries. In long-term unfairness, state the truth without exaggeration or humiliation, and entrust final judgment to God.

31:43-50

拉班声称女儿、孩子和羊群都属他，却承认不能对他们做什么。于是他提议立约。雅各取石头立作柱子，众人堆成石堆，在那里吃饭。拉班称它为伊迦尔撒哈杜他，雅各称它为迦累得；又称米斯巴，说：“我们彼此离别以后，愿耶和华在你我中间鉴察。”

Laban claimed the daughters, children, and flocks as his, yet admitted he could do nothing to them. He proposed a covenant. Jacob set up a stone pillar, and the others made a heap and ate there. Laban named it in Aramaic and Jacob in Hebrew, both marking it as a witness heap; it was also called Mizpah, “May the LORD watch between you and me when we are out of one another’s sight.”

经文含义 Meaning

见证石堆划清界限

拉班仍以占有语言说话，但现实中他被神限制，只能转向立约。石柱和石堆成为公共记号，既记念约定，也划出不可越过的界限。米斯巴常被浪漫化，但在此处主要是冲突后的监督祷告：当彼此看不见时，神仍鉴察。

Laban still speaks in possessive language, but God’s restraint forces him toward covenant. The pillar and heap become public signs that remember the agreement and mark a boundary. Mizpah is often romanticized, but here it is chiefly a prayer of accountability after conflict: when people cannot see each other, God still watches.

灵修应用 Devotional

有些关系需要清楚边界

和解不总是恢复亲密，有时是停止伤害、承认界限、交给神监察。桥梁原则是：爱人并不等于让对方继续越界；敬畏神也包括把关系放在诚实约束中。现代家庭和职场关系中，清楚边界可以成为和平的开始。

Reconciliation does not always restore closeness; sometimes it stops harm, acknowledges limits, and entrusts the relationship to God’s watch. The bridge principle is that loving others does not mean allowing continued violation; reverence for God includes honest boundaries. Clear limits can be the beginning of peace in family and work relationships.

31:51-55

拉班指着石堆和柱子说，双方都不可越过这记号去害对方。雅各指着以撒所敬畏的神起誓，在山上献祭，请众弟兄吃饭。他们住宿一夜，清早拉班亲吻外孙和女儿，为他们祝福，然后离开回本地去了。

Laban pointed to the heap and pillar and said that neither side should cross the marker to harm the other. Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac, offered sacrifice on the mountain, and shared a meal. They spent the night there; early in the morning Laban kissed his grandchildren and daughters, blessed them, and returned home.

经文含义 Meaning

分离中的敬畏与祝福

结尾不是完美团圆，而是受约束的分离。雅各献祭，显示他把这段路交在神面前；拉班祝福后回去，冲突暂时止息。应许之家的下一步不是留在拉班控制下，而是在神的保守中继续前行，面对将来与以扫相见的更大考验。

The ending is not a perfect reunion but a bounded separation. Jacob’s sacrifice places the journey before God, and Laban’s blessing closes the conflict for the moment. The promise household does not remain under Laban’s control; it moves forward under God’s care toward the coming test of meeting Esau.

灵修应用 Devotional

和平有时是带着祝福分别

并非每段关系都能回到从前，但可以在神面前停止报复、守住界限、带着祝福分别。桥梁原则是：成熟的信心既不否认伤害，也不让伤害决定未来。今天可为一个复杂关系祷告：求神赐我诚实、边界和不被苦毒牵引的自由。

Not every relationship can return to what it was, but people can stop revenge, keep boundaries, and part with blessing before God. The bridge principle is that mature faith neither denies harm nor lets harm define the future. Pray for honesty, boundaries, and freedom from bitterness in a complicated relationship.

Methodology · 方法论

双轨阅读：先听经文，再回应神

Two-track reading: hear the text first, then respond before God.

正统解经原则

Responsible exegesis

本章先按上下文阅读：雅各离开不是任性逃跑，而是在拉班长期不公、神明确命令、妻子共同确认之下发生。家神、追赶、石堆和誓言都要放在古代家庭、财产和边界语境中理解。 Read the chapter in context: Jacob’s departure is not impulsive escape but follows Laban’s sustained injustice, God’s clear command, and his wives’ confirmation. The idols, pursuit, heap, and oath belong within ancient household, property, and boundary settings.

灵修应用路径

Devotional application

应用时不把雅各每个行为都当成榜样，而是抓住经文显明的桥梁原则：神看见不公，神呼召人离开旧控制，神限制伤害，也呼召人面对隐藏偶像并建立合宜边界。 Application does not treat every action of Jacob as exemplary. It follows the bridge principles revealed by the text: God sees injustice, calls people out of old control, restrains harm, and summons them to face hidden idols and establish proper boundaries.

实际落地指南

Practical guide for reading Genesis 31 today

1. 先分辨神的话与环境压力。 环境变化可能提醒人留意方向，但最终顺服要建立在神清楚的真理和责任上。 First distinguish God’s word from circumstantial pressure; circumstances may alert us, but obedience rests on God’s truth and responsibility.

2. 诚实处理隐藏依靠。 拉结的家神提醒读者，离开旧环境之后仍要让神光照旧安全感。 Deal honestly with hidden dependencies; Rachel’s idols remind us that old securities can travel with us.

3. 用敬畏神建立边界。 基列石堆说明，和平有时不是模糊问题，而是在神面前说清界限、停止伤害。 Build boundaries in reverence for God; the Gilead heap shows that peace sometimes requires clear limits before God.

"If the God of my father, the God of Abraham and the Fear of Isaac, had not been on my side, surely now you would have sent me away empty-handed."
