创世记 39章 Genesis Chapter 39

本章核心主题 Core Theme

在异地与冤屈中仍有耶和华同在

The Lord’s Presence in Exile and Injustice

创世记39章把约瑟从波提乏家带到监牢。经文反复强调“耶和华与约瑟同在”，说明神的同在不等于环境立刻顺利，而是在被卖、被诱惑、被诬告、被囚禁中仍塑造忠心、智慧与可信任的品格。约瑟的成功不是技巧上的自我实现，而是神赐福、人在看不见结果时仍敬畏神的见证。

Genesis 39 follows Joseph from Potiphar’s house into prison. The repeated phrase “the Lord was with Joseph” shows that God’s presence does not always mean immediate comfort; it forms faithfulness, wisdom, and trustworthiness amid enslavement, temptation, false accusation, and confinement. Joseph’s success is not self-made achievement but God’s favor expressed through reverent integrity when outcomes remain hidden.

耶和华同在

The Lord Was With Joseph

YHWH hayah et-Yosef

本章四次突出神与约瑟同在。同在不是免除苦难，而是在受限处境中仍赐下能力、秩序和可信任的见证。 Presence does not remove hardship; it gives capacity, order, and witness inside hardship.

手中亨通

Prosperity in His Hand

tsalach / beyado

“亨通”指神使约瑟所管理的事有效推进。重点不是约瑟掌控命运，而是神借他的手祝福别人。 Prospering means God advances the work entrusted to Joseph and blesses others through his hands.

不作这大恶

How Could I Do This Evil?

ra‘ah gedolah

约瑟拒绝试探，首先因他看见罪是得罪神，也破坏主人对他的信任。敬畏神使私下的选择有清楚边界。 Joseph resists because sin is against God and betrays entrusted responsibility.

被囚中的恩惠

Favor in Prison

chen / chesed

监牢不是神同在的终点，而成为下一段预备的地方。神的恩惠在低处继续运行，使约瑟学习托付与治理。 Prison becomes a place of preparation where God’s favor continues to work in a low place.

创世记39章逐段双轨研读

A bilingual two-track study through Genesis Chapter 39

本章把约瑟的生命放在权力、欲望和冤屈的压力下阅读。研读轨道关注经文结构和重复词，灵修轨道则把“神同在中的忠心”连接到今日职场、关系边界和低谷中的信靠。 This chapter reads Joseph under pressure from power, desire, and injustice. The study track follows structure and repeated phrases, while the devotional track connects faithful life in God’s presence to work, relational boundaries, and trust in low places.

39:1-6a

约瑟被带到埃及，法老的臣仆护卫长波提乏买了他。耶和华与约瑟同在，使他所办的事顺利；波提乏看见这一点，就派他管理家中一切，因约瑟的缘故，耶和华赐福给埃及人的家。

Joseph is taken down to Egypt and bought by Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh. The Lord is with Joseph and makes his work prosper. Potiphar sees this and entrusts his household to him; because of Joseph, the Lord blesses the Egyptian’s house.

经文含义 Meaning

被卖之处成为服事之处

“下到埃及”延续约瑟被兄弟出卖的伤痛，但叙事立刻把焦点放在耶和华的同在上。约瑟没有职位和自由，却在被托付的小范围内显出可靠。经文把祝福扩展到波提乏家，说明神给亚伯拉罕后裔“使万族得福”的应许，已经在异邦家庭中微微发光。

The descent to Egypt continues Joseph’s wound, yet the narrative centers on the Lord’s presence. Joseph lacks status and freedom, but he proves trustworthy in what is entrusted to him. The blessing spreading to Potiphar’s house quietly echoes the promise that Abraham’s family would bless the nations.

灵修应用 Devotional

在不理想的位置上忠心

现代人常把价值感绑在平台、头衔和公平待遇上。约瑟提醒我们：当处境并非自己选择，仍可以在手边责任上敬畏神。职场、家庭或服事中的忠心，可能先祝福旁人，再慢慢显明神的带领。

We often tie worth to platform, title, or fair treatment. Joseph reminds us that when circumstances are not chosen, we can still honor God through the responsibility in front of us. Faithfulness at work, home, or ministry may bless others before God’s direction becomes visible.

39:6b-10

约瑟俊美，波提乏的妻子天天引诱他。约瑟拒绝，说主人把一切交在他手中，唯独留下她；他不能作这大恶得罪神。虽然她天天催逼，约瑟仍不听从，也不与她同在。

Joseph is handsome, and Potiphar’s wife repeatedly urges him to lie with her. Joseph refuses, saying his master has entrusted everything to him except her; he cannot commit this great evil and sin against God. Though she speaks day after day, he will not listen or stay with her.

经文含义 Meaning

敬畏神划出私下边界

约瑟的回答有两层：他尊重主人托付的关系界限，也知道罪最终是得罪神。经文强调试探不是一次性事件，而是“天天”的压力。约瑟不只口头拒绝，也避免停留在危险空间，显示智慧的逃避也是敬虔的一部分。

Joseph’s answer has two layers: he honors the boundaries of entrusted relationship and recognizes sin as ultimately against God. The temptation is daily, not momentary. Joseph refuses verbally and avoids remaining in the dangerous space; wise avoidance belongs to faithful reverence.

灵修应用 Devotional

把边界提前设好

试探常以私密、重复、似乎无人知道的方式靠近。桥梁原则是：敬畏神的人不等到情绪最强时才决定原则，而是在平时设立清楚边界。面对暧昧关系、权力诱惑或隐秘消费，逃离不是软弱，而是保护信任。

Temptation often approaches privately, repeatedly, and under the illusion of secrecy. The bridge principle is this: those who fear God set boundaries before desire is strongest. In ambiguous relationships, power temptations, or hidden habits, fleeing is not weakness; it protects trust.

39:11-18

有一天，屋里没有别人，妇人抓住约瑟的衣裳。约瑟把衣裳留在她手里，逃到外面。她就叫家里人来，扭曲事实，又向丈夫重复控告，说约瑟进来戏弄她。

One day, when no one else is in the house, the woman grabs Joseph’s garment. Joseph leaves it in her hand and flees outside. She calls the household, distorts the event, and later repeats the accusation to her husband, claiming Joseph came in to mock her.

经文含义 Meaning

衣裳再次成为扭曲见证

创世记37章中，约瑟的彩衣被兄弟用来欺骗父亲；这里，另一件衣裳又被用来制造假见证。经文并不浪漫化义人的道路：正确选择可能带来即时损失。约瑟逃离罪，却无法控制别人如何解释他的行动。

In Genesis 37 Joseph’s robe was used by his brothers to deceive their father; here another garment is used to manufacture false testimony. The text does not romanticize the righteous path: the right choice may bring immediate loss. Joseph can flee sin, but he cannot control how others interpret him.

灵修应用 Devotional

清白不总能立刻被看见

许多人害怕坚持原则，因为担心被误解、被报复、被孤立。约瑟的经历给受冤屈者真实安慰：神看见事实，即便人暂时只看见被操控的证据。我们仍要选择可在神面前站立的道路，并把无法辩明的部分交托给他。

Many fear integrity because it can lead to misunderstanding, retaliation, or isolation. Joseph’s experience comforts the falsely accused: God sees the truth even when people only see manipulated evidence. We choose the path that can stand before God and entrust what we cannot clarify to him.

39:19-23

波提乏听见妻子的控告就发怒，把约瑟下在王的囚犯被囚之处。然而耶和华与约瑟同在，向他施恩，使司狱看重他；司狱把囚牢事务交给约瑟，因耶和华与他同在，使他所做的尽都顺利。

Potiphar hears his wife’s accusation and becomes angry, placing Joseph in the prison where the king’s prisoners are kept. Yet the Lord is with Joseph and shows him steadfast favor. The prison keeper trusts him with the prison’s affairs, and the Lord makes what he does prosper.

经文含义 Meaning

监牢没有隔绝神的同在

本章结尾几乎复现开头的结构：耶和华同在、掌权者看见、事务被托付、所做的亨通。地点从家宅降到监牢，但神同在的主题没有下降。叙事也预备下一章，因为“王的囚犯”这个地点将成为约瑟进入法老宫廷的隐蔽通道。

The ending mirrors the opening: the Lord is with Joseph, an authority notices, responsibilities are entrusted, and his work prospers. The location descends from household to prison, but the theme of God’s presence does not descend. The “king’s prisoners” also prepares the path toward Pharaoh’s court.

灵修应用 Devotional

低谷也可能是预备场

我们常把挫折视为故事中断，但神能把低谷变成品格、忍耐和下一步呼召的预备场。今天若你在被限制的位置，不必把生命暂停；可以问：在这个小范围里，我如何继续诚实、服事、学习，并相信神没有离开？

We often treat setbacks as interruptions, but God can turn low places into preparation for character, patience, and future calling. If you are in a confined season, your life is not on pause. Ask how you can continue with honesty, service, learning, and trust that God has not left.

方法论 Methodology

从经文原意到今日忠心

From textual meaning to faithful practice today

正统解经路径

The exegetical path

先观察叙事重复：耶和华同在、所做亨通、权柄者托付、约瑟被降卑。再把本章放在创世记37-41章中，看见衣裳、下去、认不出的真相与神隐藏护理的连续性。这样的读法避免把约瑟简化成成功学榜样，而是看见神在苦难中保存应许、塑造器皿。

Begin with repeated narrative markers: the Lord’s presence, prospering work, entrusted authority, and Joseph’s humiliation. Then read Genesis 39 within Genesis 37-41, noting garments, descent, hidden truth, and God’s providence. This keeps Joseph from becoming a mere success model and highlights God’s promise-preserving work.

灵修应用路径

The devotional path

应用时采用桥梁原则：神的同在使人在限制中仍可忠心；敬畏神使私下选择有边界；被误解时仍把真相交托给神；低谷可以成为预备场。应用不是把古代情节直接复制到现代，而是把经文中稳定的真理带入今日工作伦理、性伦理、人际信任和受冤屈时的盼望。

Application uses bridge principles: God’s presence enables faithfulness under limits; reverence sets boundaries in private choices; the falsely accused entrust truth to God; low places can become preparation. We do not copy ancient scenes directly; we carry stable truths into work ethics, sexual integrity, relational trust, and hope under injustice.

落地指南 Practical Guide

阅读本章时，可用三个问题祷告反省：我现在被托付了什么？我需要提前设立哪一条边界？若我正在低谷中，怎样在小范围内继续忠心？ Pray through three questions: What has been entrusted to me now? What boundary should I establish before pressure rises? If I am in a low place, how can I remain faithful in the small sphere before me?
