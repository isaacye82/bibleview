出埃及记 35章 Exodus Chapter 35

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

安息中的顺服，甘心中的建造

Obedience in Sabbath, Building through Willing Hearts

出埃及记第 35 章是会幕实际建造的开端。摩西先重申安息日，使百姓明白神的居所不能靠焦躁和自我表现来建成；随后他召聚全会众，邀请凡心里受感、甘心乐意的人献上材料，并让心中有智慧的人参与制作。这里的焦点不是财富本身，而是被救赎群体如何把所有资源、技能和时间交回给神。

Exodus 35 begins the actual preparation for the tabernacle. Moses first restates Sabbath, showing that God's dwelling is not built by restless self-display. Then the congregation is invited to bring materials willingly and to serve with Spirit-given skill. The chapter highlights a redeemed community returning resources, craft, and time to the Lord.

Rising gifts, moving threads, and a drawn sanctuary picture willing hearts becoming a dwelling place.

研读视图 / Study View

出埃及记 35 章经文双轨研读

本章把神的圣洁节奏、百姓的甘心奉献和巧匠的智慧事奉连在一起。每个单元都先观察上下文经文含义，再进入今日生活中的灵修应用。

This chapter joins God's holy rhythm, the people's willing offerings, and skilled service. Each unit moves from contextual meaning into devotional application for today.

Sabbath and the Boundary of Sacred Work

安息日与圣工的边界 (35:1-3)

35:1-3

摩西招聚以色列全会众，吩咐他们六日作工，第七日要守为圣安息日，归耶和华为圣；连住处中生火的日常劳作也要停止。

Moses gathers Israel and commands six days for work, but the seventh day is a holy Sabbath to the LORD; even ordinary household fire is to cease.

经文含义 Meaning

圣工不能越过神的节奏

本章即将开始会幕工程，却先谈安息日。经文含义很清楚：建造敬拜空间不能破坏敬拜本身。安息日提醒百姓，他们不是用生产效率换取神的同在，而是在神已经救赎他们的身份中顺服祂。

The tabernacle work begins only after Sabbath is restated. The meaning is clear: sacred construction must not violate worshipful obedience. Israel does not earn God's presence through productivity; they respond within redeemed identity.

灵修应用 Devotional

停止也是敬拜

今天的服事、工作和家庭责任都可能披上“必要”的外衣。本段邀请我们把停止当作信心操练：承认世界不是靠我不停运转，事奉也不是靠焦虑维持。愿我们在忙碌前先把节奏交给神。

Work, ministry, and family duties can feel unquestionably necessary. This passage invites rest as faith: the world is not sustained by our constant motion, and service is not preserved by anxiety. We begin by yielding our rhythm to God.

Materials Offered by Willing Hearts

甘心献上的材料 (35:4-9)

35:4-9

摩西传达耶和华的吩咐：凡乐意的人，可以把金、银、铜，蓝色、紫色、朱红色线，细麻、山羊毛、皮、皂荚木、油、香料和宝石带来，作为献给耶和华的礼物。

Moses announces the LORD's command: whoever is willing may bring gold, silver, bronze, colored yarns, linen, skins, acacia wood, oil, spices, and stones as an offering to the LORD.

经文含义 Meaning

奉献从“心被感动”开始

经文没有用强制语气向每个人索取同样数量，而是强调“乐意”。这些材料曾来自埃及，却现在被重新定向，用于神的居所。救赎改变资源的目的：从自我保存转向敬拜与群体建造。

The command is not coercive or uniform; it emphasizes willingness. Materials once connected to Egypt are now redirected for God's dwelling. Redemption changes the purpose of possessions from self-preservation to worship and communal building.

灵修应用 Devotional

把手中的资源重新定向

现代人的金银可能是收入、时间、专业能力、人脉或家中的空间。灵修应用不是制造愧疚，而是诚实询问：哪些资源可以从只服务自己，转向祝福神的家和身边的人？

Our “gold and silver” may be income, time, professional skill, relationships, or home space. Application is not guilt-driven; it asks honestly which resources can be redirected from self-only use toward God's household and neighborly good.

Skilled Workers Join the Making

有智慧的人参与制作 (35:10-19)

35:10-19

凡心里有智慧的人都要来，照耶和华所吩咐的制作帐幕、罩棚、柜、桌子、灯台、香坛、燔祭坛、院子、橛子、圣衣和各样器具。

All who are skillful are called to make what the LORD commanded: the tabernacle, its coverings, ark, table, lampstand, altars, courtyard, pegs, holy garments, and utensils.

经文含义 Meaning

智慧表现为照神吩咐制作

这里的“智慧”不是抽象聪明，而是能把神的指示落实成可用、合宜、荣耀神的工艺。经文把属灵顺服和实际技能连在一起，说明神看重细节，也使用人的手艺完成祂吩咐的事。

Wisdom here is not abstract intelligence but the ability to embody God's instructions in fitting, usable, God-honoring craftsmanship. The text joins spiritual obedience with practical skill and shows that God values detail.

灵修应用 Devotional

专业能力也能成为敬拜

职场能力、设计审美、工程逻辑、行政组织、教学表达，都可以在神面前被分别为圣。关键不是把所有人变成同一种服事者，而是让各人的恩赐在顺服中被放对位置。

Professional competence, design sense, engineering logic, administration, and teaching can be set apart before God. The goal is not making everyone the same servant, but placing each gift under obedient purpose.

The Congregation Comes Willingly

会众甘心而来 (35:20-24)

35:20-24

全会众离开摩西以后，凡心里受感、甘心乐意的，都把礼物带来，用于会幕和圣衣；男女都来献上首饰、金器、线、皮、银、铜和皂荚木。

After leaving Moses, all whose hearts were stirred and willing brought offerings for the tent and holy garments: men and women brought jewelry, gold, yarns, skins, silver, bronze, and acacia wood.

经文含义 Meaning

顺服从听见进入行动

百姓不是只听命令，而是离开聚会后实际行动。经文反复说“心里受感”“甘心乐意”，让读者看见真正的回应既有内在感动，也有外在交付。会幕由整个群体共同预备。

The people do not merely hear; after the assembly they act. Repeated phrases about stirred and willing hearts show that genuine response includes inward movement and outward surrender. The sanctuary is prepared by the whole community.

灵修应用 Devotional

感动需要落地

我们常在聚会、读经或危机中被触动，却容易停在情绪层面。本段提醒我们把感动转化为可执行的一步：一次探访、一笔奉献、一段时间、一次道歉，或一个长期承担。

We may feel moved in worship, Scripture, or crisis, yet remain at the emotional level. This passage calls us to one concrete step: a visit, a gift, a block of time, an apology, or a sustained responsibility.

Women's Craft and Leaders' Gifts

妇女的手艺与领袖的礼物 (35:25-29)

35:25-29

心中有智慧的妇女亲手纺线，把蓝色、紫色、朱红色线和细麻献上；有能力的人纺山羊毛。众首领也带来红玛瑙、宝石、香料和油。以色列人都甘心献给耶和华。

Skillful women spun yarns and linen with their hands, and capable women spun goat hair. The leaders brought stones, spices, and oil. The Israelites gave freely to the LORD.

经文含义 Meaning

经文看见常被忽略的服事

本段特别记下妇女的纺织与首领的奉献，显示会幕不是少数人的舞台。细线、毛料、宝石、香料都不可互相替代。神的工作需要可见的领导，也需要安静细致、长期重复的手艺。

The text specifically records women's spinning and leaders' gifts, showing that the tabernacle is not a stage for a few. Thread, hair, stones, and spices are not interchangeable. God's work needs visible leadership and quiet, careful, repeated craft.

灵修应用 Devotional

隐藏的劳作被神记念

许多现代服事不在台前：整理资料、照顾孩子、预备饭食、修补关系、长期陪伴。出埃及记 35 章告诉我们，神记下这些细小而真实的献上；没有一种甘心的服事在祂面前是透明的。

Much modern service is not public: organizing, childcare, meals, repairing relationships, patient presence. Exodus 35 assures us that God records small and real offerings; no willing service is invisible before Him.

Bezalel and Oholiab Are Called

比撒列与亚何利亚伯蒙召 (35:30-35)

35:30-35

摩西宣告耶和华已经提名召比撒列，使他被神的灵充满，有智慧、聪明、知识，能设计各样巧工；又赐亚何利亚伯同工，并使他们能教导别人。

Moses declares that the LORD has called Bezalel by name and filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, understanding, and knowledge for artistic work; Oholiab is given as a coworker, and they are enabled to teach others.

经文含义 Meaning

蒙召、充满、同工、教导

本章结尾把工艺能力归给神的灵。比撒列不是单独英雄；他被提名、被充满，也与亚何利亚伯同工，并被赋予教导他人的能力。神的恩赐不仅完成作品，也培养更多服事者。

The chapter ends by attributing craftsmanship to the Spirit of God. Bezalel is not a lone hero: he is called, filled, paired with Oholiab, and enabled to teach. God's gifts complete work and multiply workers.

灵修应用 Devotional

把恩赐传给别人

成熟的服事不只是“我能做”，也包括“我能和谁一起做，并帮助谁学会”。在职场、家庭和教会中，神给人的能力若只建立个人名声，就偏离了方向；若能栽培他人，就更接近本章的图画。

Mature service is not only “I can do this,” but “Who can I work with, and whom can I help learn?” In work, family, and church, gifts drift when they build only personal reputation; they mature when they equip others.

方法论 / Methodology

从经文原意到今日顺服

阅读出埃及记 35 章，需要先把它放在金牛犊事件、重新立约和会幕指示之后。神没有放弃祂的同在计划，却把建造重新放在安息、甘心和智慧之下。

Exodus 35 should be read after the golden calf, covenant renewal, and tabernacle instructions. God has not abandoned His plan to dwell among His people, but the work is now framed by Sabbath, willingness, and wisdom.

正统解经路径

先观察重复词：“安息日”“甘心乐意”“心里有智慧”“照耶和华所吩咐”。再留意叙事位置：会幕工程不是独立建筑项目，而是约民在赦免之后学习让神居中的回应。

The exegetical path observes repeated phrases such as Sabbath, willing heart, skillful heart, and as the LORD commanded. It also reads the chapter within covenant renewal after forgiveness.

灵修应用路径

应用时不要只问“我该奉献多少”，还要问“我的节奏是否顺服神、我的资源是否重新定向、我的技能是否造就群体、我的恩赐是否能栽培别人”。这使古代会幕叙事连接到现代工作、家庭与教会生活。

The devotional path asks not only how much to give, but whether rhythm, resources, skill, and mentoring are yielded to God in work, family, and church life.

落地指南 / Practical Guide

本章的桥梁原则是：被救赎的人以安息守住身份，以甘心献上资源，以智慧服事群体。今天可以具体落实为固定安息界限、预算中的奉献安排、专业能力的公益或教会服事，以及主动训练下一位同工。

The bridge principle is this: redeemed people guard identity through Sabbath, offer resources willingly, and serve the community with wisdom. Concrete practice may include rest boundaries, planned generosity, skilled service, and training another coworker.
