出埃及记 28章 Exodus Chapter 28

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

圣衣：尊荣、分别与代求

Holy Garments: Honor, Consecration, and Intercession

出埃及记第 28 章从会幕转向祭司。神吩咐摩西为亚伦和他的儿子分别出来，制作“为荣耀、为华美”的圣衣。以弗得、胸牌、十二块宝石、乌陵和土明、蓝袍、金牌和细麻衣，不只是宗教服饰，而是让祭司在神面前代表百姓、承载名字、承担圣洁责任的可见记号。本章提醒读者：亲近神不是随意进入，而是借着神所设立的中保、圣洁和恩典进入。

Exodus 28 moves from the tabernacle structure to the priesthood. Aaron and his sons are set apart, and garments are made “for glory and for beauty.” The ephod, breastpiece, twelve stones, Urim and Thummim, blue robe, gold plate, and linen garments visibly mark representation, remembrance, discernment, and holiness before God.

This study follows the movement of the priestly garments and office: calling, representation, remembrance, discernment, holiness, and embodied obedience.

Priests Set Apart; Garments for Glory and Beauty

祭司被分别，圣衣为荣耀华美 (28:1-5)

28:1-5

神吩咐摩西从以色列人中召亚伦和他的儿子，使他们作祭司事奉祂；又要召有智慧的人，照神赐的智慧为亚伦制作圣衣，使他分别为圣。所要作的包括胸牌、以弗得、外袍、杂色内袍、冠冕和腰带，材料用金线、蓝色、紫色、朱红色线和细麻。

The LORD tells Moses to bring Aaron and his sons near to serve as priests. Skillful workers, endowed with wisdom, are to make holy garments for Aaron’s consecration: a breastpiece, ephod, robe, tunic, turban, and sash, using gold, blue, purple, scarlet yarns, and fine linen.

经文含义 Meaning

圣职来自呼召，不来自自我任命

经文先说“使他们作祭司事奉我”，再谈衣服与工艺。顺序很重要：祭司身份不是凭血气争取，也不是凭审美包装出来，而是神主动分别。圣衣“为荣耀、为华美”，不是给人制造虚荣，而是让人看见神的圣洁、秩序和尊贵。工匠的智慧同样来自神，说明属灵服事需要恩赐、技能和顺服共同参与。

The order matters: God first calls Aaron and his sons to priestly service, then commands the making of garments. The office is not self-appointed or cosmetically manufactured; it is God’s consecrating gift. “Glory and beauty” point beyond vanity to God’s holiness, order, and dignity. Even the artisans’ skill is God-given, showing that faithful service joins gifting, craft, and obedience.

灵修应用 Devotional

把角色当作托付，而不是舞台

今天无论在家庭、教会或职场，身份与职责都容易被包装成个人形象。本段提醒我们：真正的服事先问“神托付我什么”，再问“我如何做得合宜”。当你承担带领、照顾或专业责任时，可以操练把能力献给神，而不是用能力证明自己。

In family, church, and work, roles can easily become personal branding. This passage asks first, “What has God entrusted to me?” and only then, “How can I carry it well?” When you lead, care, or work professionally, offer competence back to God instead of using it to prove yourself.

The Ephod and Shoulder Stones: The People Carried Before God

以弗得与肩石：百姓被带到神面前 (28:6-14)

28:6-14

以弗得要用金线、蓝色、紫色、朱红色线和捻的细麻，以巧匠手工作成，并有相连的肩带和精致织成的带子。两块红玛瑙刻上以色列众子的名字，按出生次序各六名，镶在金槽中，安在以弗得两条肩带上，作为记念。

The ephod is made of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarns and fine linen, with joined shoulder pieces and a woven band. Two onyx stones are engraved with the names of Israel’s sons, six names on each stone, set in gold and placed on the shoulders of the ephod as stones of remembrance.

经文含义 Meaning

肩上担负的是名字，不只是制度

肩膀在圣经意象中常与承担有关。祭司进入圣所时，不是只代表一个宗教系统，而是把十二支派的名字带在肩上。名字被刻在石头上，表示这记念不是临时情绪，而是稳固、公开、按约的记念。神设立祭司制度，是让百姓知道自己被代表、被记念，也被带进神面前。

Shoulders often symbolize bearing a load. When the priest enters the holy place, he does not represent an abstract system; he carries the names of the twelve tribes. Engraving the names on stones portrays a stable, public, covenantal remembrance rather than a passing feeling. God’s priestly order assures the people that they are represented, remembered, and brought before Him.

灵修应用 Devotional

用名字记念人

现代生活容易把人缩成任务、工号、群体标签或麻烦来源。肩石提醒我们，属神的服事会重新看见人的名字。为家人、同事、学生、病人或组员代祷时，不妨具体说出名字；这会改变我们对责任的感受，也使爱从泛泛的善意变成真实承担。

Modern life can reduce people to tasks, employee numbers, labels, or problems. The shoulder stones train us to recover names. When praying for family members, coworkers, students, patients, or group members, name them specifically; this turns vague goodwill into concrete responsibility and love.

The Breastpiece of Judgment: Remembrance at the Heart and Guidance

决断胸牌：心上记念与寻求引导 (28:15-30)

28:15-21

决断胸牌要以巧匠手工作成，与以弗得同样用金线、蓝色、紫色、朱红色线和细麻。胸牌四方、两层，镶嵌四行宝石，共十二块，每块刻一个以色列支派的名字，像刻图章一样。

The breastpiece of judgment is made with the same fine materials as the ephod. It is square and doubled, set with four rows of precious stones, twelve stones in all. Each stone is engraved like a seal with the name of one tribe of Israel.

经文含义 Meaning

神百姓各有名字，也同在一块胸牌上

十二块宝石不同，却同属一个胸牌；各支派有分别的名字，却共同被带到神面前。宝石的贵重并不来自支派本身无瑕，而是来自神给他们的约中位置。决断胸牌说明，神的判断与引导不是冷冰冰的行政动作，而是与祂对百姓的记念相连。

The twelve stones differ, yet belong to one breastpiece; each tribe has its own name, yet all are carried together before God. Their preciousness comes not from tribal flawlessness but from their covenant place given by God. The breastpiece of judgment shows that divine discernment is not cold administration; it is joined to God’s remembrance of His people.

灵修应用 Devotional

在差异中守住共同归属

群体生活常因差异产生比较、偏见或疏离。本段给我们的桥梁原则是：神记念每一个名字，也把不同的人放在同一个约的群体里。今天面对教会、家庭或团队差异时，可以先承认每个人在神面前的重量，再处理意见分歧。

Community life often turns difference into comparison, bias, or distance. This passage offers a bridge: God remembers each name and places different people within one covenant people. In church, family, or teams, first honor each person’s weight before God, then address disagreements.

28:22-30

胸牌要用金链、金环和蓝细带牢牢系在以弗得上，不可离开。亚伦进圣所时，要把以色列众子的名字带在胸前，在耶和华面前常作记念；又要把乌陵和土明放在决断胸牌里，使亚伦在耶和华面前常将以色列人的决断牌带在心上。

Gold chains, rings, and blue cords fasten the breastpiece securely to the ephod so it does not come loose. When Aaron enters the holy place, he bears the names of Israel’s sons over his heart as a continual remembrance before the LORD. The Urim and Thummim are placed in the breastpiece, so Aaron bears Israel’s judgment over his heart before the LORD continually.

经文含义 Meaning

判断要贴近心，也要在神面前

本段反复强调“不可离开”“带在心上”“常作记念”。乌陵和土明与寻求神旨意有关，但经文的重点不是满足好奇，而是让关乎百姓命运的判断留在神面前。真正合宜的属灵判断不是冲动决定，也不是单靠技术分析，而是在神的同在中，把人的名字放在心上。

The repeated ideas are security, the heart, and continual remembrance. The Urim and Thummim relate to seeking God’s direction, but the point is not curiosity; decisions affecting the people are held before the LORD. Faithful discernment is neither impulsive choice nor mere technique; it brings people’s names before God with heart-level responsibility.

灵修应用 Devotional

作决定前先把人放回心上

在管理、家庭决定或冲突处理里，我们容易只问效率、输赢或风险。本段邀请我们在决定前停一下：这个选择会怎样影响具体的人？我是否愿意在神面前承担这个判断？这样的操练能让权柄减少冷硬，让智慧多一点敬畏和怜悯。

In management, family decisions, or conflict, we often ask only about efficiency, winning, or risk. This passage invites a pause: How will this choice affect real people? Am I willing to carry this judgment before God? Such practice softens authority and adds reverence and mercy to wisdom.

Robe, Bells, and Gold Plate: Drawing Near in Holiness

外袍、铃铛与金牌：在圣洁中进前 (28:31-39)

28:31-35

以弗得的外袍全是蓝色，领口要织好，使它不致破裂。袍子底边要有蓝色、紫色、朱红色线作的石榴，并在四围石榴中间挂金铃铛。亚伦供职进出圣所时，铃声要被听见。

The robe of the ephod is entirely blue, with a reinforced opening so it will not tear. Around its hem are pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, with gold bells between them. When Aaron ministers and enters or leaves the holy place, the sound is heard.

经文含义 Meaning

生命果实与可听见的服事

蓝袍与圣所相关，石榴有丰盛生命的意象，铃铛则使祭司的进出被听见。经文强调服事神不是隐秘的自我发挥，而是在神规定的圣洁秩序中进行。铃声不是表演声量，而是提醒：亲近圣洁之神需要按神启示的方式，不可轻慢。

The blue robe is associated with the holy place, pomegranates suggest fruitful life, and bells make the priest’s movement audible. Ministry is not private improvisation; it occurs within God’s revealed holy order. The sound is not performance volume, but a reminder that drawing near to the holy God must be done as He commands.

灵修应用 Devotional

让生命的果实和声音一致

我们在公众场合可能有“声音”，在私下却缺少果实；也可能默默结果，却害怕承担可见责任。石榴与铃铛放在一起，提醒我们让生命品格和外在见证彼此配合。职场与家庭中的信仰见证，不只是说正确的话，也包括结出可被辨认的耐心、诚实和节制。

We may have a public “sound” without private fruit, or bear quiet fruit while avoiding visible responsibility. Pomegranates and bells together call for integrity between character and witness. In work and family, faithful testimony is not only right words but recognizable patience, honesty, and self-control.

28:36-39

要用精金作一面牌，刻上“归耶和华为圣”，用蓝细带系在冠冕前面，在亚伦额上。亚伦要担当圣物上的罪孽，使以色列人分别为圣的一切礼物可蒙悦纳；又要作细麻内袍、冠冕和绣花腰带。

A plate of pure gold is engraved with “Holy to the LORD” and fastened with a blue cord to the front of the turban on Aaron’s forehead. Aaron bears the guilt connected with the holy gifts, so Israel’s offerings may be accepted. A linen tunic, turban, and embroidered sash are also made.

经文含义 Meaning

圣洁不是口号，而是被承担的现实

金牌上的“归耶和华为圣”放在额上，显示祭司公开承认自己属于神。更深的是，亚伦要担当圣物上的罪孽，使礼物蒙悦纳。这指出人即使献上分别为圣的礼物，也需要神所设立的中保来处理不洁与亏欠。圣洁既是身份宣告，也是严肃的责任。

The gold plate on the forehead publicly declares belonging to the LORD. More deeply, Aaron bears the guilt associated with the holy gifts so that Israel’s offerings may be accepted. Even consecrated gifts require God-appointed mediation for impurity and failure. Holiness is both declared identity and weighty responsibility.

灵修应用 Devotional

把“属于神”带进思想与选择

额上的金牌提醒我们，圣洁首先影响思想方向：我如何判断价值？如何使用时间、金钱和身体？今天的应用不是把“圣洁”变成自责压力，而是在基督里学习把自己重新交托给神，让日常选择逐渐配得上“属于主”的身份。

The plate on the forehead reminds us that holiness shapes thought: How do I judge value? How do I use time, money, and body? The application is not anxious self-condemnation, but renewed surrender in Christ, so daily choices increasingly fit the identity of belonging to the Lord.

Garments for Aaron’s Sons and a Lasting Statute

亚伦儿子的衣服与永远定例 (28:40-43)

28:40-43

又要为亚伦的儿子作内袍、腰带和裹头巾，为荣耀、为华美。摩西要给亚伦和他儿子穿上，膏他们，将他们分别为圣，使他们作祭司事奉神。他们进会幕或近坛供职时要穿细麻裤子遮身，免得担罪而死；这要作亚伦和他后裔永远的定例。

Tunics, sashes, and caps are made for Aaron’s sons, for glory and beauty. Moses is to clothe, anoint, ordain, and consecrate them so they may serve as priests. When they enter the tent or approach the altar, they must wear linen undergarments to cover themselves, lest they bear guilt and die. This is a lasting statute for Aaron and his descendants.

经文含义 Meaning

圣洁延伸到看不见的地方

最后一段把焦点扩展到亚伦的儿子，并提到内在、贴身、通常不被人看见的衣物。经文不是羞辱身体，而是说明在圣洁神面前，连隐藏处也要被合宜遮盖。祭司服事不是只在外袍、胸牌、金牌等显眼部分显出庄重，也在无人称赞的细节中显出敬畏。

The final paragraph extends the focus to Aaron’s sons and includes garments usually unseen by others. The text does not shame the body; it teaches that before the holy God even hidden places must be rightly covered. Priestly service shows reverence not only in visible robe, breastpiece, and plate, but also in unnoticed details.

灵修应用 Devotional

无人看见处也属于神

许多属灵失败不是从公开场合开始，而是在无人看见的习惯、欲望、金钱处理或网络使用中慢慢形成。本段给我们温柔但严肃的提醒：神关心隐藏处，不是为了羞辱我们，而是为了使我们的整个人被祂医治和分别。可以从一个具体隐秘习惯开始，邀请神的光进入。

Many failures do not begin publicly; they form in unseen habits, desires, money practices, or online behavior. This passage gives a gentle but serious reminder: God cares about hidden places not to shame us, but to heal and consecrate the whole person. Begin with one concrete hidden habit and invite God’s light there.

方法论总结 / Methodology

从圣衣到生命：两条路径如何配合

正统解经路径先尊重出埃及记的上下文：第 28 章连接会幕、祭司和西奈之约，重点不是服饰审美本身，而是神如何安排有罪百姓在圣洁神面前被代表、被记念并蒙悦纳。读者应先观察材料、位置、重复词和职分功能，再归纳经文含义。

The exegetical path begins with context: Exodus 28 connects tabernacle, priesthood, and Sinai covenant. Its focus is not fashion but God’s ordered way for sinful people to be represented, remembered, and accepted before His holiness.

解经原则 / Exegetical Principles

先问“这件衣物在祭司进入圣所时承担什么功能？”再问“它怎样服务于约、圣洁和代求？”避免把每个颜色或宝石任意灵意化，也避免只停留在古代工艺说明。

Ask first what each garment does within priestly service, then how it serves covenant, holiness, and intercession. Avoid arbitrary symbolism while also refusing to reduce the text to ancient craftsmanship alone.

灵修应用 / Devotional Practice

应用时用“桥梁原则”：祭司把名字带在肩上和心上，今天我们也学习在责任中记念具体的人；金牌宣告归神为圣，今天我们也把思想、判断和隐藏生活交给神更新。

Use bridge principles: as the priest carried names on shoulder and heart, we remember real people in our responsibilities; as the gold plate declared holiness to the LORD, we surrender thoughts, judgments, and hidden life for God’s renewal.
