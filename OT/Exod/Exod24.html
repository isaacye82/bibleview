出埃及记 24章 Exodus Chapter 24

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

约在山上被确认：血、话语与同在

The Covenant Confirmed on the Mountain: Blood, Words, and Presence

出埃及记第 24 章是西奈之约的庄严确认。百姓听见耶和华的一切话和典章，回应“我们都要遵行”；摩西将约书念给百姓听，并用血洒在坛和百姓身上，表明这约既是神主动设立，也呼召百姓以顺服回应。随后，以色列的代表上山，在神面前吃喝，却因神的怜悯得以存活。最后，摩西进入云中四十昼夜，预备领受会幕和敬拜的启示。本章把敬拜、盟约、顺服、代赎和神圣同在连在一起。

Exodus 24 solemnly confirms the Sinai covenant. Israel hears the words and ordinances of the LORD and answers, “We will do them.” Moses reads the book of the covenant and sprinkles blood on the altar and the people, showing that the covenant is established by God and calls for obedient response. Israel’s representatives then ascend, eat and drink before God, and live by His mercy. Finally, Moses enters the cloud for forty days and nights to receive further instruction for worship. The chapter joins worship, covenant, obedience, atonement, and holy presence.

研读视图 / Study View

出埃及记 24 章经文双轨研读

本章按约的确认、血的仪式、圣洁相交与云中启示分段阅读。每个单元都从上下文经文含义走向今日的顺服、敬拜和等候。

The People Promise to Obey the Covenant Words

百姓承诺遵行约言 (24:1-3)

24:1-3

耶和华对摩西说：“你和亚伦、拿答、亚比户，并以色列长老中的七十人，都要上到耶和华这里来，远远地下拜。惟独你可以亲近耶和华；他们却不可亲近，百姓也不可和你一同上来。”摩西下山，将耶和华的命令、典章都述说与百姓听。众百姓齐声说：“耶和华所吩咐的，我们都必遵行。”

The LORD called Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders to come up and worship from afar. Moses alone would draw near, while the others and the people would not. Moses told the people all the LORD’s words and ordinances, and they answered with one voice that they would do all the LORD had spoken.

经文含义 Meaning

听见之后才承诺

本段清楚区分神圣临近的层次：百姓在山下，领袖远远敬拜，摩西独自亲近。这样的安排不是贬低百姓，而是保护他们在圣洁神面前不轻率越界。百姓的回应发生在摩西“述说”神的话之后，说明约的顺服不是自我设计的宗教热情，而是对神启示的回应。

The passage distinguishes levels of holy approach: the people remain below, the leaders worship from afar, and Moses alone draws near. Israel’s pledge follows the hearing of God’s words, so covenant obedience is a response to revelation rather than self-made religious enthusiasm.

灵修应用 Devotional

先聆听，再许诺

今天我们也容易先承诺、后了解，或用情绪替代顺服。本段提醒我们，工作、家庭和服事中的忠心，要从认真聆听神的话开始。真正成熟的“我愿意”，不是冲动地证明自己，而是在明白神心意后，让日常决定被祂的话调整。

We often promise before listening or let emotion replace obedience. This scene calls us to begin with attentive hearing. A mature “yes” to God is not impulsive self-proving but a daily willingness to let His word reshape decisions at work, home, and in service.

The Book, the Altar, and the Blood

约书、祭坛与血 (24:4-8)

24:4-6

摩西将耶和华的命令都写上。清早起来，在山下筑一座坛，按以色列十二支派立十二根柱子，又打发少年人献燔祭和平安祭。摩西将血一半盛在盆中，一半洒在坛上。

Moses wrote down all the words of the LORD. Early in the morning he built an altar at the foot of the mountain and set up twelve pillars for the twelve tribes of Israel. Young men offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and Moses put half the blood in basins and half on the altar.

经文含义 Meaning

写下的话与代表全体的柱子

摩西把话写下，表示神的约不是随意变动的口头印象，而有可听、可读、可传承的内容。坛代表神面前的敬拜，十二柱代表以色列全体。祭牲的血先洒在坛上，强调这约首先归向神，百姓能站在约中，是因祭物生命被献上。

Writing the words gives the covenant stable and transmissible content. The altar represents worship before God, and the twelve pillars represent all Israel. Blood placed at the altar first shows that the covenant is oriented toward God and that access is mediated through sacrificed life.

灵修应用 Devotional

让信仰有可记念的形状

属灵生活若只停留在感觉，很快会被压力冲散。摩西写下约言、筑坛、立柱，提醒我们也需要具体的记念：固定读经、诚实记录神的带领、在家庭中设立敬拜节奏。这些不是形式主义，而是帮助软弱的人持续回应神。

If faith remains only a feeling, pressure quickly scatters it. Written words, altar, and pillars invite us to concrete remembrance: regular Scripture, honest records of God’s guidance, and household rhythms of worship. These practices help weak people keep responding to God.

24:7-8

摩西将约书念给百姓听。他们说：“耶和华所吩咐的，我们都必遵行。”摩西将血洒在百姓身上，说：“你看，这是立约的血，是耶和华按这一切话与你们立约的凭据。”

Moses read the book of the covenant to the people, and they again said that they would do and obey all the LORD had spoken. Moses sprinkled the blood on the people and said that this was the blood of the covenant the LORD had made with them according to all these words.

经文含义 Meaning

血把话语与百姓连接

百姓第二次回应，并且“遵行”与“听从”连在一起。约血洒在百姓身上，不是装饰性的仪式，而是严肃地说明他们属于这约，也受这约约束。血同时指向恩典和责任：罪人得以进入关系，但不能把神的话当作可有可无。

The people answer a second time, joining doing and obeying. The sprinkled blood is not decorative ritual; it marks the people as belonging to and bound by the covenant. Blood speaks both grace and responsibility: sinners are brought into relationship, yet God’s words cannot be treated as optional.

灵修应用 Devotional

恩典不是降低认真程度

现代人常把“被接纳”理解成“不需要改变”。本节给出更深的桥梁原则：神先以恩典把人带进关系，然后在关系中呼召人认真顺服。我们面对冲突、欲望和选择时，不是靠恐惧维持体面，而是因被神买赎而学习忠诚。

Modern people often turn acceptance into “no change required.” This verse gives a deeper bridge: God graciously brings His people into relationship and then calls them to serious obedience within that relationship. In conflict, desire, and choices, we pursue faithfulness because we belong to Him.

The Representatives Eat and Drink Before God

代表们在神面前吃喝 (24:9-11)

24:9-11

摩西、亚伦、拿答、亚比户，并以色列长老中的七十人，都上了山。他们看见以色列的神；祂脚下仿佛有平铺的蓝宝石，如同天色明净。祂的手不加害在以色列的尊者身上；他们观看神，他们又吃又喝。

Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders went up and saw the God of Israel. Under His feet was something like a pavement of sapphire, clear as the heavens. God did not lay His hand on the nobles of Israel; they beheld God and ate and drank.

经文含义 Meaning

圣洁中的相交

经文谨慎描述他们所见的是神荣耀的表现，特别提到祂脚下如蓝宝石的铺路，避免把神缩小为可掌控的形象。重点在于“祂的手不加害”：人在圣洁神面前仍得存活，是恩典。吃喝则表明约已确认，敬拜进入相交和筵席的喜乐。

The text carefully describes the manifestation of God’s glory, especially the sapphire-like pavement under His feet, without reducing God to a controllable image. The emphasis is that God did not strike them. Life before the Holy One is grace, and eating and drinking signal covenant fellowship and joy.

灵修应用 Devotional

敬畏不排斥亲近

很多人把神想成只能远观的威严，或只把祂当作满足需要的朋友。本段把两者放在一起：神圣洁到必须敬畏，神又施恩使人能在祂面前相交。今天的祷告也应如此，既不轻慢，也不退缩，带着谦卑和信任亲近祂。

Some imagine God only as distant majesty; others reduce Him to a helper for needs. This passage holds both together: God is holy and must be revered, yet He graciously grants fellowship. Prayer today should neither be casual nor fearful withdrawal, but humble and trusting approach.

Moses Enters the Cloud to Receive Revelation

摩西进入云中领受启示 (24:12-18)

24:12-14

耶和华对摩西说：“你上山到我这里来，住在这里，我要将石版并我所写的律法和诫命赐给你，使你可以教训百姓。”摩西和他的帮手约书亚起来，上了神的山。摩西对长老说：“你们在这里等着，等到我们再回来；亚伦、户珥与你们同在，凡有争讼的，都可以就近他们去。”

The LORD told Moses to come up and stay on the mountain, where He would give tablets of stone with the law and commandment written for Israel’s instruction. Moses and Joshua rose to go up the mountain of God, while the elders were told to wait, with Aaron and Hur available for disputes.

经文含义 Meaning

启示为了教导群体

神赐石版不是为了制造神秘感，而是“使你可以教训百姓”。摩西上山，同时安排亚伦和户珥处理争讼，显示属灵带领既需要等候神，也需要对群体秩序负责。约书亚同上山，暗示下一代领袖在神启示的环境中被塑造。

The tablets are given not to create mystique but so Moses can teach the people. Moses ascends while arranging Aaron and Hur to handle disputes, showing that spiritual leadership combines waiting on God with responsibility for communal order. Joshua’s presence hints at formation for future leadership.

灵修应用 Devotional

等候神，也安排责任

在职场、教会或家庭里，属灵追求不应成为逃避责任的理由。摩西进入神面前之前，先交代群体事务。今天我们也需要学习：把时间分别给神，同时把该沟通、该交接、该处理的事清楚安排，使等候不是混乱，而是信靠中的秩序。

Spiritual pursuit should not become an excuse to escape responsibility at work, church, or home. Before entering God’s presence, Moses delegates community matters. We likewise set apart time for God while communicating, handing off, and arranging duties clearly, so waiting becomes ordered trust.

24:15-18

摩西上山，有云彩把山遮盖。耶和华的荣耀停于西奈山；云彩遮盖山六天，第七天祂从云中召摩西。耶和华的荣耀在山顶上，在以色列人眼前，形状如烈火。摩西进入云中上山，在山上四十昼夜。

When Moses went up, the cloud covered the mountain. The glory of the LORD settled on Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days. On the seventh day God called Moses from the cloud. To Israel the glory of the LORD appeared like a consuming fire on the mountain top, and Moses entered the cloud for forty days and nights.

经文含义 Meaning

神临近，也隐藏

云彩和烈火同时出现，表达神的临近与不可轻慢。云遮盖，说明神不是人可以随意解析的对象；火显明，说明祂的荣耀真实可见、令人敬畏。摩西等候六天，第七天被召，提醒我们启示按神的时间赐下，不受人的急躁驱动。

Cloud and fire together express God’s nearness and His unapproachable holiness. The cloud hides what humans cannot control; the fire reveals real and awe-inspiring glory. Moses waits six days and is called on the seventh, showing that revelation comes by God’s timing, not human urgency.

灵修应用 Devotional

在云中学习信靠

许多信心旅程不像明亮地图，更像进入云中：看不清全部，却听见神呼召下一步。出埃及记 24 章鼓励我们，在不确定的季节不急着制造答案，而是守住神已经说过的话，接受必要的等待，并相信祂的同在比清楚掌控更可靠。

Many journeys of faith feel less like a bright map and more like entering a cloud: the whole path is unclear, yet God calls the next step. Exodus 24 encourages us not to manufacture answers in uncertainty, but to hold what God has spoken, accept needed waiting, and trust His presence more than control.

方法论 / Methodology

从正统解经到灵修应用

阅读出埃及记 24 章时，第一步不是直接把西奈山套用到个人经历，而是先看上下文：第 19-23 章已经颁布约言和典章，第 24 章则是正式确认这约。因此，“约书、血、吃喝、云彩”都应放在神主动立约与百姓回应顺服的脉络中理解。

When reading Exodus 24, the first step is not to immediately turn Sinai into private experience, but to follow the context. Chapters 19-23 give covenant words and ordinances; chapter 24 confirms the covenant. The book, blood, meal, and cloud must be understood within God’s covenant initiative and Israel’s obedient response.

正统解经原则

Principles for faithful interpretation

  • 上下文：把本章视为西奈之约的确认，而不是孤立的神秘登山故事。 Read the chapter as covenant confirmation, not an isolated mystical ascent.
  • 原意：血、坛、柱子和筵席都服务于“神立约、百姓归属”的主题。 Blood, altar, pillars, and meal serve the theme of covenant belonging.
  • 渐进启示：约血最终让读者看见神如何以代赎开启亲近祂的道路。 Covenant blood points forward to the larger biblical pattern of atoning access to God.

灵修应用路径

A bridge into daily devotion

  • 听见再回应：在重大承诺前，先让神的话校正动机和边界。 Hear before responding; let God’s word shape motives and boundaries.
  • 恩典中顺服：顺服不是换取接纳，而是被约中恩典接纳后的忠诚。 Obedience does not purchase acceptance; it expresses covenant loyalty after grace.
  • 云中等候：当未来不清楚时，持守已领受的话，并在责任中安静等候。 When the future is unclear, hold what God has spoken and wait faithfully within responsibility.
