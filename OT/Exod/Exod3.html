出埃及记 3章 Exodus Chapter 3

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

荆棘火焰中的呼召与圣名

The Call and the Holy Name in the Burning Bush

出埃及记第 3 章是拯救故事公开转折的时刻。摩西在旷野牧羊，神在不被烧毁的荆棘中显现，宣告祂已经看见、听见、知道百姓的痛苦，并要下来拯救他们。摩西被差遣回埃及，但使命的根基不是他的能力，而是“我是自有永有”的神亲自同在。

Exodus 3 is the public turning point in the story of deliverance. While Moses shepherds in the wilderness, God appears in a bush that burns without being consumed, declares that He has seen, heard, and known His people’s suffering, and sends Moses back to Egypt. The mission rests not on Moses’ adequacy but on the presence of the God who says, “I AM WHO I AM.”

神学概念卡片组合

Key concepts for reading Exodus 3 faithfully

圣地与敬畏

Holy Ground and Reverence

qodesh / holy

脱鞋不是形式主义，而是承认神的临在改变了普通之地的意义。敬畏使摩西先听见神，再理解使命。Removing sandals is not empty ritual; it recognizes that God’s presence redefines ordinary ground. Reverence prepares Moses to hear before he acts.

看见、听见、知道

Seen, Heard, Known

raah / shama / yada

神对苦难的回应不是冷淡观察，而是盟约性的介入。祂看见压迫、听见哀声、知道痛苦，并主动下来拯救。God’s response to suffering is not detached observation but covenant involvement. He sees oppression, hears the cry, knows the pain, and comes down to deliver.

我是自有永有

I AM WHO I AM

ehyeh asher ehyeh

圣名强调神不是被人定义的工具，而是自有、信实、主动同行的主。摩西的把握不在自我解释，而在神自己的启示。The divine name stresses that God is not a tool defined by people; He is self-existing, faithful, and actively present. Moses’ confidence rests in God’s self-revelation.

差遣与同在

Sending and Presence

shalach / imak

神没有先消除摩西所有不安，却给出最根本的应许：“我必与你同在。”使命的中心是差遣者的可靠。God does not remove every fear before sending Moses; He gives the deepest promise: “I will be with you.” The heart of mission is the reliability of the Sender.

旷野中的异象 (3:1-6)

The Vision in the Wilderness

3:1-3

摩西牧养他岳父米甸祭司叶忒罗的羊群；一日领羊群往野外去，到了神的山，就是何烈山。耶和华的使者从荆棘里火焰中向摩西显现。摩西观看，不料，荆棘被火烧着，却没有烧毁。摩西说：“我要过去看这大异象，这荆棘为何没有烧坏呢？”

Now Moses was keeping the flock of his father-in-law, Jethro, the priest of Midian, and he led his flock to the west side of the wilderness and came to Horeb, the mountain of God. And the angel of the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush. He looked, and behold, the bush was burning, yet it was not consumed. And Moses said, “I will turn aside to see this great sight, why the bush is not burned.”

经文含义 Meaning

普通日常中的神圣打断

摩西不是在王宫或战场被呼召，而是在牧羊的日常中被神打断。荆棘本是旷野中卑微、易燃的植物，却在火中不被吞灭，预示神的临在既圣洁有力，又能保守软弱器皿不被毁坏。经文把“看见”反复放在前面，显示呼召常从留意神主动显明开始。

Moses is called not in a palace or battlefield but in the ordinary work of shepherding. The lowly bush burns without being consumed, suggesting that God’s holy presence can dwell with weak vessels without destroying them. The repeated language of seeing shows that calling begins with attending to God’s initiative.

灵修应用 Devotional

在平凡职责里留心神

今天的职场、家庭照护、重复任务也可能成为神提醒我们的地方。灵修应用不是逃离日常去寻找刺激，而是在普通责任中保持敏锐：当压力没有吞灭你、当微小机会邀请你转向神时，停下来聆听。神常在我们以为只是“又一天”的地方开启新的顺服。

Work, caregiving, and repeated duties can become places where God gets our attention. Devotional response is not escaping ordinary life for excitement; it is staying attentive within ordinary responsibilities. When pressure does not consume you and a small moment invites you toward God, pause and listen.

3:4-6

耶和华神见他过去要看，就从荆棘里呼叫说：“摩西！摩西！”他说：“我在这里。”神说：“不要近前来。当把你脚上的鞋脱下来，因为你所站之地是圣地。”又说：“我是你父亲的神，是亚伯拉罕的神，以撒的神，雅各的神。”摩西蒙上脸，因为怕看神。

When the LORD saw that he turned aside to see, God called to him out of the bush, “Moses, Moses!” And he said, “Here I am.” Then he said, “Do not come near; take your sandals off your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground.” And he said, “I am the God of your father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.” And Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to look at God.

经文含义 Meaning

圣洁临在与盟约身份

神两次呼叫摩西的名字，显明这不是抽象宗教经验，而是位格性的呼召。“圣地”不是因地理本身神奇，而是因神临在。神接着以列祖之神自我介绍，把摩西个人经历接入亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各的盟约历史。敬畏与亲近同时存在：神呼名，却也设立圣洁边界。

God calls Moses by name, showing this is personal calling rather than vague religious experience. The ground is holy because God is present. By naming Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, God connects Moses’ moment to covenant history. Reverence and nearness stand together: God calls by name and sets holy boundaries.

灵修应用 Devotional

亲近神，也尊重神

现代人容易把神只当作安慰资源，或只当作遥远权威。本段邀请我们同时学习亲近与敬畏：祷告时可以诚实说“我在这里”，也要愿意“脱鞋”，放下控制、急躁和自我中心。面对重要决定时，先确认自己是否站在敬畏中，而不是只急着得到答案。

We often treat God either as a comfort resource or as a distant authority. This passage teaches both nearness and reverence: in prayer we may honestly say, “Here I am,” while also removing the sandals of control, hurry, and self-centeredness. Before major decisions, ask whether you are standing in reverence, not merely seeking an answer.

神看见苦情并应许拯救 (3:7-12)

God Sees the Affliction and Promises Deliverance

3:7-9

耶和华说：“我的百姓在埃及所受的困苦，我实在看见了；他们因受督工的辖制所发的哀声，我也听见了。我原知道他们的痛苦。我下来是要救他们脱离埃及人的手，领他们出了那地，到美好、宽阔、流奶与蜜之地……现在以色列人的哀声达到我耳中，我也看见埃及人怎样欺压他们。”

Then the LORD said, “I have surely seen the affliction of my people who are in Egypt and have heard their cry because of their taskmasters. I know their sufferings, and I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up out of that land to a good and broad land, a land flowing with milk and honey... And now, behold, the cry of the people of Israel has come to me, and I have also seen the oppression with which the Egyptians oppress them.”

经文含义 Meaning

神的怜悯进入历史

“看见、听见、知道、下来”形成强烈的行动链。神不是只同情受苦者，而是按祂对列祖的应许进入具体历史。流奶与蜜之地不是抽象安慰，而是从奴役到居所、从压迫到敬拜的完整拯救图景。经文也保留现实性：埃及的欺压被清楚命名，没有被属灵化抹去。

The verbs “seen, heard, known, come down” form a chain of divine action. God does not merely sympathize with the suffering; He enters history according to His promises. The land flowing with milk and honey is not vague comfort but a full picture of deliverance from bondage into settled worship. The oppression is named honestly rather than spiritualized away.

灵修应用 Devotional

把痛苦带到看见人的神面前

当人际伤害、职场不公或长期压力使你觉得无人理解时，这段经文提供桥梁原则：神的沉默不等于无知，神的看见会按祂的时间转化为行动。祷告可以具体命名痛苦，也可以求神给你下一步顺服，而不是只求立刻脱离所有困难。

When relational wounds, workplace injustice, or long pressure make you feel unseen, this passage gives a bridge principle: God’s silence is not ignorance, and His seeing becomes action in His time. Prayer may name pain specifically while also asking for the next step of obedience, not only immediate escape from every hardship.

3:10-12

“故此，我要打发你去见法老，使你可以将我的百姓以色列人从埃及领出来。”摩西对神说：“我是什么人，竟能去见法老，将以色列人从埃及领出来呢？”神说：“我必与你同在。你将百姓从埃及领出来之后，你们必在这山上事奉神；这就是我打发你去的证据。”

“Come, I will send you to Pharaoh that you may bring my people, the children of Israel, out of Egypt.” But Moses said to God, “Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh and bring the children of Israel out of Egypt?” He said, “But I will be with you, and this shall be the sign for you, that I have sent you: when you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall serve God on this mountain.”

经文含义 Meaning

使命的答案是同在

摩西问“我是什么人”，神没有列出摩西的资历，而回答“我必与你同在”。这转移了使命的中心：人的不足真实存在，但不是最终决定因素。证据指向未来在山上的敬拜，说明拯救目的不只是逃离奴役，更是归向神、事奉神。

Moses asks, “Who am I?” God does not list Moses’ qualifications but answers, “I will be with you.” The center of mission shifts from human adequacy to divine presence. The future sign of worship on the mountain shows that deliverance is not merely escape from slavery but return to God’s service.

灵修应用 Devotional

从“我是谁”转向“神与我同在”

面对超出能力的任务，我们常陷在自我评估里：我够不够强、会不会失败、别人怎么看。经文不是鼓励盲目自信，而是把信心建立在神的同在上。实际应用是：承认限制，寻求智慧和同工，然后凭神已经清楚托付的责任迈出下一步。

When tasks exceed our capacity, we often spiral into self-evaluation: Am I strong enough? What if I fail? What will others think? This text does not promote empty self-confidence; it grounds faith in God’s presence. Practically, acknowledge limits, seek wisdom and help, and take the next step in the responsibility God has made clear.

圣名启示与长老使命 (3:13-18)

The Divine Name and the Mission to the Elders

3:13-15

摩西对神说：“我到以色列人那里，对他们说：你们祖宗的神打发我到你们这里来。他们若问我说：他叫什么名字？我要对他们说什么呢？”神对摩西说：“我是自有永有的”；又说：“你要对以色列人这样说：那自有的打发我到你们这里来。”神又对摩西说：“你要对以色列人这样说：耶和华你们祖宗的神……打发我到你们这里来。耶和华是我的名，直到永远；这也是我的纪念，直到万代。”

Then Moses said to God, “If I come to the people of Israel and say to them, ‘The God of your fathers has sent me to you,’ and they ask me, ‘What is his name?’ what shall I say to them?” God said to Moses, “I AM WHO I AM.” And he said, “Say this to the people of Israel: ‘I AM has sent me to you.’” God also said to Moses, “Say this to the people of Israel: ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers... has sent me to you.’ This is my name forever, and thus I am to be remembered throughout all generations.”

经文含义 Meaning

圣名不是口号，而是信实同在

摩西的问题关乎权柄来源：谁差遣你？神启示“我是自有永有的”，不是为了满足好奇，而是宣告祂自由、永恒、信实并主动成就应许。这个名也与列祖之神连在一起，说明神现在的拯救行动与过去的盟约应许一致。

Moses’ question concerns authority: Who sent you? God’s revelation “I AM WHO I AM” is not given to satisfy curiosity but to declare His free, eternal, faithful presence. The name is joined to the God of the fathers, showing that present deliverance is consistent with past covenant promise.

灵修应用 Devotional

让神自己定义你的信靠

我们常希望神先符合自己的期待：立刻解决、清楚解释、按我们的时间行动。圣名提醒我们，神不是被我们管理的概念，而是主动启示自己的主。灵修上，信靠意味着让神的品格重新定义安全感：即使答案还未完全展开，祂仍是可信的“我是”。

We often want God to fit our expectations: solve immediately, explain clearly, act on our timetable. The divine name reminds us that God is not a concept we manage but the Lord who reveals Himself. Devotionally, trust means letting God’s character redefine security: even before every answer unfolds, He remains the trustworthy “I AM.”

3:16-18

“你去招聚以色列的长老，对他们说：耶和华你们祖宗的神……向我显现，说：我实在眷顾了你们，我也看见埃及人怎样待你们。我也说：要将你们从埃及的困苦中领出来……他们必听你的话。你和以色列的长老要去见埃及王，对他说：耶和华希伯来人的神遇见了我们，现在求你容我们往旷野去，走三天的路程，为要祭祀耶和华我们的神。”

“Go and gather the elders of Israel together and say to them, ‘The LORD, the God of your fathers... has appeared to me, saying, I have observed you and what has been done to you in Egypt, and I promise that I will bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt...’ And they will listen to your voice, and you and the elders of Israel shall go to the king of Egypt and say to him, ‘The LORD, the God of the Hebrews, has met with us; and now, please let us go a three days’ journey into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to the LORD our God.’”

经文含义 Meaning

拯救信息形成共同见证

神差遣摩西不是单独英雄式行动，而是让他招聚长老，共同向法老作见证。信息核心包括神的显现、眷顾、看见和应许。向法老提出的旷野敬拜请求，揭示真正的冲突不是劳动力安排，而是谁有权拥有并命令神的百姓。

God does not send Moses as a lone hero; he is to gather the elders and bear witness with them. The message centers on God’s appearing, care, seeing, and promise. The request to worship in the wilderness reveals that the real conflict is not labor management but who has rightful claim over God’s people.

灵修应用 Devotional

使命需要共同承担

许多属灵责任不应由一个人孤立承担。家庭带领、教会服事、职场中的公义见证，都需要可信的同伴、清楚的信息和谦卑的协作。今天可以问：我是否把神托付的事变成个人压力？我是否需要招聚成熟的人一起祷告、分辨并行动？

Many spiritual responsibilities should not be carried in isolation. Family leadership, church service, and a just witness at work require trustworthy companions, a clear message, and humble collaboration. Ask today: Have I turned God-given responsibility into private pressure? Do I need to gather mature people to pray, discern, and act together?

法老的抵挡与神的供应 (3:19-22)

Pharaoh’s Resistance and God’s Provision

3:19-20

“我知道虽用大能的手，埃及王也不容你们去。我必伸手在埃及中间施行我一切的奇事，攻击那地，然后他才容你们去。”

“But I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go unless compelled by a mighty hand. So I will stretch out my hand and strike Egypt with all the wonders that I will do in it; after that he will let you go.”

经文含义 Meaning

神预先说明抵挡，却不失控

神没有把使命描绘成顺利无阻。祂预先说明法老会抵挡，也宣告自己将伸手施行奇事。这样，困难不是神计划之外的意外，而是神彰显权柄的场域。经文帮助读者同时持守现实主义与信心：抵挡真实，神更真实。

God does not describe the mission as smooth or resistance-free. He foretells Pharaoh’s refusal and announces His own mighty action. Difficulty is not an accident outside God’s plan but the arena where His authority will be displayed. The text teaches both realism and faith: resistance is real, and God is more real.

灵修应用 Devotional

预期困难，不等于放弃顺服

当你知道一项正确的事会遇到阻力时，很容易把阻力当作不该做的理由。这里的桥梁原则是：神的呼召常包含对困难的清醒认识。你可以做风险评估、寻求保护、规划步骤，但不要因为有法老式的抵挡，就否认神已经清楚的带领。

When you know a right action will meet resistance, it is easy to treat resistance as a reason not to obey. The bridge principle is that God’s calling often includes sober awareness of difficulty. Assess risks, seek protection, and plan wisely, but do not deny God’s clear leading simply because Pharaoh-like resistance exists.

3:21-22

“我必叫你们在埃及人眼前蒙恩，你们去的时候就不至于空手而去。但各妇女必向她的邻舍，并居住在她家里的女人，要金器、银器和衣裳，好给你们的儿女穿戴。这样你们就把埃及人的财物夺去了。”

“And I will give this people favor in the sight of the Egyptians; and when you go, you shall not go empty, but each woman shall ask of her neighbor, and any woman who lives in her house, for silver and gold jewelry, and for clothing. You shall put them on your sons and on your daughters. So you shall plunder the Egyptians.”

经文含义 Meaning

不空手离开：公义的翻转

以色列人在埃及长期被剥削，神应许他们离开时不空手。这里不是鼓励贪婪，而是显示神会翻转压迫结构，使受奴役者带着尊严和供应离开。妇女被特别提及，也显示神的拯救通过家庭和日常关系展开，不只发生在政治舞台。

Israel had long been exploited in Egypt, and God promises they will not leave empty-handed. This does not encourage greed; it displays God’s reversal of oppression so enslaved people depart with dignity and provision. The mention of women also shows that God’s deliverance works through households and ordinary relationships, not only political stages.

灵修应用 Devotional

相信神关心具体供应

有时我们把属灵生活缩小成内心感受，忘记神也关心人的身体、家庭和实际需要。面对经济压力或转换阶段，这段经文鼓励我们正直地寻求供应，不羞于请求帮助，也不把神的看顾限制在抽象安慰里。神的拯救包括恢复尊严与预备前路。

Sometimes we reduce spiritual life to inward feelings and forget that God cares about bodies, households, and practical needs. In financial pressure or transition, this passage encourages honest pursuit of provision, humble asking for help, and refusing to limit God’s care to abstract comfort. His deliverance includes restored dignity and preparation for the road ahead.

方法论总结 / Methodology

先听见经文，再进入生活

Hear the text first, then carry its truth into life.

正统解经路径

The exegesis path

本章先观察叙事位置：出埃及记 1-2 章铺陈压迫与神记念，第三章进入神主动呼召。解读时注意重复动词“看见、听见、知道、下来”，以及神以列祖之神和“我是自有永有”启示自己。这样能避免把荆棘异象简化成个人成功经验，而是看见神圣洁临在、盟约信实和拯救行动。

This path begins with context: Exodus 1-2 presents oppression and God’s remembrance, while chapter 3 moves into God’s active call. Attention to repeated verbs and the divine name keeps the bush from becoming a generic success symbol and anchors it in God’s holy presence, covenant faithfulness, and saving action.

灵修应用路径

The devotional path

应用不是跳过原意直接寻找感动，而是从经文含义搭桥到今日处境：神看见受压者，所以我们可以诚实祷告；神以同在回应摩西的不足，所以我们可以在限制中顺服；神预告抵挡仍掌权，所以我们面对困难时既现实又有信心。

Application does not bypass meaning for quick inspiration. It builds a bridge from the text to today: because God sees the oppressed, we may pray honestly; because God answers Moses’ inadequacy with presence, we may obey amid limitations; because God foretells resistance while remaining sovereign, we face difficulty with realism and faith.

Practical guide for reading and response

Mark God’s verbs, Moses’ questions, and God’s answers before deciding the emphasis.

把古代情境中的原则连接到现代压力、责任、恐惧和共同承担。

Connect the ancient principles to modern pressure, responsibility, fear, and shared obedience.
