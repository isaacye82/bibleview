出埃及记 34章 Exodus Chapter 34

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

圣名宣告怜悯，破碎之后重立约

The Name Proclaims Mercy and the Covenant Is Renewed After Ruin

出埃及记第 34 章承接金牛犊之后的危机。神吩咐摩西重新凿两块石版，上西奈山；祂在云中降临，宣告自己有怜悯、有恩典、不轻易发怒，并且守约施慈爱，同时也不以有罪的为无罪。随后神重申以色列不可与迦南偶像体系结盟，要守节期、分别头生、安息日和奉献。章节最后，摩西因与神说话而面皮发光，显明真正的权柄来自与神相遇，而不是人的自我包装。

Exodus 34 follows the crisis of the golden calf. The Lord commands Moses to cut two new stone tablets and ascend Sinai. There God descends in the cloud and proclaims His name: merciful, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in covenant love, yet not clearing the guilty. The covenant is renewed with clear boundaries against idolatrous alliances, and Moses returns with a shining face, showing that true authority flows from meeting with God.

Glowing tablets, the name in the cloud, and rays from Moses' face picture renewed covenant and holy presence.

核心概念卡片组合

Key concepts for reading Exodus 34 faithfully

重刻石版

New Tablets

luḥot avanim

破碎的石版见证背约，重刻的石版见证神给悔改群体重新站立的恩典。The broken tablets witnessed covenant breach; the new tablets witness grace that lets a repentant people stand again.

怜悯恩典

Mercy and Grace

raḥum weḥannun

神首先宣告自己的性情：怜悯、恩典、忍耐和慈爱；这是更新之约的根基。God first proclaims His character: mercy, grace, patience, and loyal love; this grounds covenant renewal.

不可结盟

No Idolatrous Covenant

karat berit

神的赦免不是降低圣洁标准，而是呼召百姓不再与偶像体系交换忠诚。Forgiveness does not lower holiness; it calls the people away from alliances that trade away loyalty.

面皮发光

Shining Face

qaran or panav

摩西脸上的光不是自我表现，而是长久与神相会留下的见证。The light on Moses' face is not self-display; it is the witness left by sustained encounter with God.

研读视图 / Study View

出埃及记 34 章经文双轨研读

本章从重新预备石版开始，经过神宣告自己的圣名和性情，再进入更新 covenant 的生活边界，最后以摩西面皮发光作为与神相遇的外在见证。

This study moves from new tablets, to the proclamation of God's name and character, to the renewed covenant boundaries, and finally to Moses' shining face as the visible witness of meeting with God.

New Tablets and the Proclaimed Name

重刻法版与圣名宣告 (34:1-9)

34:1-4

耶和华吩咐摩西重新凿出两块石版，和先前一样；神要将先前版上的话写在这版上。摩西清早起来，照耶和华所吩咐的上西奈山，手里拿着两块石版。

The LORD told Moses to cut two tablets of stone like the first ones, and He would write on them the words that had been on the tablets Moses broke. Moses rose early and went up Mount Sinai with the two stone tablets in his hand.

经文含义 Meaning

破碎之后的重新书写

摩西曾摔碎石版，象征以色列已经破坏 covenant。神没有假装失败不存在，而是让摩西重新凿版，并由神重写话语。经文含义是：更新不是抹去罪的事实，而是在承认破碎之后，由神主动恢复关系和规范。

Moses' broken tablets symbolized Israel's broken covenant. God does not pretend the failure never happened; He commands new tablets and promises to write again. Renewal begins with acknowledged ruin and God's restoring initiative.

灵修应用 Devotional

让神重新刻写

当关系、服事或品格因失败而破裂时，真实的盼望不是自我粉饰，而是带着“新凿的石版”回到神面前。今天这意味着承认责任、停止逃避，并让神的话重新塑造日程、选择和忠诚。

When failure fractures relationships, service, or character, hope is not self-polishing but returning to God with new tablets. Today this means owning responsibility, stopping avoidance, and letting God's word reshape schedules, choices, and loyalties.

34:5-7

耶和华在云中降临，和摩西一同站在那里，宣告耶和华的名：“耶和华，耶和华，是有怜悯有恩典的神，不轻易发怒，并有丰盛的慈爱和诚实；为千万人存留慈爱，赦免罪孽、过犯和罪恶；万不以有罪的为无罪。”

The LORD descended in the cloud, stood with Moses, and proclaimed His name: merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness, keeping love for thousands, forgiving iniquity, transgression, and sin, yet by no means clearing the guilty.

经文含义 Meaning

恩典与公义同在的圣名

这是旧约中最重要的神自我启示之一。神把祂的名解释为怜悯、恩典、忍耐、慈爱和信实，同时说明赦免不等于纵容罪。经文拒绝两种误解：把神想成只会惩罚，或把神想成没有圣洁界限。

This is one of the Old Testament's central self-revelations of God. His name is explained through mercy, grace, patience, loyal love, and faithfulness, while forgiveness never becomes indulgence. The text rejects both a merely punitive God and a holiness without boundaries.

灵修应用 Devotional

按神宣告的样子认识神

人在压力中常按自己的恐惧想象神：要么以为祂不会再怜悯，要么以为祂不会在乎罪。此处邀请我们用神亲口宣告的名校正内心图像，在悔改中靠近祂，也在饶恕与诚实之间学习成熟的爱。

Under pressure we often imagine God through fear: either unable to show mercy or unconcerned about sin. This passage lets God's own name correct our inner picture, drawing us near in repentance and teaching mature love that holds forgiveness and truth together.

34:8-9

摩西急忙伏地下拜，说：“主啊，我若在你眼前蒙恩，求你在我们中间同行；因为这是硬着颈项的百姓。又求你赦免我们的罪孽和罪恶，以我们为你的产业。”

Moses quickly bowed to the ground and worshiped. He asked the Lord to go in their midst, though the people were stiff-necked, and to pardon their iniquity and sin and take them as His inheritance.

经文含义 Meaning

代求建立在神的性情上

摩西没有否认百姓硬着颈项，反而正因如此求神同行、赦免、收纳他们为产业。他的祷告不是靠人的优点说服神，而是抓住神刚刚宣告的怜悯和守约之爱。

Moses does not deny Israel's stubbornness. Precisely because they are stiff-necked, he asks God to go with them, forgive them, and take them as His inheritance. His intercession rests not on human merit but on God's proclaimed mercy and covenant love.

灵修应用 Devotional

为真实的人祷告

为家人、同工或教会祷告时，我们不必把问题修饰得漂亮。成熟的代求能同时说出人的刚硬和神的怜悯，把冲突、疲惫、反复失败带到神面前，请祂亲自同行并更新群体。

When praying for family, coworkers, or church, we do not need to beautify the problem. Mature intercession can name human stubbornness and divine mercy together, bringing conflict, fatigue, and repeated failure before God for His presence and renewal.

Covenant Boundaries for Worship

更新 covenant 的生活边界 (34:10-28)

34:10-17

神说祂要立 covenant，在百姓面前行奇妙的事；但以色列不可与那地居民立 covenant，反要拆毁他们的祭坛和偶像，因为耶和华名为忌邪者，是忌邪的神。百姓不可跪拜别神，也不可为自己铸造神像。

God promised to make a covenant and do marvelous works before the people. Yet Israel must not make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, but tear down their altars and idols, for the LORD is jealous. They must not worship another god or make cast idols.

经文含义 Meaning

恩典更新忠诚，而非纵容混合

神应许奇妙作为，同时要求以色列与偶像体系保持清楚界限。“忌邪”不是任性的占有欲，而是 covenant 中独一敬拜的正当要求。刚经历金牛犊失败的百姓，必须学会不把敬拜与便利、政治或婚姻利益混合。

God promises marvelous works while requiring clear boundaries from idolatrous systems. Divine jealousy is not petty possessiveness, but the rightful claim of exclusive covenant worship. After the golden calf, Israel must not blend worship with convenience, politics, or social advantage.

灵修应用 Devotional

辨认会偷走忠诚的联盟

今天的“联盟”可能是让良心让步的职业规则、把身份建立在业绩上的文化，或用关系安全感取代敬拜。神的恩典不是让我们更会妥协，而是释放我们重新选择忠诚。

Today's alliances may be career rules that silence conscience, cultures that build identity on performance, or relationships that replace worship with security. Grace does not train us to compromise better; it frees us to choose loyalty again.

34:18-26

神吩咐以色列守除酵节，分别头生的归祂，六日做工第七日安息，并守七七节和收藏节；一切男丁要一年三次朝见主耶和华。初熟之物要带到耶和华的殿。

Israel must keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread, devote the firstborn to the Lord, rest on the seventh day, observe the Feast of Weeks and the Feast of Ingathering, and have all males appear before the Lord three times a year. The firstfruits belong in the house of the LORD.

经文含义 Meaning

用时间、产业和节期记住救赎

这些命令不是零散规条，而是把出埃及的记忆嵌入日历、劳动、家庭和收成。安息日宣告生命不由苦工定义；头生和初熟之物宣告供应来自神；节期让群体反复回到救赎故事。

These commands are not scattered rules; they embed exodus memory into calendar, labor, family, and harvest. Sabbath says life is not defined by forced labor. Firstborn and firstfruits confess God's provision. Feasts bring the community repeatedly back to the story of deliverance.

灵修应用 Devotional

让日程成为信仰记忆

如果信仰只停留在想法，很快会被忙碌稀释。我们需要把记念神放进时间表、金钱使用、家庭节奏和休息方式中。规律的敬拜、安息和奉献，不是赚取恩典，而是帮助心不断回到恩典。

If faith remains only an idea, busyness quickly dilutes it. We need remembrance built into schedules, money, family rhythms, and rest. Regular worship, Sabbath, and giving do not earn grace; they help the heart return to grace.

34:27-28

耶和华吩咐摩西写下这些话，因为祂是按这些话与摩西和以色列人立 covenant。摩西在耶和华那里四十昼夜，不吃饭也不喝水，耶和华将这 covenant 的话，就是十条诫，写在版上。

The LORD told Moses to write these words, for according to these words He made a covenant with Moses and Israel. Moses was with the LORD forty days and forty nights without eating or drinking, and the Lord wrote the words of the covenant, the Ten Words, on the tablets.

经文含义 Meaning

更新的 covenant 有可听见的话

神的同在不只是神秘经验，也以可传递、可记忆、可顺服的话语临到群体。四十昼夜强调摩西作为中保的严肃托付；十条诫不是抽象道德，而是被救赎群体的生活形状。

God's presence is not only mystical experience; it comes as words that can be received, remembered, and obeyed. The forty days highlight Moses' solemn mediating role. The Ten Words shape the life of a redeemed community.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要只追求感动，也要接受塑造

许多人渴望属灵感动，却抗拒清楚的话语约束。出埃及记 34 章提醒我们，真正遇见神会带来可实践的顺服：在说话、金钱、休息、性关系和敬拜上，让神的话有实际权柄。

Many desire spiritual emotion while resisting clear instruction. Exodus 34 reminds us that real encounter with God produces embodied obedience: in speech, money, rest, sexuality, and worship, God's word receives practical authority.

The Shining Face of Moses

与神说话后的荣光见证 (34:29-35)

34:29-32

摩西手里拿着两块法版下西奈山时，不知道自己的面皮因耶和华和他说话就发了光。亚伦和以色列众人看见他面皮发光就怕挨近他；摩西呼唤他们，向他们吩咐耶和华在西奈山与他说的一切话。

When Moses came down from Sinai with the two tablets, he did not know that the skin of his face shone because he had been speaking with the LORD. Aaron and Israel feared to come near, but Moses called them and commanded all that the LORD had spoken.

经文含义 Meaning

真正的光来自与神说话

摩西不知道自己面皮发光，这细节避免把他描写成自我炫耀的领袖。光来自耶和华与他说话，也服务于传达神的话。领袖的权柄不是魅力包装，而是被神话语塑造后忠心传递。

Moses does not know his face shines, which keeps the scene from becoming self-display. The radiance comes from speaking with the LORD and serves the communication of God's word. Authority is not charisma packaging, but faithful transmission shaped by God's presence.

灵修应用 Devotional

让相遇留下痕迹

真正的灵修不会只停在私下情绪，也会在公共生活中留下痕迹：更诚实的表达、更温柔的权柄、更不急着证明自己。我们不需要制造光，而是持续来到神面前，让祂改变别人能感受到的生命质地。

True devotion does not remain private emotion; it leaves marks in public life: more honest speech, gentler authority, less need to prove oneself. We do not manufacture radiance; we keep coming before God and let Him alter the texture others can feel.

34:33-35

摩西与他们说完了话，就用帕子蒙上脸；但摩西进到耶和华面前与祂说话，就揭去帕子，出来再将所吩咐的告诉以色列人。以色列人看见摩西的面皮发光，摩西又用帕子蒙上脸，等到他进去与耶和华说话就揭去。

When Moses finished speaking, he put a veil over his face. Whenever he went before the LORD to speak with Him, he removed the veil; then he came out and told Israel what he was commanded. Israel saw his shining face, and Moses veiled it again until he went in to speak with the LORD.

经文含义 Meaning

揭开与遮盖之间的中保职分

帕子显示神圣荣光与百姓之间仍有距离；摩西在神面前揭开，在百姓面前按需要遮盖，体现他作为中保的职分。经文让读者感到亲近已经恢复，却仍在圣洁界限中进行。

The veil marks a remaining distance between holy glory and the people. Moses unveils before God and veils before Israel as needed, embodying his mediating role. Fellowship is restored, yet still ordered by holy limits.

灵修应用 Devotional

在亲近和敬畏中生活

健康的信仰既不是随便，也不是疏离。我们可以真实亲近神，也要尊重祂的圣洁；可以坦诚服事人，也要知道属灵经历不是表演素材。亲近神越深，越能安静、清楚、负责任地面对人。

Healthy faith is neither casual nor distant. We may draw near to God honestly while honoring His holiness; we may serve people openly while refusing to turn spiritual experience into performance. Deeper nearness makes us quieter, clearer, and more responsible before others.

方法论 / Methodology

从经文含义到灵修应用的双轨阅读

本页采用双轨结构：左轨先问“经文在出埃及记上下文中原本说明什么”，右轨再问“这个真理如何通过桥梁原则进入今天的生活”。这样既避免把经文任意寓意化，也避免把查经停留在资料层面。

This page uses two tracks: the left asks what the text meant in Exodus' own context, and the right asks how that truth crosses a bridge into present life. This avoids arbitrary allegory while keeping study from remaining mere information.

正统解经原则

Contextual Exegesis

  • 上下文：第 34 章必须放在金牛犊、摩西代求和神同在危机之后阅读。Read Exodus 34 after the golden calf, Moses' intercession, and the crisis of divine presence.
  • 字词观察：“怜悯”“恩典”“忌邪”“covenant”“面皮发光”等词决定本章重心。Terms such as mercy, grace, jealousy, covenant, and shining face shape the chapter's emphasis.
  • 整体神学：神的赦免与圣洁不可分开，更新与顺服也不可分开。God's forgiveness and holiness belong together; renewal and obedience belong together.

灵修应用路径

Devotional Bridge

  • 桥梁原则：神怎样在破碎后更新 covenant，也呼召今天的人在失败后真实悔改并重新被话语塑造。As God renews covenant after ruin, He calls people today to repent honestly and be reshaped by His word.
  • 生活落点：把忠诚落实在时间表、工作伦理、关系边界、奉献和安息中。Practice loyalty through schedules, work ethics, relational boundaries, giving, and rest.
  • 属灵辨识：真正与神相遇会产生谦卑和责任，不会变成自我展示。True encounter with God produces humility and responsibility, not self-display.
