出埃及记 21章 Exodus Chapter 21

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

约中的公义：限制强权，保护生命，修复损害

Covenant Justice: Restraining Power, Protecting Life, Repairing Harm

出埃及记第 21 章开启“约书”的具体典章。它把西奈山上所领受的敬畏带进日常社会：仆人的年限、女性的保障、谋杀与误杀的分别、伤害赔偿、孕妇受伤、仆人身体受损、危险牲畜与疏忽造成的损失。经文的重点不是建立一个理想化的现代法典，而是在古代近东现实中显明神如何约束强者、限制报复、保护生命，并要求人对邻舍的损害负责。

Exodus 21 opens the Covenant Code by moving Sinai's reverence into daily social life: servants, vulnerable women, murder and manslaughter, bodily injury, harm to pregnant women, injured servants, dangerous animals, and negligent loss. The chapter shows covenant justice restraining power, limiting revenge, protecting life, and requiring repair for harm done to neighbors.

出埃及记 21 章经文双轨研读

本章研读先观察典章如何从十诫延伸到社会生活，再把“保护生命、限制报复、承担责任”的原则带入今日家庭、职场、冲突处理与公共责任。

This study first observes how the judgments extend the Ten Words into social life, then applies protection of life, restrained retaliation, and responsibility to family, work, conflict, and public duty.

Release and Protection in Servant Laws

仆人条例中的释放与保护 (21:1-11)

21:1-6

经文摘述：神吩咐摩西把典章摆在百姓面前。希伯来仆人服事六年，第七年可以自由出去；若他因爱主人和家人自愿留下，就在审判者面前作公开确认。

Summary: The LORD gives judgments to be set before Israel. A Hebrew servant serves six years and goes free in the seventh; if he freely chooses to remain with his household, the decision is publicly confirmed before the judges.

经文含义 Meaning

自由不是恩赐之外的例外，而是典章的方向

这段把“第七年释放”放在仆人条例的开头，说明以色列自己的被救赎经历要塑造他们怎样对待弱势者。自愿留下的程序公开化，限制主人私下操控，也承认家庭责任的复杂处境。

The servant laws begin with release in the seventh year, letting Israel’s own redemption shape treatment of vulnerable people. Public confirmation restrains private coercion and recognizes complex household realities.

灵修应用 Devotional

在权力关系里主动给人出路

今天我们未必处在古代仆役制度中，却常在职场、家庭或服事里拥有某种影响力。本段提醒我们：敬畏神的人不把别人困在自己的利益里，而是在权力关系中创造尊重、选择与释放的空间。

We may not live inside ancient servitude, yet we hold influence at work, home, or ministry. Reverence for God means not trapping others for our benefit but creating space for dignity, choice, and release.

21:7-11

经文摘述：若女子被卖为婢，条例要求她不可被当作普通男仆随意打发；若主人不履行婚约或生活供应的责任，她可以无偿出去。

Summary: If a daughter is given as a servant-wife, she is not to be dismissed as a male servant might be. If the man fails to provide covenantal rights, food, clothing, and marital care, she may go free without payment.

经文含义 Meaning

经文在古代现实中保护被交易的女性

这段处境艰难，却显明律法在当时社会里限制男性家主权力。女子不是可任意丢弃的物件；若承诺、供应和婚姻责任被破坏，她有离开的权利。经文把弱者的保障写进约民秩序。

The setting is difficult, yet the law restrains patriarchal power within its ancient context. The woman is not disposable property; if promises and provision are violated, she has a protected way out.

灵修应用 Devotional

把承诺落实为具体照顾

爱不是口头身份，必须落在供应、尊重和忠诚中。现代关系里的承诺也需要可见的责任：按时出现、照顾需要、拒绝利用脆弱者，并在失败时愿意修复。

Love is not merely a verbal status; it takes shape in provision, respect, and faithfulness. Modern commitments also require visible responsibility: presence, care, refusal to exploit vulnerability, and repair when we fail.

Life Must Be Honored and Guilt Discerned

生命不可轻看，罪责须分辨 (21:12-17)

21:12-14

经文摘述：打人致死者要受严肃审判；但若不是预谋，神为误杀者设逃避之处。若人蓄意杀害邻舍，即使逃到祭坛也不能借宗教外衣逃避公义。

Summary: Striking a person so that death results requires grave judgment. Yet unplanned killing has a place of refuge. Premeditated murder cannot hide behind the altar or religious appearance.

经文含义 Meaning

律法区分误杀与谋杀

经文同时维护生命价值和司法辨别。不是所有死亡案件都被同样处理；动机、预谋与事实需要审察。祭坛不能成为罪的避难所，敬拜也不能遮盖故意的暴力。

The law upholds life while requiring judicial discernment. Not every death is treated alike; motive, intent, and facts matter. The altar cannot become a refuge for deliberate violence.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要用属灵语言遮盖真实伤害

人在冲突中容易把自己包装成无辜，甚至借信仰语言逃避责任。本段呼召我们诚实面对行为的后果：该认罪就认罪，该接受查明就接受查明，让敬拜与公义同行。

In conflict we may present ourselves as innocent or use spiritual language to avoid responsibility. This passage calls us to face consequences honestly, allowing worship and justice to walk together.

21:15-17

经文摘述：击打父母、拐卖人口、咒骂父母都被列为严重罪行。家庭权柄、人的自由与代际尊重都受到约中公义的保护。

Summary: Striking parents, kidnapping and selling a person, and cursing parents are treated as grave offenses. Family order, human freedom, and generational honor are protected under covenant justice.

经文含义 Meaning

尊重父母与保护自由同属约民秩序

这些条例紧接在杀人之后，显示神看重家庭中的暴力、人口掠夺和言语背叛。拐卖人口尤其显明，人不能把神所造的人当作商品。第五诫在这里得到社会层面的展开。

These laws follow murder because violence in the home, human theft, and verbal betrayal matter to God. Kidnapping especially shows that people made by God must not be commodified.

灵修应用 Devotional

让尊重从家中和语言中开始

现代人可能不会参与拐卖，却可能用言语贬损、控制或利用别人。本段提醒我们，敬畏神从最亲近的关系开始，也延伸到尊重每个人的自由与尊严。

We may not be kidnappers, but we can demean, control, or use others with words and power. Reverence for God begins in close relationships and extends to every person’s freedom and dignity.

Injury, Restitution, and Proportion

伤害、赔偿与相称原则 (21:18-27)

21:18-19

经文摘述：两人相争，一方受伤卧床，若后来能起来行走，伤人者仍要赔偿误工与医治费用。

Summary: When a fight injures someone who later recovers and walks again, the offender must still compensate for lost time and medical care.

经文含义 Meaning

伤害即使不致死，也需要修复

经文不把“没有死”当作无事发生。误工、医疗和恢复期都被纳入责任范围。约中的公义关注实际损失，不只关注案件是否达到最严重程度。

The text does not treat “no death” as “no harm.” Lost time, care, and recovery are included. Covenant justice attends to concrete losses, not only the most severe outcomes.

灵修应用 Devotional

道歉之后还要承担修复成本

我们伤害别人后，容易只想用一句“对不起”结束。但真正的悔改会关心对方实际承担了什么：时间、精力、名誉、情绪和资源。修复常需要付代价。

After harming someone, we may want one apology to close the matter. True repentance asks what the other person actually bore: time, energy, reputation, emotion, and resources. Repair often costs us.

21:20-21, 26-27

经文摘述：若主人伤害仆人致死，要受审判；若打坏仆人的眼或牙，仆人因身体受损可以得自由。

Summary: If a master strikes a servant and death follows, judgment is required. If the master destroys a servant’s eye or tooth, the servant goes free because of the injury.

经文含义 Meaning

主人权力受到神的审判限制

在古代世界，仆人常被视为主人财产；本段却把主人暴力带到审判之下，并用释放作为身体伤害的补偿。经文朝着保护被支配者的方向设限。

In the ancient world servants were often treated as property, yet this law brings masterly violence under judgment and uses release as remedy for bodily injury. Power is placed under divine limits.

灵修应用 Devotional

看见制度中最容易被忽略的人

任何组织都有更容易受伤的人：新人、基层员工、孩子、病人、移民或依赖者。属神的公义要求我们留意权力下方的人，并为他们建立可申诉、可保护的空间。

Every setting has people more easily harmed: newcomers, lower-level workers, children, the ill, immigrants, or dependents. God’s justice teaches us to notice those under power and build protective channels.

21:22-25

经文摘述：若争斗中伤及怀孕妇人，造成伤害，就要按损害程度受审；经文以“命偿命、眼还眼、牙还牙”等表达相称审判的原则。

Summary: If fighting harms a pregnant woman and injury follows, judgment is made according to the harm. “Life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth” expresses proportional justice.

经文含义 Meaning

相称原则限制报复扩张

这段强调孕妇与胎中生命不应被卷入暴力。所谓“眼还眼”在司法语境中是相称原则：处罚不能轻忽伤害，也不能超过伤害而变成报复狂潮。

The passage refuses to treat a pregnant woman and her child as collateral damage. “Eye for eye” functions as a judicial principle of proportion, neither minimizing harm nor escalating revenge.

灵修应用 Devotional

愤怒也要接受神的边界

受伤之后，人很容易想让对方付出更多代价。神的典章不否认伤害，却给愤怒设边界。今天处理冲突时，我们需要寻求公正、事实与修复，而不是让情绪决定惩罚。

After being hurt, we may want the offender to pay more than justice requires. God does not deny harm, but He sets boundaries for anger. We seek truth, justice, and repair instead of emotional retaliation.

Danger, Negligence, and Neighbor Responsibility

危险、疏忽与邻舍责任 (21:28-36)

21:28-32

经文摘述：牛若触死人，要处理危险；若牛素来会触人而主人不看管，主人要承担更重责任。若受害者是仆人，也要有赔偿。

Summary: If an ox gores a person, the danger must be addressed. If the ox was known to be dangerous and the owner failed to guard it, the owner bears heavier responsibility. Harm to a servant also requires compensation.

经文含义 Meaning

知道危险却不处理，就是有责的疏忽

本段把“可预见风险”纳入道德责任。主人若不知道危险，处理方式较轻；若明知却不看管，就不是意外这么简单。约民生活要求人管理自己的资源，使邻舍不被伤害。

The law treats foreseeable risk as moral responsibility. Unknown danger is handled differently from known danger ignored. Covenant life requires managing one’s resources so neighbors are not harmed.

灵修应用 Devotional

为自己制造的风险负责

现代生活中的“牛”可能是车辆、设备、言语习惯、网络平台、财务决定或团队文化。若我们知道某件事会伤人却继续放任，就需要在神面前承认并采取实际防护。

Today’s “ox” may be a car, tool, speech pattern, online platform, financial choice, or workplace culture. If we know something can harm others and leave it unchecked, we must confess and act protectively.

21:33-36

经文摘述：人若敞开坑或挖坑不遮盖，导致牲畜掉入，就要赔偿。若牛伤害别人的牛，也按责任程度处理；若主人早知牛会触人而不看管，就要全额赔还。

Summary: If someone opens or digs a pit and fails to cover it, causing an animal to fall in, restitution is required. If one ox injures another, responsibility is weighed; known negligence brings full repayment.

经文含义 Meaning

公义关心日常疏忽造成的损失

本章最后从重大暴力转向看似普通的财产损失。神的典章并不只处理“属灵大事”，也处理坑是否遮盖、牲畜是否看管。敬畏神会改变人管理日常事务的方式。

The chapter closes by moving from severe violence to ordinary property loss. God’s judgments address not only “big” matters but also whether pits are covered and animals are guarded.

灵修应用 Devotional

在小事上预先爱邻舍

遮盖一个坑，是还没有出事以前的爱。许多关系破裂、工作事故和财务损害，都可以因提前沟通、清楚流程和负责管理而避免。灵修不是逃离细节，而是在细节里爱人。

Covering a pit is love before an accident happens. Many relational, workplace, and financial harms can be prevented by communication, clear process, and responsible stewardship. Devotion loves people in details.

方法论 / Methodology

从古代典章读出现代责任

出埃及记 21 章需要特别谨慎地读：它处理的是古代以色列约民社会中的具体案件，不应被直接剪贴成现代民法，也不应被用来替暴力、奴役或轻看女性辩护。稳健读法先问：这些条例在当时如何限制强者、保护生命、分辨责任、要求赔偿？然后再把这些原则转化为今天可实践的邻舍之爱。

Exodus 21 must be read carefully. It addresses concrete cases in ancient Israel’s covenant society and should not be copied as modern civil law or used to defend violence, slavery, or contempt for women. Sound reading asks how these laws restrained the powerful, protected life, discerned guilt, and required restitution, then carries those principles into neighbor-love today.

正统解经原则

Sound Exegesis

  • 读在约书脉络中：第 21 章紧接十诫，说明敬拜神必然影响社会公义。Read the chapter as the Covenant Code following the Ten Words: worship of God shapes social justice.
  • 区分描述与方向：经文面对古代制度现实，同时用释放、赔偿和限制权力指向更合神心意的秩序。The text addresses ancient institutions while directing them toward release, restitution, and restrained power.
  • 重视相称原则：“以眼还眼”不是私刑许可，而是司法边界，防止报复失控。“Eye for eye” is not a license for vengeance but a judicial boundary against disproportionate retaliation.

灵修应用路径

Devotional Application

  • 桥梁原则：古代典章今日转化为责任伦理：不滥用权力、不扩大报复、主动修复损害。The ancient judgments become responsibility ethics today: do not abuse power, escalate revenge, or ignore repair.
  • 个人省察：问自己是否有“未遮盖的坑”：明知可能伤人却没有处理的风险、习惯或关系模式。Ask whether you have an “uncovered pit”: a known risk, habit, or relational pattern left unaddressed.
  • 祷告落地：求神使我们的公义不止停在观点上，而成为保护弱者、赔还亏欠、守护邻舍的具体行动。Pray that justice becomes concrete action: protecting the vulnerable, repaying what is owed, and guarding neighbors.
