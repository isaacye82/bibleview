出埃及记 1章 Exodus Chapter 1

本章核心主题 · Chapter Theme

压迫中的保存与兴起

Preserved and Multiplied Under Oppression

出埃及记第一章从雅各家进入埃及的名单开始，显示创世记的应许没有中断。新王不认识约瑟，以恐惧和政策压迫以色列人；但越压迫，神的子民越发增多。两个收生婆敬畏神，拒绝把国家命令置于生命之上，成为本章安静却关键的见证。

Exodus 1 begins with Jacob's family in Egypt and shows that the promise of Genesis has not failed. A new king who does not know Joseph turns fear into policy and oppresses Israel; yet the more they are afflicted, the more they multiply. Two midwives fear God and refuse to place a royal command above human life, becoming quiet but decisive witnesses.

神学概念卡片组合

Key concepts for reading Exodus 1 faithfully

应许的延续

Continuity of Promise

parah / ravah

“生养众多”的语言回响创世记，说明神的祝福在异地、低处、受压环境中仍然运行。
The language of multiplying echoes Genesis, showing that God's blessing continues even in a foreign, pressured place.

恐惧驱动的权力

Power Driven by Fear

nithakkemah

法老以“来吧，我们用巧计待他们”把不安全感制度化；经文揭露压迫常从惧怕他人开始。
Pharaoh turns insecurity into policy; the chapter exposes how oppression often begins with fear of the other.

苦工与砖坯

Forced Labor and Bricks

avodah qashah

严酷劳役不只是经济剥削，也是身份压制；埃及试图用砖和城定义以色列人的未来。
Harsh labor is not merely economic exploitation; Egypt tries to define Israel's future through bricks and store cities.

敬畏神的勇气

Courage That Fears God

yare Elohim

收生婆的敬畏不是抽象情绪，而是在具体命令面前保护生命、拒绝参与暴力。
The midwives' fear of God is practical: they protect life and refuse to cooperate with violence.

研读路径 · Study Flow

从增多、压迫到敬畏神的见证

From multiplication and oppression to the witness of God-fearing courage.

1:1-7

以色列的众子各带家眷来到埃及。约瑟和他的弟兄，并那一代的人都死了；以色列人生养众多，极其强盛，满了那地。

The sons of Israel came into Egypt with their households. Joseph, his brothers, and that generation died; yet the children of Israel were fruitful, increased abundantly, and the land was filled with them.

经文含义 Meaning

从家族名单到应许扩展

开头的名单把出埃及记接续到创世记。约瑟死了，但神给亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各的应许没有死。“生养众多”不是普通人口统计，而是应许在历史压力中继续发芽。

The opening genealogy connects Exodus to Genesis. Joseph has died, but God's promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob has not. Israel's multiplication is not mere population growth; it is promise taking root under historical pressure.

灵修应用 Devotional

看见神在低处仍工作

有些季节像埃及：不是理想之地，却是神继续塑造生命的地方。当熟悉的支持离开时，信心学习不把盼望绑在某个人或环境上，而是记念神仍能使生命增长。

Some seasons feel like Egypt: not ideal, yet still a place where God forms life. When familiar supports are gone, faith learns not to tie hope to one person or setting, but to remember that God can still bring growth.

1:8-14

有不认识约瑟的新王兴起，惧怕以色列人强盛，就派督工辖制他们，叫他们建造积货城，并用严酷苦工使他们生活愁烦。只是越发苦害，他们越发多起来。

A new king arose who did not know Joseph. Fearing Israel's strength, he set taskmasters over them, forced them to build store cities, and made their lives bitter with hard bondage. Yet the more they were afflicted, the more they multiplied.

经文含义 Meaning

恐惧变成制度性压迫

法老“不认识约瑟”不只是资料缺失，也表示对过去恩典的拒绝。经文让我们看见权力若被恐惧支配，就会把人变成威胁、劳力和数字；但压迫不能废掉神保存百姓的旨意。

Pharaoh's failure to know Joseph is more than missing information; it signals rejection of remembered grace. Fearful power turns people into threats, labor, and numbers, yet oppression cannot cancel God's preserving purpose.

灵修应用 Devotional

不让惧怕支配待人方式

职场、家庭或社群中，惧怕常让人用控制代替信任。出埃及记提醒我们先辨认心里的恐惧，再拒绝把压力转嫁给更弱的人。敬畏神的人学习在紧张局面中仍按人的尊严待人。

In workplaces, families, and communities, fear can replace trust with control. Exodus calls us to name our fears and refuse to pass pressure downward. Those who fear God learn to treat people with dignity even in tense settings.

1:15-21

埃及王命令希伯来收生婆施弗拉和普阿，若是男孩就杀他；收生婆却敬畏神，不照王命而行，存留男孩的性命。神厚待收生婆，百姓也越发强盛。

The king of Egypt commanded the Hebrew midwives, Shiphrah and Puah, to kill the boys. But the midwives feared God and did not do as the king commanded; they let the boys live. God dealt well with the midwives, and the people grew stronger.

经文含义 Meaning

敬畏神高过惧怕王

施弗拉和普阿的名字被记下，法老的名字反而没有出现。经文含义清楚：真正有重量的不是帝国头衔，而是敬畏神、保护生命的忠心行动。她们的抵抗安静，却合乎神的心意。

Shiphrah and Puah are named while Pharaoh is not. The point is clear: imperial titles are not the true weight in history; faithful actions that fear God and protect life are. Their resistance is quiet, but aligned with God's heart.

灵修应用 Devotional

在小岗位上守住生命原则

很多信心抉择不是发生在讲台上，而是在岗位、流程、签字、会议和照护现场。现代应用不是鼓励鲁莽对抗，而是问：当命令伤害生命和良心时，我能否用智慧、勇气和代价保护正确的事？

Many acts of faith happen not on a platform but in jobs, procedures, signatures, meetings, and care. The application is not reckless defiance; it asks whether we can use wisdom, courage, and costly integrity to protect what is right when commands wound life and conscience.

1:22

法老吩咐他的众民说：以色列人所生的男孩都要丢在河里，一切女孩可以存留。

Pharaoh charged all his people, saying that every son born to the Hebrews must be cast into the river, while every daughter might live.

经文含义 Meaning

黑暗加深，拯救伏笔出现

命令从暗中的收生婆扩大到全社会，罪恶公开制度化。河本是死亡工具，却将在下一章成为摩西被保存的地方；经文在最黑暗处预备读者看见神反转人的恶谋。

The command expands from private pressure on midwives to public participation by society. The river is meant as an instrument of death, yet in the next chapter it becomes the place where Moses is preserved. The darkest point prepares us to see God overturn evil intent.

灵修应用 Devotional

在未见出路时持守盼望

有些处境暂时只看见命令、压力和风险，尚未看见“摩西”。本章邀请我们不轻看黑暗，却也不把黑暗当作最后一句话。祷告中的盼望，是在出路未显明时仍选择忠心。

Some situations show only orders, pressure, and risk before any “Moses” is visible. This chapter does not minimize darkness, but neither does it let darkness speak the final word. Hope in prayer remains faithful before the way forward is clear.

路径一 · Path One

正统解经原则

先看上下文：出埃及记1章承接创世记中神给列祖的应许，也为摩西出生和出埃及拯救铺路。再看重复词：增多、苦害、敬畏神、存留生命，构成本章的阅读线索。

Begin with context: Exodus 1 continues the patriarchal promises in Genesis and prepares for Moses' birth and the deliverance from Egypt. Then observe repeated ideas: multiplication, affliction, fear of God, and preserving life.

路径二 · Path Two

灵修应用原则

灵修应用不是把自己直接放成主角，而是从经文原意搭桥到今日生活：当环境压迫、权力制造惧怕、良心面对压力时，信徒如何敬畏神、尊重生命、持守盼望。

Devotional application does not make us the main character. It builds a bridge from the text's meaning to life today: when circumstances oppress, power creates fear, and conscience is pressured, believers learn to fear God, honor life, and hold hope.

落地指南 · Practical Guide

观察 / Observe
列出谁在行动、谁在受苦、神的应许如何仍在推进。
Notice who acts, who suffers, and how God's promise continues.
辨认 / Discern
分辨惧怕如何塑造人的命令、制度和关系。
Discern how fear shapes commands, systems, and relationships.
回应 / Respond
在今天具体岗位中选择敬畏神、保护生命、拒绝参与伤害。
Choose in concrete roles to fear God, protect life, and refuse harm.
