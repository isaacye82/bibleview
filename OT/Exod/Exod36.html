出埃及记 36章 Exodus Chapter 36

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

甘心奉献与受托手艺使会幕成形

Willing Gifts and Gifted Hands Shape the Tabernacle

出埃及记第 36 章从神所赐的智慧开始，转向百姓甘心献上的充足，再细述会幕各部分的制作。经文重复“照耶和华所吩咐的”，强调敬拜不是即兴发挥，而是按神启示的样式把材料、技巧与顺服编织在一起。最醒目的场景是奉献多到必须停止：神同在的工程显出一种被恩典唤醒的慷慨，也需要忠心治理。

Exodus 36 begins with wisdom given by God, moves to abundant willing gifts, and then describes the tabernacle being made piece by piece. The repeated concern for the commanded pattern shows that worship is not improvisation but obedience shaped through material, skill, and generosity. The people give so much that Moses must stop them, revealing both grace-awakened generosity and faithful stewardship.

百姓持续带来礼物，直到摩西必须吩咐停止。真正的奉献不是被压力驱动，而是被神同在的盼望吸引。The people give until restraint is needed; generosity is drawn by the hope of God dwelling among them.

研读视图 / Study View

出埃及记 36 章经文双轨研读

本章从工人的蒙召和百姓奉献开始，再进入会幕具体制作。每个单元先观察经文含义，再把古代圣所工程连接到今日工作、奉献、界限与群体生活。

The chapter moves from called workers and willing gifts into the making of the tabernacle. Each unit reads the text first, then connects the ancient sanctuary work to modern labor, generosity, boundaries, and community life.

Skilled Workers Receive the Charge

智慧工人领受托付 (36:1-2)

36:1-2

比撒列、亚何利亚伯和一切心中有智慧的人，要照耶和华所吩咐的做圣所各样的工。凡心里受感、愿意前来作工的人，都被摩西召来。

Bezalel, Oholiab, and every wise-hearted person were to do the work of the sanctuary according to all that the LORD had commanded. Moses called those whose hearts were stirred to come and serve.

经文含义 Meaning

恩赐与顺服相连 / Gift Joined to Obedience

本段把“智慧”放在神的吩咐之下。工人的才能不是个人表现的舞台，而是为圣所服务的托付；“心里受感”也说明参与者不是被强迫，而是被神的旨意与同在吸引。

The workers’ wisdom is placed under the Lord’s command. Skill is not self-display but entrusted service, and stirred hearts show willing participation in God’s dwelling project.

灵修应用 Devotional

把专业变成敬拜 / Turning Skill into Worship

今天的工作技能、管理能力、设计能力或照顾能力，都可以在顺服中成为敬拜。关键不是把宗教标签贴在工作上，而是在动机、诚实和服务对象上让神居首位。

Modern skills in work, design, leadership, or care can become worship when offered in obedience. The issue is not a religious label but motive, integrity, and service before God.

Offerings Become More Than Enough

奉献充足直到停止 (36:3-7)

36:3-7

百姓早晨仍不断把甘心礼带来。工匠告诉摩西：“百姓为耶和华吩咐使用之工所拿来的，富富有余。”于是摩西传令停止奉献；材料已经够做一切工作，而且有余。

The people kept bringing freewill offerings morning by morning. The craftsmen told Moses that the people were bringing more than enough for the work commanded by the LORD, so Moses ordered the giving to stop. The material was sufficient and more.

经文含义 Meaning

慷慨也需要治理 / Generosity Needs Stewardship

经文罕见地记录“停止奉献”的命令。这不是轻看奉献，而是说明圣工不应靠无止境的索取维持。神唤起充足供应，领袖也要诚实判断需要与界限。

The unusual command to stop giving honors generosity by governing it truthfully. Sacred work should not be sustained by endless extraction; leaders must discern both need and limits.

灵修应用 Devotional

在丰盛中学习节制 / Learning Restraint in Abundance

当我们热心投入服事、家庭或公益时，也需要问：现在真正需要什么？是否已经足够？爱心若没有界限，可能变成消耗；忠心包括给，也包括适时停下。

In ministry, family, or public service we must ask what is truly needed and when enough has been reached. Love without boundaries can become depletion; faithfulness includes giving and stopping.

The Fine Curtains Are Woven

帐幕幔子被精细编织 (36:8-13)

36:8-13

心中有智慧的人用捻的细麻和蓝色、紫色、朱红色线制造帐幕的十幅幔子，并在幔子上绣基路伯。幅与幅彼此相连，金钩使帐幕成为一个整体。

The wise-hearted workers made ten curtains of fine linen with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, with cherubim skillfully worked into them. The curtains were joined together with gold clasps so the tabernacle became one whole.

经文含义 Meaning

美丽服务于同在 / Beauty Serves Presence

色彩、刺绣、尺寸和连接方式都被详细记录，表明美不是附属装饰。会幕的美见证神同在的尊贵；金钩把分散的幅连接成一体，也显出敬拜空间的秩序。

The details of color, embroidery, measure, and joining show that beauty is not incidental. It witnesses to the dignity of God’s presence, and the clasps order many pieces into one dwelling.

灵修应用 Devotional

把碎片连接成整体 / Joining Fragmented Pieces

我们的生活常被切成工作、家庭、信仰和休息的片段。本段提醒我们，让神的同在成为中心时，分散的责任能被重新连接，形成更整全的生活节奏。

Our lives are often fragmented into work, family, faith, and rest. When God’s presence becomes central, scattered responsibilities can be joined into a more whole rhythm.

Outer Coverings Protect the Sanctuary

外层遮盖保护圣所 (36:14-19)

36:14-19

他们又用山羊毛制造罩棚的幔子，并做羊皮、海狗皮等遮盖，使圣所外层得到保护。这些遮盖不像内层幔子那样华丽，却承担遮蔽与防护的功能。

They made curtains of goats’ hair for the tent and additional coverings of skins to protect the sanctuary. These outer layers were less ornate than the inner curtains, yet they carried the vital work of shelter.

经文含义 Meaning

隐藏的层也重要 / Hidden Layers Matter

外层材料提醒读者，圣所不仅有可见的美，也有不显眼的保护结构。神的居所需要华丽的幔子，也需要耐用的遮盖；两者都属于顺服的工作。

The outer materials show that the sanctuary includes visible beauty and unseen protection. God’s dwelling needs ornate curtains and durable coverings; both are obedient work.

灵修应用 Devotional

尊重不被看见的服事 / Honoring Unseen Service

许多重要劳动并不被人称赞：整理、维护、倾听、守时、承担细节。出埃及记 36 章帮助我们看见，神也悦纳那些保护群体、却很少被注意到的工作。

Much essential labor is rarely applauded: organizing, maintaining, listening, punctual care, and detail work. Exodus 36 teaches us to honor service that protects the community without public attention.

Frames, Bases, and Bars Form the Structure

板、卯座与闩形成结构 (36:20-34)

36:20-30

他们用皂荚木做帐幕的竖板，每块板有两榫相对，并用银卯座承托。南面、北面、西面都有规定数目的板与卯座，使帐幕有稳定结构。

They made upright frames of acacia wood, each with two tenons, set in silver bases. The south, north, and west sides had their appointed number of frames and bases, giving the tabernacle a stable structure.

经文含义 Meaning

属灵热心需要结构 / Zeal Needs Structure

本段的数字和构件显得重复，却说明神同在的空间需要稳定、可承重的结构。敬拜不只靠感动，也需要按样式建造的秩序与可靠性。

The repeated numbers and parts show that the dwelling of God requires stable, weight-bearing structure. Worship involves feeling, but also ordered reliability according to the pattern.

灵修应用 Devotional

为呼召建立承重系统 / Building Load-bearing Rhythms

若没有结构，热心很快耗尽。规律的祷告、诚实的预算、清楚的责任分工、健康的休息，都是今天“卯座与竖板”式的操练，使呼召可以长期站立。

Without structure, zeal burns out. Prayer rhythms, honest budgets, clear responsibilities, and healthy rest function like frames and bases, allowing a calling to stand over time.

36:31-34

他们又用皂荚木做闩，穿过各面的板，并把板和闩包上金子，又为闩做金环，使整体彼此联结。

They made bars of acacia wood to run through the frames on each side, overlaid the frames and bars with gold, and made gold rings so the structure held together.

经文含义 Meaning

联结使整体坚固 / Connection Makes Strength

闩与金环的作用是把单独的板连成整体。会幕不是许多独立构件的堆放，而是彼此连接、共同承重的圣所结构。

Bars and rings connect individual frames into one structure. The tabernacle is not a pile of isolated parts but a joined sanctuary that bears weight together.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要单独承受重量 / Do Not Bear Weight Alone

现代人常把压力个人化，独自扛起家庭、事奉或职场重担。本段提醒我们，神的工程有连接机制；成熟的信心会寻求同行、问责与彼此扶持。

Modern life often isolates pressure. This passage reminds us that God’s work includes structures of connection; mature faith seeks companionship, accountability, and mutual support.

The Veil and Screen Establish Worshipful Boundaries

幔子与门帘设立敬拜边界 (36:35-38)

36:35-38

他们用蓝色、紫色、朱红色线和细麻织幔子，又绣上基路伯；并为会幕门口做帘子，配上柱子、钩子和座。

They made the veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn with fine linen and cherubim, and they made the screen for the entrance of the tent with its pillars, hooks, and bases.

经文含义 Meaning

亲近与敬畏并存 / Nearness with Reverence

幔子和门帘说明神愿意住在百姓中间，却也按圣洁次序设立边界。会幕欢迎人按神的方式亲近，而不是把神变成可随意支配的对象。

The veil and entrance screen show that God dwells among His people while establishing holy order. The tabernacle welcomes approach in God’s way, not casual control of God.

灵修应用 Devotional

用边界保护亲密 / Boundaries Protect Intimacy

健康关系需要亲近，也需要界限。无论是与神、家人或同工相处，尊重边界不是冷漠，而是让爱不被占有欲、操控或混乱破坏。

Healthy relationships need both nearness and boundaries. With God, family, or coworkers, honoring limits is not coldness; it protects love from control, possession, and confusion.

方法论 / Methodology

从经文原意走向今日操练

本页采用双轨读经：先问经文在出埃及记整体叙事中的原意，再问这原意如何塑造今天的信心、工作与群体生活。

This page uses a two-track reading: first asking what the text means in Exodus, then asking how that meaning shapes present faith, work, and community.

正统解经原则 / Exegetical Principles

读出埃及记 36 章时，先尊重上下文：它承接 35 章的奉献与工人呼召，并落实 25-31 章神启示的会幕样式。因此重点不是随意寓意化每个材料，而是看见神的吩咐如何通过受托工人和甘心百姓成为现实。

The interpretation begins with context: Exodus 36 follows the offerings and call of chapter 35 and implements the pattern revealed in chapters 25-31. The point is not uncontrolled symbolism but obedience embodied through workers and gifts.

灵修应用路径 / Devotional Application

灵修应用不跳过原意，而是用“桥梁原则”连接：神赐智慧给手、唤醒甘心奉献、也设立圣洁界限。今天可落实为专业中的忠心、慷慨中的节制、团队中的连接，以及关系中的健康边界。

Devotional use does not bypass meaning. It bridges from the text: God gives wisdom to hands, awakens willing generosity, and establishes holy boundaries. Today this becomes faithful skill, restrained generosity, connected teamwork, and healthy limits.

落地指南 / Practical Guide

观察 / Observe:
找出重复词，如“智慧”“甘心”“够用有余”“做”。Notice repeated words such as wisdom, willing, enough, and made.

解释 / Interpret:
把每段放在会幕建造命令与神同住主题中理解。Read each unit within the tabernacle pattern and God’s dwelling theme.

回应 / Respond:
选择一个具体领域，把技能、资源或界限重新交托给神。Choose one concrete area to entrust skill, resources, or boundaries to God.
