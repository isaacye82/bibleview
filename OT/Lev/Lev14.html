利未记 14章 Leviticus Chapter 14

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

从营外到归回：洁净、恢复与住处更新

From Outside the Camp to Return: Cleansing, Restoration, and Renewed Dwelling

利未记 14 章接续第 13 章的辨察条例，说明当人或房屋从灾病中得恢复时，如何被正式宣告洁净。祭司走到营外察看，使用活水、鸟血、香柏木、朱红线与牛膝草；第八日再以祭物、血和油标记耳、手、脚，使人重新进入敬拜与群体生活。后半章转向迦南地的房屋，显示圣洁也关乎家庭空间、隐藏腐坏和必要修复。

Leviticus 14 follows the discernment laws of chapter 13 and explains how a healed person or house is formally declared clean. The priest goes outside the camp, uses fresh water, bird blood, cedarwood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop, and on the eighth day marks ear, hand, and foot with blood and oil. The second half turns to houses in Canaan, showing that holiness reaches domestic space, hidden decay, and needed repair.

神学概念卡片组合

Key concepts for reading Leviticus 14 faithfully

得洁净的日子

Day of Cleansing

yom tahorato

患病者得洁净后，不是自己直接回营，而是由祭司到营外察看并启动恢复礼。This day emphasizes restoration by priestly confirmation, not self-declared acceptance.

香柏木、朱红线、牛膝草

Cedar, Scarlet, Hyssop

erez / sheni / ezov

这些材料连同活水和鸟血组成洁净记号，显示污秽需要被洗净，也需要生命重新进入群体。The ritual materials picture cleansing, life, and return to covenant fellowship.

血与油

Blood and Oil

dam / shemen

血抹在右耳、右手、右脚的大拇指上，油也抹在同处，表明整个人重新归入圣洁的听从、行动与行走。Blood and oil mark restored hearing, serving, and walking before God.

房屋灾病

House Affliction

nega bayit

圣洁不仅关乎个人身体，也进入家庭空间、墙壁与居住秩序。Holiness reaches domestic life; even a house must be examined, repaired, or removed when corruption spreads.

研读视图 / Study View

利未记 14 章经文双轨研读

本章按恢复流程展开：营外察看、七日洗净、第八日献祭、贫穷者的祭物安排，以及房屋灾病的查验、修复或拆毁。每个单元都从经文含义走向今日的群体接纳、家庭更新和实际顺服。

This chapter follows a movement of restoration: examination outside the camp, seven-day washing, eighth-day offerings, provision for the poor, and the examination, repair, or demolition of afflicted houses. Each unit moves from textual meaning to communal welcome, household renewal, and practical obedience today.

The Cleansed Person Examined Outside the Camp

得洁净者在营外受察看 (14:1-9)

14:1-9

耶和华晓谕摩西说：长大麻风得洁净的日子，其例乃是这样：要带他去见祭司；祭司要出到营外察看。若见他的大麻风痊愈了，就吩咐人为那求洁净的拿两只洁净的活鸟、香柏木、朱红色线和牛膝草。祭司要吩咐用瓦器盛活水，把一只鸟宰在上面；又把活鸟和材料蘸在血水中，向求洁净的人洒七次，宣告他洁净。那人要洗衣、剃毛、用水洗身，然后可以进营，只是要在自己的帐棚外居住七天。

The LORD told Moses that on the day of cleansing the person is brought to the priest, and the priest goes outside the camp to examine him. If the disease is healed, two clean living birds, cedarwood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop are used with fresh water; one bird is killed, the living bird is dipped and released, and the person is sprinkled seven times, declared clean, washes, shaves, bathes, and returns to the camp while waiting outside his tent seven days.

经文含义 Meaning

恢复从察看开始

这一段延续第 13 章的营外状态，但重心从隔离转向恢复。祭司主动出到营外察看，显示圣洁群体不能假装污秽不存在，也不能让已得痊愈的人永远留在外面。两只鸟、活水、血与释放的活鸟构成强烈画面：洁净既处理死亡的污染，也指向生命重新得自由。

The passage turns from exclusion to restoration. The priest goes outside the camp, showing that holiness requires truthful examination and mercy toward the healed. The birds, fresh water, blood, and released life create a ritual picture of cleansing from death and reentry into freedom.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要把恢复的人留在营外

今天的群体也需要学会辨认真实的恢复。一个曾经失败、受伤或长期被标签化的人，不应只被过去定义。桥梁原则是：真理需要察看，恩典也需要接回。我们在家庭、教会和职场中，可以既不轻率忽略问题，也不拒绝给悔改和更新的人重新站立的位置。

Modern communities also need to recognize real restoration. A person with a painful past should not be permanently defined by it. The bridge principle is that truth examines, and grace receives back. In family, church, and work, we can avoid denial while making room for repentant and renewed people to stand again.

Eighth-Day Offerings and Restored Belonging

第八日的祭物与重新归属 (14:10-20)

14:10-20

第八天，他要取两只没有残疾的公羊羔、一只没有残疾的一岁母羊羔，并调油的细面为素祭，又取一罗革油。祭司要把赎愆祭的羊羔和油一同作摇祭，宰赎愆祭牲，把血抹在求洁净者的右耳垂、右手大拇指和右脚大拇指上；又把油弹在耶和华面前，抹在同处，并倒在他的头上。随后献赎罪祭、燔祭和素祭，为他赎罪，他就洁净了。

On the eighth day the person brings unblemished lambs, a ewe lamb, grain offering mixed with oil, and a log of oil. The priest presents the guilt offering and oil, applies blood to the right ear, right thumb, and right big toe of the one being cleansed, then applies oil to the same places and pours oil on his head. Sin offering, burnt offering, and grain offering follow, and the person is clean.

经文含义 Meaning

耳、手、脚重新归给神

赎愆祭、赎罪祭、燔祭和素祭共同表明恢复不是单一动作，而是重新进入敬拜秩序。血与油抹在耳、手、脚，类似祭司承接圣职时的标记，说明得洁净者不只是“问题消失”，而是整个人被重新安置在听命、做工、行路都属神的生命中。

The guilt, sin, burnt, and grain offerings show that restoration is a return to worshiping order. Blood and oil on ear, hand, and foot echo consecration imagery, indicating that the cleansed person is not merely problem-free but restored to hear, serve, and walk before God.

灵修应用 Devotional

恢复不是只回到原点

神的恢复常常不只是让生活“恢复正常”，而是让我们重新学习听什么、做什么、往哪里走。经历病痛、失败或关系破裂之后，真正的更新会触及注意力、工作方式和人生方向。今天可以问：我的耳、手、脚是否重新被神的真理和爱引导？

God’s restoration is often more than returning to normal; it retrains what we hear, what we do, and where we walk. After illness, failure, or relational fracture, renewal touches attention, work, and direction. We may ask whether our ears, hands, and feet are being guided again by God’s truth and love.

Provision for the Poor

贫穷者也可亲近 (14:21-32)

14:21-32

他若贫穷，力量不够，就要取一只公羊羔作赎愆祭，可以摇一摇，为他赎罪；也取调油的细面和一罗革油，又照他的力量取两只斑鸠或雏鸽，一只作赎罪祭，一只作燔祭。祭司照前例把血与油抹在求洁净者身上，为他在耶和华面前赎罪。这是那有大麻风灾病的人，不能将关乎得洁净之物预备够数的条例。

If the person is poor and cannot afford the full offering, he brings one lamb for a guilt offering, grain offering with oil, a log of oil, and two turtledoves or pigeons according to his means. The priest applies blood and oil in the same way and makes atonement before the LORD. This is the law for the one whose means are insufficient for his cleansing.

经文含义 Meaning

恩典按能力开路

经文没有降低洁净的严肃性，却为贫穷者预备可承担的祭物。关键的血与油动作仍然保留，说明人归回神面前的道路不是富人的特权。礼仪保持圣洁的秩序，同时显明神顾念人的实际处境。

The text does not make cleansing casual, yet it provides an affordable path for the poor. The essential blood and oil actions remain, showing that return to God is not a privilege of the wealthy. Ritual order is preserved while God’s concern for real circumstances is displayed.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要让资源成为归回的墙

在现代生活中，金钱、时间、教育和社会资源常会变成属灵参与的门槛。利未记 14 章提醒我们，群体制度若真反映神的心意，就应当让软弱和贫穷的人也能认真地走恢复之路。我们可以在陪伴、费用、时间安排和语言表达上，为人拆除不必要的障碍。

Today money, time, education, and social resources can become barriers to spiritual participation. Leviticus 14 reminds us that practices reflecting God’s heart should make serious restoration accessible to the weak and poor. We can remove needless obstacles through companionship, cost, scheduling, and language.

Examination of Afflicted Houses in Canaan

迦南房屋灾病的察看 (14:33-42)

14:33-42

耶和华晓谕摩西、亚伦说：你们到了我赐给你们为业的迦南地，我若使你们所得为业之地的房屋中有大麻风的灾病，房主就要告诉祭司说：“据我看，房屋中似乎有灾病。”祭司要吩咐腾空房子，再进去察看；若墙上有发绿或发红的凹斑，现象洼于墙，就把房子关锁七天。若灾病发散，就挖出有灾病的石头，扔在城外不洁净之处，又刮房内四围，另换石头和灰泥。

When Israel enters Canaan and a defiling disease appears in a house, the owner tells the priest that something like a disease is in the house. The priest has the house emptied before examination. If greenish or reddish depressions appear in the walls, the house is shut seven days. If it spreads, affected stones are removed outside the city, the house is scraped, and new stones and plaster are put in place.

经文含义 Meaning

圣洁进入居住空间

这一段面向将来的迦南生活，说明应许地不是自动免疫的空间。房主先诚实报告，祭司再察看；家具先搬出，避免不必要损失。若问题扩散，就必须拆除受污染的石头并重建。经文把敬拜与居家环境连在一起：神关心人住在哪里，也关心隐藏的腐坏是否被处理。

This future-oriented section shows that the promised land is not automatically immune from corruption. The owner reports honestly, the priest examines, and possessions are protected before inspection. If the problem spreads, contaminated stones are removed and the house repaired. Worship and domestic space are connected.

灵修应用 Devotional

处理家中的隐藏霉斑

房屋灾病提醒我们，家庭和工作空间也可能积累无人处理的问题：冷漠、怨气、隐瞒、成瘾或不公。桥梁原则是：爱一个家，不是粉刷问题，而是诚实请光进来，拆掉该拆的，补上该补的。越早承认，越可能修复。

House affliction reminds us that homes and workplaces can accumulate untreated problems: coldness, resentment, secrecy, addiction, or injustice. The bridge principle is that loving a house does not mean repainting over damage; it means inviting light, removing what must go, and repairing what can be repaired. Early honesty opens the way to healing.

Demolition, Cleansing, and Summary Instruction

拆毁、洁净与总结条例 (14:43-57)

14:43-57

若挖出石头、刮了房子、墁了以后，灾病又在房子里发现，这就是房内蚕食的大麻风，是不洁净。要拆毁房子，把石头、木头、灰泥都搬到城外不洁净之处。若祭司察看，见灾病没有发散，就要宣告房子洁净，并用两只鸟、香柏木、朱红色线和牛膝草洁净房子。末了说明，这是为各类大麻风灾病、头疥、衣服与房子的灾病，指明何时为洁净，何时为不洁净的条例。

If the disease returns after stones are removed and the house is replastered, it is a persistent defiling disease and the house is unclean. The house must be torn down and its materials carried outside the city to an unclean place. If the disease has not spread, the priest declares the house clean and cleanses it with birds, cedarwood, scarlet yarn, and hyssop. The chapter concludes that these instructions distinguish clean from unclean in skin, garments, and houses.

经文含义 Meaning

有些腐坏必须拆毁

经文区分可修复与必须拆毁的情况。若灾病持续返回，问题不再只是表面修补，而是结构性腐坏。拆毁并非任意严苛，而是保护整个群体的圣洁与健康。总结句把 13-14 章连在一起：神赐下条例，是为了教导人分辨洁净与不洁净，并知道如何恢复。

The text distinguishes what can be repaired from what must be demolished. If the disease keeps returning, the problem is structural rather than cosmetic. Demolition protects the holiness and health of the community. The summary joins chapters 13-14: God gives instruction so His people can discern uncleanness and pursue restoration.

灵修应用 Devotional

勇敢承认不可保留的东西

有时我们想保留熟悉的结构，即使它不断伤害人。利未记 14 章给出的现代提醒是：有些习惯、关系模式、制度漏洞或隐秘妥协，不能只靠重新包装。真正的洁净可能包括痛苦的拆除，好让新的顺服、诚实与安全重新建立。

Sometimes we want to preserve familiar structures even when they keep harming people. Leviticus 14 reminds us that some habits, relational patterns, institutional gaps, or secret compromises cannot be fixed by rebranding. True cleansing may require painful removal so new obedience, honesty, and safety can be rebuilt.

方法论总结 / Methodology

如何读利未记 14 章

释经路径：按礼仪流程读 / Exegetical Path

先注意本章与第 13 章相连：第 13 章辨认不洁净，第 14 章处理得洁净后的恢复。经文中的鸟、活水、血、油和祭物不是任意象征，而是在会幕敬拜体系中宣告人重新归入神的同在与群体秩序。

Read the chapter as the continuation of Leviticus 13: diagnosis is followed by restoration. Birds, fresh water, blood, oil, and offerings function within Israel’s worship system to declare a person restored to God’s presence and communal order.

灵修路径：从恢复到更新 / Devotional Path

应用时不要把古代洁净条例简化成现代卫生规则，也不要把不洁净者看作道德低等。更稳妥的桥梁是：神关心真实问题被察看，也关心人、家庭和群体被带回健康秩序。今日的回应包括诚实检查、愿意修复、接纳已恢复的人，以及拆除持续伤害人的结构。

For application, do not reduce the laws to modern hygiene or treat the unclean as morally inferior. A sound bridge is that God cares for truthful examination and for people, homes, and communities being restored to healthy order. Today’s response includes honest inspection, repair, welcome, and removal of what keeps harming others.
