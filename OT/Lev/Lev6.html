利未记 6章 Leviticus Chapter 6

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

赔偿、祭坛与不熄的火

Restitution, the Altar, and the Unending Fire

利未记第 6 章把圣洁带进两个方向：一方面，得罪邻舍和亏负财物的罪必须在神面前承认，并以归还、加赔和赎愆祭回应；另一方面，祭司每日管理燔祭、素祭和赎罪祭，使祭坛的火常常烧着。经文不把敬拜缩小为仪式，也不把悔改缩小为情绪。神所喜悦的悔改包括对人的修复，也包括在祂面前持续、谨慎、圣洁的服事。

Leviticus 6 brings holiness in two directions. Wrong done to a neighbor through deceit or withheld property must be confessed before God, restored with added compensation, and answered through a guilt offering. At the same time, the priests must tend the burnt offering, grain offering, and sin offering so that the altar fire keeps burning. Worship is not reduced to ritual, and repentance is not reduced to feeling. God-shaped repentance repairs what was damaged and keeps faithful service alive before Him.

The Guilt Offering and Concrete Restitution

赎愆祭与真实赔偿 (6:1-7)

6:1-7

耶和华吩咐摩西：人若在交托、交易、抢夺、欺压或拾得遗失物的事上欺骗邻舍，又起假誓，就犯了罪。他要在献赎愆祭的日子归还所亏负的，并另外加上五分之一交给原主；祭司要为他在耶和华面前赎罪，他就必蒙赦免。

The LORD instructed Moses that if someone deceives a neighbor over a deposit, bargain, robbery, oppression, or lost property, and then swears falsely, he has sinned. He must return what was wrongfully taken, add one fifth to it, give it to the owner, and bring a guilt offering. The priest will make atonement before the LORD, and he will be forgiven.

经文含义 Meaning

得罪邻舍也是得罪神

本段把商业欺诈、侵占财物、假誓和敬拜连在一起。人亏负的是邻舍，但罪首先被说成“干犯耶和华”，因为神的圣洁覆盖日常经济关系。赎愆祭不是替代赔偿，而是与赔偿同行：先承认具体亏欠，再按神所定的方法寻求赦免。

The passage links economic deceit, false oaths, restitution, and worship. The neighbor suffers the loss, yet the offense is also against the LORD because daily dealings stand under His holiness. The guilt offering does not replace restitution; it accompanies repaired responsibility.

灵修应用 Devotional

悔改要走到被伤害的人那里

今天的应用不只是“我心里觉得抱歉”。若我们在金钱、承诺、工作成果、信用或沟通中亏负人，桥梁原则是：先面对事实，再尽力修复。属灵成熟常常表现为愿意发信息道歉、补偿损失、澄清误导、归还不属于自己的利益。神的赦免邀请我们诚实，也释放我们不再用遮掩维持形象。

Application begins beyond private regret. When money, promises, work, credit, or communication have wronged someone, repentance faces facts and seeks repair. Spiritual maturity may mean apologizing, compensating loss, correcting a misleading claim, or returning an unfair gain. God's forgiveness invites honesty and frees us from image management.

The Burnt Offering: The Fire Must Not Go Out

燔祭的条例：火不可熄灭 (6:8-13)

6:8-13

耶和华吩咐摩西晓谕亚伦和他的子孙：燔祭要整夜在坛上，坛上的火要常常烧着。祭司要穿细麻布衣服，收起坛上的灰，放在坛旁；再换衣服，把灰拿到营外洁净之处。坛上的火要不断添柴，不可熄灭。

The LORD told Moses to command Aaron and his sons concerning the burnt offering. It remains on the altar through the night, and the altar fire must be kept burning. The priest wears linen garments, removes the ashes, changes clothes, carries the ashes to a clean place outside the camp, and adds wood so the fire does not go out.

经文含义 Meaning

持续敬拜中的细节圣洁

燔祭强调全然献上，而本段关注祭司如何维持祭坛。灰烬处理、衣服更换、添柴守火都显示圣洁不是抽象概念，而是有秩序的服事。火“不可熄灭”说明以色列每日都活在神面前，敬拜不是偶发事件，而是持续关系。

The burnt offering expresses total dedication, but this unit focuses on priestly maintenance. Ash removal, garment changes, and adding wood show holiness embodied in ordered service. The unending fire portrays Israel living continually before God, not meeting Him only in occasional moments.

灵修应用 Devotional

信心需要每日添柴

现代人很容易把属灵生命寄托在一次退修会、一次感动或一次危机祷告上。但本段提醒我们，火需要每日被看顾。桥梁原则是：稳定的小顺服保护深层关系。固定祷告、诚实认罪、规律安息、在忙碌中守住敬拜时间，都是今日“添柴”的方式。

It is easy to depend on rare moments of inspiration, retreats, or crisis prayers. This passage reminds us that fire is tended daily. The bridge principle is that steady small obedience protects deep relationship: prayer, confession, rest, and guarded worship time are ways of adding wood today.

The Grain Offering: Memorial Portion and Priestly Share

素祭的条例：记念与祭司份额 (6:14-18)

6:14-18

素祭要在耶和华面前献上。祭司从细面、油和乳香中取出一把，烧在坛上作为记念，余下的要归亚伦和他的子孙，在圣处吃，不可有酵；这是从献给耶和华火祭中赐给他们的份，是至圣的。

The grain offering is presented before the LORD. The priest takes a handful of fine flour, oil, and incense and burns it on the altar as a memorial portion. What remains belongs to Aaron and his sons; it is eaten in a holy place without leaven. It is their portion from the LORD's food offerings and is most holy.

经文含义 Meaning

感恩献上也在圣洁秩序中

素祭多与感恩、日常供应和献上劳作果实有关。本段说明祭物既向神献上记念部分，也成为祭司在圣所中的供应。无酵和圣处强调即使是食物分配，也属于神圣秩序。神看重人的劳作，也使服事者在祂的安排中得供养。

The grain offering is associated with gratitude, provision, and the fruit of labor. A memorial portion is offered to God, while the remainder sustains the priests in a holy setting. The unleavened bread and holy place show that even provision and distribution belong to God's sacred order.

灵修应用 Devotional

把普通劳作带到神面前

我们的工作、薪水、家务和专业能力似乎很普通，但素祭提醒我们，日常产出也可以成为敬拜。桥梁原则是：不把“属灵”与“日常”切开。认真工作、公平分配、支持服事、用收入表达感恩，都是把普通生活带到神面前的方式。

Work, income, household labor, and professional skills can feel ordinary, yet the grain offering teaches that daily output can become worship. The bridge principle is not to split the spiritual from the ordinary. Honest work, fair distribution, support for ministry, and grateful giving bring ordinary life before God.

The Priestly Grain Offering at Consecration

祭司承接职分时的素祭 (6:19-23)

6:19-23

亚伦和他的子孙受膏的日子，要献细面十分之一伊法为常献的素祭，早晨一半，晚上一半，用油调和，在煎盘上作成；祭司受膏接续时也要如此献。这素祭要全然烧给耶和华，不可吃。

On the day Aaron and his sons are anointed, they must offer a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half in the morning and half in the evening, mixed with oil and prepared on a griddle. The anointed priest who succeeds him must do the same. This offering is wholly burned to the LORD and not eaten.

经文含义 Meaning

带领者先把自己献上

前一段素祭有祭司可吃的份，但这里祭司自己的素祭要全然烧尽。受膏服事的人不能把职分当作取得资源的管道；他首先站在神面前，把自己和每日服事归给神。早晚献上也呼应持续、规律的委身。

The previous grain offering provided a priestly portion, but the priest's own offering is wholly burned. Ministry is not first a channel for receiving benefits; the servant stands before God and offers himself and his daily work to Him. Morning and evening underline regular dedication.

灵修应用 Devotional

职位越高，越需要先奉献自己

无论在教会、家庭还是职场，带领都容易变成控制、表现或索取。本段给出的桥梁原则是：影响力必须先被奉献净化。父母、主管、小组长、老师和服事者都需要问：我是在利用角色满足自己，还是先把角色交给神，成为祝福他人的管道？

Leadership in church, family, or work can become control, performance, or entitlement. The bridge principle is that influence must first be offered to God. Parents, managers, group leaders, teachers, and servants can ask whether they use the role for themselves or entrust it to God for others' good.

The Sin Offering and Holy Handling

赎罪祭的圣洁处理 (6:24-30)

6:24-30

赎罪祭要在宰燔祭牲的地方宰在耶和华面前，是至圣的。为罪献祭的祭司要在圣处吃这祭物。凡摸这肉的要成为圣；若血弹在衣服上，要在圣处洗净。煮祭物的瓦器要打碎，铜器要擦磨冲洗；但血带进会幕为赎罪的祭，肉不可吃，必须用火焚烧。

The sin offering is slaughtered before the LORD where the burnt offering is slaughtered; it is most holy. The priest who offers it eats it in a holy place. Whatever touches the flesh becomes holy; blood on a garment must be washed in a holy place. An earthen vessel used for boiling is broken, a bronze vessel is scoured and rinsed, and any offering whose blood is brought into the tent for atonement must be burned, not eaten.

经文含义 Meaning

赦罪之物不可随便处理

经文详细规定肉、血、器皿和地点，显示赎罪祭承载圣洁严肃性。血不是普通污渍，祭肉不是普通食物，器皿也不能随意再用。神借这些细节教导以色列：罪的处理需要按祂的方法，赦免是恩典，却绝不廉价。

The detailed rules for flesh, blood, vessels, and location reveal the seriousness of the sin offering. Blood is not an ordinary stain, the meat is not ordinary food, and vessels are not casually reused. Israel learns that sin must be handled by God's appointed means; forgiveness is gracious but never cheap.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要把恩典变成轻率

我们相信神赦免人，但本段防止我们把赦免当作随口一句“没事”。桥梁原则是：恩典越真实，越带来敬畏。面对反复的罪、破裂的关系或良心的迟钝，我们需要认真处理，而不是用属灵话语淡化后果。来到神面前的人可以得洁净，也要学习珍惜洁净。

We trust that God forgives, but this passage keeps forgiveness from becoming a casual phrase. The bridge principle is that real grace deepens reverence. Repeated sin, broken relationships, and a dull conscience need serious attention, not spiritual language that minimizes consequences. Those cleansed by God learn to treasure cleansing.

方法论 / Methodology

从条例到生命：两条阅读路径

From regulation to life: two faithful reading paths

正统解经原则

Contextual exegesis

先确认利未记处在会幕敬拜和祭司制度的上下文中，观察赎愆祭、燔祭、素祭、赎罪祭各自的功能。不要把古代祭礼直接等同为现代动作，而要先问：这条条例怎样显明神的圣洁、罪的真实、赦免的途径和群体关系的修复？

Read Leviticus within tabernacle worship and priestly service. Identify each offering's function before applying it. Ask how the regulation reveals God's holiness, the reality of sin, appointed forgiveness, and repaired community life.

灵修应用路径

Devotional bridge

再用桥梁原则进入今日生活：赔偿对应真实修复，坛火对应每日敬拜，祭司条例对应服事中的警醒，至圣祭物对应对恩典的敬畏。应用时避免空泛化，要落到金钱、关系、工作诚信、时间安排和服事动机等具体层面。

Then bridge into present life: restitution becomes repair, altar fire becomes daily worship, priestly rules become attentive service, and most holy offerings become reverent gratitude. Apply concretely to money, relationships, work integrity, time, and motives in service.

观察 / Observe

列出经文重复的词：归还、五分之一、火、圣处、至圣。重复词帮助我们抓住本章重点。

Trace repeated words: return, one fifth, fire, holy place, most holy.

解释 / Interpret

把每条条例放在会幕和祭司职责中理解，避免脱离上下文作随意象征化。

Interpret each rule within tabernacle worship and priestly responsibility.

实践 / Practice

选择一个具体亏欠去修复，或选择一个每日敬拜节奏去守住，让经文进入生活。

Repair one concrete wrong or guard one daily rhythm of worship.
