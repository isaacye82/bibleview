利未记 7章 Leviticus Chapter 7

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

圣洁的餐桌：赎罪、感谢与相交的边界

A Holy Table: Atonement, Thanksgiving, and Boundaries for Fellowship

利未记第 7 章完成前七章献祭条例的收束。赎愆祭说明亏欠必须在至圣的秩序中处理；平安祭展开感谢、还愿和甘心献的不同场景；洁净、脂油与血的禁令则提醒敬拜者，亲近神不是随意享用圣物，而是在神的圣洁中领受相交。全章把“祭坛上的归神”和“餐桌上的分享”连在一起，使敬拜成为赦免、感恩、责任和群体供应的综合生活。

Leviticus 7 closes the first major block of sacrificial instruction. The guilt offering handles liability in a most holy order; the peace offering unfolds thanksgiving, vows, and freewill gifts; the commands about cleanness, fat, and blood remind worshipers that fellowship with God is never casual consumption of holy things. The chapter joins altar and table, making worship a life of forgiveness, gratitude, responsibility, and shared provision.

神学概念卡片组合

Key concepts for reading Leviticus 7 faithfully

至圣的赎愆祭

The Most Holy Guilt Offering

asham qodesh qadashim

赎愆祭与赎罪祭同属“至圣”，强调罪不只是内在亏欠，也会造成真实损害，需要按神所定的方式处理、赔补与归正。
The guilt offering is “most holy,” showing that guilt includes real damage and must be handled, repaired, and reordered in God’s way.

感谢与还愿

Thanksgiving and Vows

todah / neder

平安祭可以回应神的拯救，也可以表达所许的愿。敬拜不是抽象情绪，而是在神面前承认恩典、守住承诺、与人分享。
Peace offerings respond to deliverance and vows; worship acknowledges grace, keeps promises, and shares fellowship before God.

洁净的相交

Clean Fellowship

tahor / tame

平安祭强调同桌共享，却不是无边界的共享。人与神相交必须尊重圣洁，罪污与不洁不可被当作小事带进敬拜。
Shared fellowship is not boundaryless; communion with God honors holiness and refuses to treat impurity as a small matter.

脂油与血归神

Fat and Blood Belong to God

chelev / dam

脂油象征最肥美的部分，血代表生命。以色列不可随意取用，学习把生命与最好的部分都承认属于耶和华。
Fat represents the richest portion and blood represents life; Israel learns to confess that life and the best belong to the LORD.

7:1-10

“赎愆祭的条例乃是如此：这祭是至圣的……赎罪祭怎样，赎愆祭也是怎样，两个祭是一个条例。祭司要用以赎罪的，就得这祭物。”

“This is the law of the guilt offering. It is most holy... As is the sin offering, so is the guilt offering; there is one law for them. The priest who makes atonement with it shall have it.”

经文含义 Meaning

亏欠必须被带到圣洁中处理 / Guilt Brought into Holiness

本段把赎愆祭称为“至圣”，并说明宰杀地点、洒血、焚烧脂油和祭司所得。赎愆祭处理的不是模糊的内疚感，而是人在神和邻舍面前造成的亏欠。经文把赎愆祭与赎罪祭并列，说明赦免不是轻率抹去责任，而是在祭坛前承认罪的真实重量。

The guilt offering is called “most holy” and follows ordered actions of slaughter, blood, fat, and priestly portions. It addresses concrete liability before God and neighbor. By linking it with the sin offering, the text shows that forgiveness does not trivialize responsibility; guilt is brought into the holy order God provides.

灵修应用 Devotional

面对责任而不是逃离责任 / Facing Responsibility

当我们伤害人、失信或造成损失时，属灵成熟不是只说“我心里很难过”，而是愿意在神面前承认、修复并承担。今天的职场、家庭和教会关系中，真实悔改常常从一句清楚的道歉、一次实际的补偿、一个改变习惯的决定开始。

When we harm others, fail commitments, or create loss, maturity is more than saying we feel bad. We come before God, admit what happened, repair what we can, and accept responsibility. In work, family, and church life, repentance often begins with a clear apology, concrete restitution, and a changed pattern.

7:11-18

“人献与耶和华平安祭的条例乃是这样：他若为感谢献上……若所献的是为还愿，或是甘心献的，必在献祭的日子吃。”

“This is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings that one may offer to the LORD. If he offers it for a thanksgiving... if the sacrifice is a vow offering or a freewill offering, it shall be eaten on the day that he offers his sacrifice.”

经文含义 Meaning

平安祭有记忆，也有时限 / Fellowship with Memory and Boundaries

平安祭包括感谢、还愿和甘心献。感谢祭配有无酵饼与有酵饼，并要求在当天吃完；还愿或甘心祭可到第二天，第三天则不可再吃。这些时限教导敬拜者：与神相交是恩典的筵席，也必须按神的节奏和界限进行。

Peace offerings include thanksgiving, vows, and freewill offerings. The thanksgiving offering includes unleavened and leavened bread and must be eaten the same day; vow or freewill offerings may extend to the second day, but not the third. Fellowship with God is a gracious meal ordered by God’s own boundaries.

灵修应用 Devotional

把恩典及时说出来 / Giving Thanks in Time

感谢祭提醒我们不要把神的恩典存成沉默的记忆。一次被扶持、一次危机中得帮助、一次关系被修复，都值得及时向神献上感谢，也值得与身边人分享。延迟太久，感恩可能变成习惯性的麻木；及时感恩能重新训练我们的眼光。

The thanksgiving offering teaches us not to store grace as silent memory. Support in weakness, help in crisis, or a repaired relationship deserves timely thanks to God and honest sharing with others. Delayed gratitude can become numbness; timely gratitude retrains our sight.

7:19-21

“挨了污秽物的肉就不可吃，要用火焚烧……只是献与耶和华平安祭的肉，人若不洁净而吃了，这人必从民中剪除。”

“Flesh that touches any unclean thing shall not be eaten; it shall be burned up with fire... if anyone who is unclean eats flesh from the sacrifice of the LORD’s peace offerings, that person shall be cut off from his people.”

经文含义 Meaning

相交不是降低圣洁 / Fellowship Does Not Lower Holiness

平安祭是共享的祭，但共享不取消洁净要求。经文区分普通可吃的肉与献给耶和华的祭肉，强调越靠近圣物，越需要按圣洁处理。不洁不是被轻描淡写的礼仪细节，而是提醒百姓：神的同在不能被人任意支配。

The peace offering is shared, yet sharing does not remove the demand for cleanness. The passage distinguishes ordinary food from sacrificial meat offered to the LORD. The nearer one comes to holy things, the more seriously holiness must be honored; God’s presence is not at human disposal.

灵修应用 Devotional

亲近神也要诚实处理污秽 / Honest Nearness to God

今天我们不是靠礼仪洁净来到神面前，却仍不可把隐藏的罪、操控人的习惯或不诚实的生活包装成敬虔。真正的亲近神，会使我们愿意让祂光照生活中不合宜的部分。灵修不是麻醉良心，而是让恩典带我们进入真实。

We do not approach God through ritual cleanness today, yet we must not disguise hidden sin, manipulation, or dishonesty as devotion. True nearness to God welcomes His light into disordered parts of life. Devotion does not numb the conscience; grace brings us into truth.

7:22-27

“牛的脂油、绵羊的脂油、山羊的脂油，你们都不可吃……无论是谁吃血，那人必从民中剪除。”

“You shall eat no fat, of ox or sheep or goat... Whoever eats any blood, that person shall be cut off from his people.”

经文含义 Meaning

生命和最美之分归耶和华 / Life and the Best Belong to the LORD

脂油在祭坛上焚烧，血则严禁食用。脂油代表祭牲最肥美、最有价值的部分；血代表生命。禁令不是任意限制饮食，而是在日常生活中刻下信仰记号：生命不归人掌控，最好的也不该被人据为己有。

Fat is burned on the altar, and blood is strictly forbidden as food. Fat represents the richest portion of the animal, while blood represents life. These commands are not arbitrary diet rules; they mark daily life with the confession that life is God’s, and the best portion belongs to Him.

灵修应用 Devotional

把最好的和生命主权交还给神 / Returning the Best to God

我们容易把剩余的时间、精力和金钱给神，却把最好的留给自己的野心和安全感。利未记第 7 章邀请我们重新排序：生命不是自我拥有的资产，而是受托管理的恩典。今天可以从时间表、消费选择和工作动机上问：什么正在占据本该归给神的位置？

We often give God leftover time, energy, and money while reserving the best for ambition or security. Leviticus 7 invites reordered trust: life is not a self-owned asset but a grace entrusted to us. We can examine our calendar, spending, and work motives to ask what has taken God’s place.

7:28-34

“献平安祭给耶和华的，要从平安祭中取些来奉给耶和华……摇的胸，举的腿，归亚伦祭司和他子孙。”

“Whoever offers the sacrifice of his peace offerings to the LORD shall bring his offering to the LORD... the breast that is waved and the thigh that is contributed shall be for Aaron the priest and his sons.”

经文含义 Meaning

敬拜包含共同承担 / Worship Includes Shared Provision

献祭者亲手把脂油和胸带来，脂油归祭坛，胸作摇祭，右腿作举祭，成为祭司的分。这里显出敬拜的群体性：献祭者、祭司和祭坛都在神所定的秩序中各有位置。祭司所得不是私人索取，而是神为服事者设立的供应。

The worshiper personally brings the fat and breast; the fat goes to the altar, the breast is waved, and the right thigh is contributed for the priests. Worship is communal and ordered: worshiper, altar, and priest each have a place. Priestly provision is not private exploitation but God’s arrangement for those who serve.

灵修应用 Devotional

支持服事，也学习分享 / Supporting Service and Sharing Grace

敬拜从来不只是个人获得安慰，也包含对群体责任的承担。今天这可以表现为忠心支持教会服事、尊重承担牧养与教导的人，也愿意把所得与有需要者分享。被神恩待的人，不把平安私有化，而让平安流向群体。

Worship is never merely private comfort; it includes responsibility for the community. Today this may mean faithfully supporting church ministry, honoring those who shepherd and teach, and sharing resources with people in need. Those who receive peace from God do not privatize it; they let peace flow outward.

7:35-38

“这是亚伦受膏的分和他子孙受膏的分……这是燔祭、素祭、赎罪祭、赎愆祭和平安祭的条例。”

“This is the portion of Aaron and of his sons from the LORD’s food offerings... This is the law of the burnt offering, of the grain offering, of the sin offering, of the guilt offering, and of the peace offering.”

经文含义 Meaning

西奈山下的敬拜秩序 / Worship Ordered at Sinai

结尾总结第 1 至 7 章的各类献祭条例，并把它们定位在西奈山下神向以色列吩咐的历史时刻。献祭制度不是人类宗教想象的集合，而是立约之神教导祂百姓如何回应祂的圣洁、赦免和同在。

The conclusion gathers the offerings of chapters 1-7 and locates them at Sinai, where the LORD commanded Israel. The sacrificial system is not a collection of human religious inventions; it is covenant instruction for responding to God’s holiness, forgiveness, and presence.

灵修应用 Devotional

让敬拜有根有序 / Rooted and Ordered Worship

真实敬拜既有心，也有形；既有感动，也有顺服。利未记提醒我们，不要只按个人喜好定义亲近神的方式。稳定读经、诚实认罪、感恩奉献、群体敬拜和生活圣洁，都是让信仰从情绪变成道路的具体秩序。

True worship has both heart and form, both affection and obedience. Leviticus warns us not to define nearness to God by preference alone. Steady Scripture reading, honest confession, grateful giving, gathered worship, and holy living turn faith from passing emotion into a walked path.

方法论 / Methodology

从祭坛条例到今日敬拜

From altar instruction to faithful worship today

正统解经路径 / Sound Exegesis

先按利未记自身的语境阅读：第 7 章总结并补充前面各祭，重点不是现代读者任意寻找象征，而是观察祭物、祭司、献祭者、洁净、脂油和血在约中承担的功能。这样能避免把经文简化成道德寓言。

Read first in Leviticus’ own context. Chapter 7 completes and supplements the offerings, focusing on the roles of sacrifice, priest, worshiper, cleanness, fat, and blood within covenant worship. This guards against reducing the text to moral allegory.

灵修应用路径 / Devotional Application

再用桥梁原则连接今日生活：赎愆祭引导我们承担亏欠，平安祭训练及时感恩，洁净条例提醒真实亲近神，脂油与血的禁令呼召我们把生命主权和最好的部分交还给神。应用必须从经文原意生长出来。

Then bridge the text into life today: the guilt offering teaches responsibility, peace offerings train timely gratitude, cleanness calls for honest nearness, and the fat and blood commands summon us to return life and the best to God. Application must grow from the text’s meaning.

实际落地 / Practice

读本章时，可以问四个问题：我是否有需要承认并修复的亏欠？我是否及时感谢神的恩典？我是否把不洁或不诚实带进敬拜？我是否把生命和最好的部分承认为属于主？

As you read this chapter, ask: What liability must I confess and repair? What grace should I thank God for now? What uncleanness or dishonesty am I bringing into worship? Do I acknowledge my life and best portions as belonging to the Lord?
