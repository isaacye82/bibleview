利未记 22章 Leviticus Chapter 22

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

圣物、圣名与蒙悦纳的敬拜

Holy Things, the Holy Name, and Worship That Is Accepted

利未记第 22 章继续说明祭司如何在圣物、洁净、家庭边界和祭物质量上尊神为圣。经文先警戒祭司不可在不洁中亲近圣物，又说明谁可以吃圣物；随后转向以色列人与寄居者所献的祭，强调献给耶和华的要完整、合宜、蒙悦纳。结尾把所有规条归结到一句话：不可亵渎神的圣名，因为祂是使百姓成圣、领他们出埃及、作他们神的耶和华。

Leviticus 22 explains how priests and worshipers honor the LORD in holy food, cleanness, household boundaries, and the quality of offerings. It warns priests not to approach holy things while unclean, clarifies who may eat the sacred food, and then teaches Israel and resident sojourners to bring offerings that are whole and acceptable. The chapter closes by grounding every command in the LORD’s holy name and redeeming identity.

研读视图 / Study View

利未记 22 章逐段研读

A paired reading of holiness, acceptable offerings, and the name of the LORD.

22:1-3

耶和华吩咐摩西告诉亚伦和他子孙，要谨慎对待以色列人分别为圣的圣物，免得亵渎神的圣名；凡不洁而亲近圣物的，要从神面前被剪除。

The LORD tells Moses to warn Aaron and his sons to handle Israel’s holy offerings with care, so they do not profane His holy name; an unclean priest who approaches holy things is cut off from His presence.

经文含义 Meaning

圣职不是免于敬畏

本段开头把焦点放在“圣名”上。祭司被拣选服事圣所，但他们不能把职分当作自动通行证。“亲近”圣物必须与洁净相配，因为圣物属于神，不属于服事者个人。

The opening places the holy name at the center. Priests are chosen to serve, yet office does not remove reverence. Nearness to holy things must be matched by cleanness because the holy gifts belong to God, not to the servant.

灵修应用 Devotional

越亲近，越谦卑

今天人也可能因熟悉属灵语言、岗位或服事流程而失去敬畏。本段提醒我们：越常接触神的事，越要让内心、动机和生活重新被神校准。

Familiarity with ministry, religious words, or responsibility can dull reverence. This passage calls us to let God recalibrate our motives and conduct precisely where we are most familiar with sacred things.

22:4-9

亚伦子孙若患大麻风、漏症，或因死尸、遗精、爬物、不洁之人而不洁，就不可吃圣物，直到洁净。日落以后，洗身得洁，方可吃圣物；不可吃自死或被野兽撕裂的。

If a descendant of Aaron is unclean through disease, bodily discharge, contact with death, unclean creatures, or unclean persons, he must not eat the holy food until cleansed. After washing and sunset he may eat, but he must not eat what dies naturally or is torn by beasts.

经文含义 Meaning

洁净规条保护圣洁秩序

这些条例不是把身体本身看为邪恶，而是在圣所生活中教导“生命、死亡、洁净、边界”的次序。祭司要守神所吩咐的职责，免得因轻忽而担当罪责。

These laws do not treat the body as evil. They teach an ordered awareness of life, death, cleanness, and boundaries in sanctuary life. Priests must keep the LORD’s charge rather than carry guilt through negligence.

灵修应用 Devotional

承认有限，接受恢复

人有软弱、污染和需要暂停的时候。经文给出的不是永久排斥，而是洁净、等候、再恢复参与。现代生活中，诚实承认状态，比强撑属灵形象更健康。

Weakness and contamination require honest pauses. The text gives a path of cleansing, waiting, and restored participation. In modern life, acknowledging our true condition is healthier than performing a spiritual image.

22:10-16

外人不可吃圣物；祭司的寄居者和雇工不可吃。祭司用银子买的人和生在他家的人可以吃。祭司女儿若嫁给外人不可吃；若无子回到父家，可以吃父亲的食物。误吃圣物的，要加上五分之一归给祭司。

No outsider may eat the holy food; a priest’s guest or hired worker may not eat it. A purchased servant or one born in his house may eat. A priest’s daughter married to an outsider may not, but if widowed or divorced without children and returned to her father’s house, she may eat. Accidental misuse requires restitution with an added fifth.

经文含义 Meaning

圣物共享有盟约边界

本段显示圣物不是普通福利，而是与祭司家庭身份和圣所秩序相连。误吃之后要赔还并加五分之一，说明无意之错仍需认真修复。

Holy food is not ordinary provision; it is tied to priestly household identity and sanctuary order. Restitution with an added fifth shows that even unintentional misuse requires careful repair.

灵修应用 Devotional

界限也能保护恩典

现代人常把界限视为冷漠，但健康界限能保护信任、资源和群体秩序。无论在家庭、教会或职场，误用他人托付时，真实悔改需要具体补偿与修复。

Modern people often read boundaries as coldness, yet healthy limits protect trust, resources, and communal order. In family, church, or work, misusing what was entrusted to another calls for concrete repair, not vague regret.

22:17-25

凡以色列人或寄居者献燔祭、还愿祭或甘心祭，要献没有残疾的公牛、绵羊或山羊，才蒙悦纳。瞎眼、折伤、残废、有瘤、疥癣等不可献；从外人手中也不可献这类残缺之物作神的食物。

Israelites and sojourners who present burnt offerings, vows, or freewill offerings must bring an unblemished male from the cattle, sheep, or goats to be accepted. Blind, injured, maimed, diseased, or defective animals must not be offered, even if received from foreigners.

经文含义 Meaning

悦纳关乎完整与尊荣

“蒙悦纳”与“无残疾”反复出现。经文并非追求华丽，而是禁止把明显残缺、不能代表尊荣的祭物带到神面前。敬拜的对象决定献礼的质量。

Acceptance and wholeness are repeated themes. The issue is not luxury but the refusal to bring an obviously defective gift before God. The worth of the One worshiped shapes the quality of the offering.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要把剩余当敬拜

我们不再献动物祭，却仍会把残余时间、残余注意力、残余诚实给神和他人。经文邀请我们问：我献上的，是方便丢出的剩余，还是带着爱与尊重的完整回应？

We no longer bring animal sacrifices, yet we can still offer leftover time, attention, and honesty to God and others. The passage asks whether our response is disposable surplus or a whole-hearted act of love and respect.

22:26-30

牛或绵羊、山羊生下七天要跟着母亲，第八天以后才可作火祭。母牛或母羊不可和它的子同日宰杀。感谢祭要当天吃完，不可留到早晨。

A newborn ox, sheep, or goat remains with its mother seven days; from the eighth day it may be accepted as an offering by fire. A cow or ewe and its young must not be slaughtered on the same day. A thanksgiving sacrifice must be eaten the same day and not left until morning.

经文含义 Meaning

献祭中仍有怜悯与秩序

这些细节显明，敬拜不是任意消耗生命。第八日、母与子不可同日宰杀、感谢祭当天吃完，都把敬拜放在神所定的时间、怜悯和圣洁界限中。

These details show that worship is not careless consumption of life. The eighth day, the protection of mother and young, and the same-day thanksgiving meal place worship within God-given timing, mercy, and holy limits.

灵修应用 Devotional

感恩要及时，也要温柔

感谢祭当天吃完，提醒感恩不是收藏情绪，而是在神面前及时回应、与人分享。同时，经文中的怜悯边界也提醒我们：热心不能成为粗暴的理由。

The same-day thanksgiving meal reminds us that gratitude is not stored sentiment but timely response and shared joy before God. The mercy in these limits also warns us that zeal must never become harshness.

22:31-33

你们要谨守遵行我的诫命；不可亵渎我的圣名，我要在以色列人中被尊为圣。我是叫你们成圣、把你们从埃及地领出来、要作你们神的耶和华。

You shall keep and do My commandments. You shall not profane My holy name, but I will be sanctified among the people of Israel. I am the LORD who sanctifies you, who brought you out of Egypt to be your God.

经文含义 Meaning

规条归结于救赎身份

结尾把全部条例收束到神的身份和救赎行动：祂使百姓成圣，祂把他们从埃及领出来，祂要作他们的神。因此顺服不是赚取关系，而是活出已被救赎的关系。

The conclusion gathers the laws into God’s identity and saving action: He sanctifies His people, brought them out of Egypt, and is their God. Obedience does not earn the relationship; it lives out a redeemed relationship.

灵修应用 Devotional

让神的名在日常被看重

圣名是否被尊为圣，不只发生在礼拜场所，也发生在付款、说话、道歉、处理压力和承担责任的时刻。被救赎的人用日常选择见证：神配得最高的尊重。

God’s name is honored not only in formal worship but also in payments, speech, apologies, pressure, and responsibility. Redeemed people witness through ordinary choices that God is worthy of highest regard.

方法论 / Methodology

从圣所规条到日常敬拜

From sanctuary regulations to daily worship without flattening the text.

正统解经路径 / Exegetical Path

先把本章放在利未记 21-22 章的祭司圣洁语境中阅读，再观察“圣物、洁净、悦纳、圣名”等重复词。经文的首要意思不是一般道德劝勉，而是圣约群体如何在敬拜秩序中承认耶和华为圣。

Read the chapter within Leviticus 21-22, noting repeated terms such as holy things, cleanness, acceptance, and holy name. Its first meaning concerns how the covenant community honors the LORD as holy within ordered worship.

灵修应用路径 / Devotional Path

应用时不把旧约祭物规条直接复制成现代清单，而是透过桥梁原则：神的名配得尊荣，神所托付的不可轻忽，真实敬拜要有完整、诚实和修复关系的行动。

Application should not copy Israel’s sacrificial system into a modern checklist. The bridge principle is that God’s name deserves honor, entrusted holy things must not be treated casually, and true worship includes wholeness, honesty, and relational repair.

落地指南 / Practical Guide

读本章时，可以问三组问题：我是否把神的名当作轻省口号，还是在选择中尊祂为圣？我是否在职责、资源和关系边界上忠心？我献上的时间、注意力和顺服，是完整回应，还是只剩下不能用的部分？

Ask three questions: Do my choices honor God’s name as holy? Am I faithful with responsibilities, resources, and relational boundaries? Is my time, attention, and obedience a whole response or merely what is left over?
