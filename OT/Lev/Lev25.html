利未记 25章 Leviticus Chapter 25

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

安息、归回与释放：土地属于耶和华

Rest, Return, and Release: The Land Belongs to the LORD

利未记第 25 章把圣洁生活带进经济、土地与社会关系。安息年要求土地停止耕种，禧年则在第五十年宣告产业归回、债务松开、被卖为仆的人重新得自由。经文不是鼓励懒惰，而是训练神的百姓承认供应来自耶和华，并防止贫穷、债务和权势变成永久性的枷锁。

Leviticus 25 brings holiness into economics, land, and social relationships. The Sabbath year gives the land rest, while the Jubilee announces return of inheritance, release from debt pressure, and freedom for those sold into service. The chapter trains God’s people to trust His provision and to resist systems where poverty, debt, or power become permanent chains.

本章按安息年、禧年、产业赎回、贫弱扶助与债务性服事分段。每个单元都从上下文经文含义走向今日对时间、金钱、权柄与邻舍责任的实践。

Sabbath, Jubilee, Redemption, and Release

土地安息与人得释放 (25:1-55)

25:1-7

耶和华在西奈山吩咐摩西：以色列人进入所赐之地后，六年耕种修理，第七年地要向耶和华守圣安息；不可撒种，也不可修剪葡萄园。地在安息中自出的，可以作众人和牲畜的食物。

At Sinai the LORD commands Israel that, after entering the land, they may sow and prune for six years, but the seventh year is a Sabbath rest for the land to the LORD. What grows by itself during that rest is food for all, including servants, sojourners, livestock, and animals.

经文含义 Meaning

土地也在神的节奏中

本段把安息原则从每周扩展到土地与农业。经文含义不是否定劳动，而是限制人对土地的绝对占有感：连土地都要在耶和华面前停止被榨取。第七年自出的供所有人使用，显示安息与共享相连。

The Sabbath principle extends from weekly time to agricultural life. The text does not despise work; it limits possessiveness and extraction. Even the land rests before the LORD, and its spontaneous produce is shared widely.

灵修应用 Devotional

停止榨取，学习信靠

现代人也会把时间、身体、关系和资源当作永不停歇的产能。安息年邀请我们问：我是否只用效率衡量一切？信靠神常从有节制地停止开始，让生命重新承认供应者不是自己。

Modern life can treat time, bodies, relationships, and resources as nonstop production. The Sabbath year asks whether efficiency has become our only measure. Trust often begins with restrained stopping, acknowledging that we are not our own provider.

25:8-17

要数七个安息年，共四十九年；七月十日赎罪日吹角，宣告第五十年为圣年，在遍地给居民宣告自由。这年是禧年，各人要归自己的产业、归自己的本家；买卖土地要按离禧年的年数公平计算，不可彼此亏负。

Israel is to count seven Sabbath years, forty-nine years, and on the Day of Atonement sound the trumpet. The fiftieth year is holy, liberty is proclaimed throughout the land, and each person returns to his property and clan. Land transactions are priced by the number of harvest years remaining until Jubilee, so no one defrauds another.

经文含义 Meaning

自由以赎罪日为起点

禧年的自由宣告发生在赎罪日，说明社会更新与人在神面前被洁净相连。土地买卖本质上是收成年数的租让，不是永久转移。经文用制度保护弱者，防止财富累积把别人永远排除在产业之外。

Jubilee liberty begins on the Day of Atonement, linking social renewal with cleansing before God. Land sale functions as a lease of harvest years, not permanent alienation. The system protects vulnerable families from permanent exclusion.

灵修应用 Devotional

让公义进入交易

信仰不只在敬拜场所，也在合同、价格、工资和债务安排里显明。今天的应用不是机械复制古代土地制度，而是在每一次交易中拒绝占便宜，把人的尊严看得比最大利润更重。

Faith appears not only in worship spaces but in contracts, prices, wages, and debt arrangements. The point is not a mechanical copy of ancient land law, but refusing exploitation and valuing human dignity over maximum gain.

25:18-22

你们若遵行律例典章，就可以在地上安然居住。地必结果子，你们必吃饱。若问第七年不种不收吃什么，神应许第六年赐三年的出产，直到第九年仍可吃陈粮。

If Israel obeys the LORD’s statutes and judgments, they will dwell securely and eat the land’s fruit. If they ask what they will eat in the seventh year, the LORD promises such blessing in the sixth year that its produce will sustain them until the ninth year’s harvest arrives.

经文含义 Meaning

顺服面对现实问题

经文没有回避实际疑问：“我们吃什么？”神的回答不是责备发问，而是给出供应的应许。安息年要求真实信心，因为它触及粮食安全；同时神把顺服与安然居住、充足供应连在一起。

The text does not avoid the practical question, “What shall we eat?” God answers with promised provision rather than contempt. Sabbath-year obedience requires real trust because it touches food security and daily survival.

灵修应用 Devotional

把焦虑带到应许面前

许多顺服都卡在“如果我照神的方式做，损失怎么办？”本段允许我们诚实说出焦虑，也呼召我们让神的应许重新训练想象力。在职场和家庭决定中，信心不是无视风险，而是不让风险成为唯一主人。

Many acts of obedience stall at the question, “What if I lose out?” This passage lets us name anxiety while allowing God’s promise to retrain imagination. Faith does not ignore risk; it refuses to let risk become the only lord.

25:23-28

地不可永卖，因为地是我的；你们在我面前是客旅、寄居的。各处所得之地要准人赎回。若弟兄渐渐贫穷卖了产业，至近的亲属可以来赎；若后来自己富足，也可按余年赎回；若不能赎回，就等到禧年归还。

The land must not be sold permanently, for it belongs to the LORD; Israel are sojourners and residents with Him. Every inheritance must allow redemption. A poor brother’s land may be redeemed by a near kinsman, by himself if able, or returned to him in the Jubilee.

经文含义 Meaning

神的所有权限制人的所有权

“地是我的”是本章经济伦理的根基。以色列人的产权真实，却不是绝对；神的所有权要求人给失去产业者留下赎回路径。近亲赎回把家庭共同责任写进经济生活。

“The land is mine” grounds the chapter’s economic ethic. Israel’s property rights are real but not absolute. God’s ownership requires paths of redemption, and kinship responsibility enters economic practice.

灵修应用 Devotional

拥有而不被拥有

我们可以管理房屋、账户和事业，却不能让它们定义全部安全感。寄居者意识帮助人慷慨，也提醒成功者别把优势看成永恒资格。属神的人拥有资源，是为了忠心管理并扶持邻舍。

We may steward homes, accounts, and work, but they must not define our entire security. Living as resident strangers forms generosity and warns the successful not to treat advantage as permanent entitlement.

25:29-34

城内住宅卖出后，一年内可以赎回；若一年内不赎，就归买主为业，不在禧年归还。但无城墙村庄的房屋要看同乡下的田地一样，可以赎回，禧年归还。利未人城邑的房屋永可赎回；他们郊野之地不可卖。

A house in a walled city may be redeemed within one year; if not, it remains with the buyer and does not return at Jubilee. Houses in unwalled villages are treated like fields and return at Jubilee. Levite houses retain redemption rights, and their pasturelands must not be sold.

经文含义 Meaning

按处境保护生计

本段区分城内房屋、乡村房屋与利未人产业。城内房屋更接近市场资产，乡村房屋连着耕地和生计，利未人的城邑则关乎他们在以色列中的职分。律法不是抽象平均，而是按处境保护生活根基。

The law distinguishes urban houses, village homes, and Levitical property. Urban houses function more like market assets; village homes are tied to livelihood; Levitical holdings protect their vocation. Justice is contextual rather than simplistic.

灵修应用 Devotional

看见制度背后的人

公平不只是同样处理每个案例，而是看见不同处境中的真实脆弱。今天面对家庭、教会或公司政策时，我们也需要问：这个规则会保护人的生计，还是只让强者更方便？

Fairness is not always identical treatment; it sees real vulnerability in different contexts. In families, churches, and workplaces, we should ask whether a rule protects livelihood or merely makes life easier for the strong.

25:35-38

你的弟兄若渐渐贫穷，手中缺乏，你就要帮补他，使他与你同住，像外人和寄居的一样。不可向他取利，也不可多要；只要敬畏你的神，使弟兄与你同住。神曾领你们出埃及，把迦南地赐给你们，并要作你们的神。

If your brother becomes poor and cannot maintain himself, you must support him so he may live beside you, like a sojourner or resident. Do not take interest or profit from him; fear your God and let your brother live with you. The LORD brought Israel out of Egypt to give them the land and be their God.

经文含义 Meaning

敬畏神表现为扶持贫弱

这里的命令从土地转向借贷和邻舍扶助。贫穷者不是获利机会，而是需要被扶住的弟兄。经文把不取利与出埃及救赎连在一起：蒙释放的人不可复制压迫。

The commands move from land to lending and neighborly support. The poor are not an opportunity for profit but brothers to be upheld. The Exodus grounds the ethic: those delivered from oppression must not reproduce it.

灵修应用 Devotional

不要从别人的危机获利

人在压力中最容易接受不公平条件。属灵应用很具体：当同事、亲友或弱势者陷入困境时，我是利用他的急迫，还是帮助他重新站稳？敬畏神会改变我们对利息、机会和人情的计算。

People under pressure often accept unfair terms. The application is concrete: when someone is in crisis, do we exploit urgency or help them stand again? Fear of God changes how we calculate interest, opportunity, and obligation.

25:39-46

你的弟兄若贫穷卖身给你，不可叫他像奴仆服事；他要像雇工和寄居者，到禧年与你同住，然后和儿女一同离去，归回本家本业。因为以色列人是神从埃及领出来的仆人，不可严严辖管弟兄。

If an Israelite brother becomes poor and sells himself to you, he must not be treated as a slave but as a hired worker or resident until Jubilee. Then he and his children return to his clan and inheritance, for the Israelites are the LORD’s servants whom He brought out of Egypt. They must not be ruled harshly.

经文含义 Meaning

神的仆人不可被永久占有

本段限制债务性服事，不让以色列弟兄沦为永久奴隶。核心理由是身份：他们已经属于领他们出埃及的神。人的管理权不能越过神对人的主权，也不能用困境抹去弟兄身份。

This section limits debt service so an Israelite brother is not turned into permanent property. The reason is identity: Israel belongs to the God who brought them out of Egypt. Human authority cannot erase brotherhood.

灵修应用 Devotional

权柄必须有边界

无论是老板、父母、领袖还是债主，权柄都容易越界。本段提醒我们：在我影响之下的人不是我的工具。真正敬畏神的权柄，会给人休息、尊严和未来，而不是用依赖关系控制他们。

Employers, parents, leaders, and creditors can all overreach. This passage reminds us that people under our influence are not tools. God-fearing authority gives rest, dignity, and a future instead of controlling dependence.

25:47-55

若寄居的外人富足，以色列弟兄贫穷卖给他或他的家族，卖了以后仍可赎回；弟兄、叔伯、本家近亲，或自己富足时都可赎回。要按禧年前余下年数与雇工工价计算；不可严严辖管他。若无人赎回，到了禧年他和儿女仍要出去，因为以色列人是耶和华的仆人。

If a resident foreigner becomes wealthy and an Israelite brother sells himself to him or his clan, the Israelite retains the right of redemption by relatives or by himself if able. The price is calculated by remaining years until Jubilee, like wages for a hired worker. If not redeemed, he and his children go free at Jubilee, for Israel are the LORD’s servants.

经文含义 Meaning

赎回权跨越复杂社会关系

最后一段处理以色列人与寄居外人之间的债务服事，显示律法面对真实社会复杂性。无论债主是谁，神都保留祂百姓的赎回权。禧年成为最终保障：人的困境不会拥有最后一句话。

The final section addresses debt service under a resident foreigner, showing that the law engages social complexity. Whoever holds the debt, God preserves redemption rights for His people. Jubilee is the final safeguard.

灵修应用 Devotional

困境不是最终身份

人可能因失败、债务、移民处境或职业挫折而觉得自己被定义了。禧年信息把盼望放回中心：在神面前，最后定义我们的不是欠缺，而是祂的归属与释放。教会也应成为帮助人归回尊严的群体。

Failure, debt, migration, or career loss can feel identity-defining. Jubilee restores hope: before God, our lack is not the final word; His ownership and release are. The church should become a community that helps people recover dignity.

方法论 / Methodology

正统解经原则：先听原处境

释经轨道先观察本章在利未记中的位置：圣洁不只是祭坛礼仪，也塑造土地使用、债务安排、商业公平与弱者保护。安息年和禧年属于以色列在应许地中的约民制度，因此今日应用应抓住经文原则，而不是简单复制古代农业法规。

Orthodox exegesis first reads the chapter in its Leviticus context: holiness shapes not only altar worship but land use, debt, commerce, and protection of the vulnerable. Sabbath year and Jubilee belong to Israel’s covenant life in the land, so modern application should carry the principles rather than mechanically copy ancient agrarian law.

桥梁原则 / Bridge Principles

灵修应用路径：进入今日忠心

灵修应用路径把本章原则带到现代处境：承认资源属于神，给工作和土地留下安息，拒绝从他人危机获利，限制权柄越界，并主动为贫弱者保留归回尊严与未来的道路。它与释经轨道不同：释经先问“经文当时说什么”，灵修再问“这真理今天如何塑造我的选择”。

The devotional application path brings the principles into modern life: acknowledge that resources belong to God, make room for rest, refuse to profit from another’s crisis, limit abusive power, and preserve paths for vulnerable people to recover dignity and a future. It differs from exegesis by asking, after the original meaning is heard, how this truth should shape today’s choices.
