利未记 26章 Leviticus Chapter 26

本章核心主题 / Core Theme

圣约道路：顺服得生命，悖逆受管教，悔改蒙记念

The Covenant Path: Life in Obedience, Discipline in Rebellion, Mercy in Return

利未记第 26 章是西奈圣约律例的高潮：神把两条道路摆在百姓面前。听从祂的话，就有雨水、收成、平安、得胜和神同住；若厌弃祂的律例、背弃祂的约，管教会一步步加重，直到土地荒凉、百姓被分散。然而本章最后没有停在咒诅，而是转向认罪、谦卑和神记念所立的约。

Leviticus 26 forms the climax of the Sinai covenant instructions. God sets two paths before His people: listening brings rain, harvest, peace, victory, and divine presence; rejecting His statutes brings escalating discipline, desolation, and exile. Yet the chapter does not end with curse, but with confession, humility, and God remembering His covenant.

研读视图 / Study View

利未记 26 章经文双轨研读

本章按敬拜根基、顺服祝福、悖逆管教、被掳荒凉与认罪归回分段。每个单元都从上下文经文含义走向今日的敬拜、工作、压力、界限与悔改实践。

This study follows worship, blessing, discipline, desolation, and confession, moving from contextual meaning to present practice in worship, work, pressure, limits, and repentance.

Blessing, Warning, Confession, and Covenant Mercy

圣约祝福、管教与记念 (26:1-46)

26:1-2

耶和华禁止百姓制造偶像、立雕像或柱像，也不可在地上安设石像跪拜；因为祂是耶和华他们的神。百姓要守安息日，敬畏祂的圣所。

The LORD forbids idols, carved images, sacred pillars, and stones set up for worship, because He is their God. Israel is to keep His Sabbaths and reverence His sanctuary.

经文含义 Meaning

圣洁生活从专一敬拜开始

本章开头回到利未记反复强调的根基：不可用可操控的形像替代永活的神。安息日和圣所把时间与空间都归给耶和华，提醒百姓顺服不是零散规则，而是围绕神同在形成的生活秩序。

The chapter begins with exclusive worship. Israel must not replace the living God with controllable images. Sabbath and sanctuary consecrate time and space, showing that obedience is a life ordered around God’s presence.

灵修应用 Devotional

辨认今日的偶像

现代偶像未必是石像，也可能是成就、控制感、消费欲或他人的认可。当这些东西要求我们牺牲安息、良心和敬拜时，它们就在争夺神的位置。本段邀请我们重新安排时间和忠诚。

Modern idols may be achievement, control, consumption, or approval. When they demand our rest, conscience, and worship, they compete for God’s place. This passage invites us to reorder our time and allegiance.

26:3-5

若以色列人遵行神的律例、谨守祂的诫命，神就按时降雨，地必出产，树木结果；打粮要持续到摘葡萄，摘葡萄要持续到撒种，百姓可以吃饱并安然居住。

If Israel walks in God’s statutes and keeps His commandments, He will give rain in season. The land will yield produce, the trees will bear fruit, and harvest will overlap with vintage and sowing, so the people dwell securely and eat their fill.

经文含义 Meaning

顺服带来受造界的和谐

祝福首先表现为雨水与收成，说明以色列的圣约生活与土地命运相连。经文不是机械地承诺每个敬虔人都永远丰收，而是在圣约群体层面说明：活在神秩序中，会使劳动、土地与供应回到应有的节奏。

Blessing first appears as rain and harvest, linking covenant life with the land. This is not a mechanical promise that every godly person always prospers; it describes the communal order where work, land, and provision align under God.

灵修应用 Devotional

把供应看作恩典

在职场和家庭中，我们容易把成果全归给技术、努力或资源。本段训练我们在丰收时仍保持谦卑：工作要认真，心却不把自己当作最终供应者。感恩能保护我们不被焦虑和贪婪牵引。

At work and home, we easily credit results only to skill, effort, or resources. This text trains humility in seasons of harvest: work diligently, but do not become your own ultimate provider. Gratitude guards against anxiety and greed.

26:6-13

神应许赐平安在地上，使百姓躺卧无人惊吓；恶兽和刀剑要离开。他们要追赶仇敌，人数虽少也能得胜。神要眷顾他们，使他们生养众多，坚定所立的约，并在他们中间立帐幕，作他们的神；祂曾折断埃及的轭，使他们挺身而行。

God promises peace in the land, safety from terror, beasts, and sword, victory over enemies, fruitfulness, covenant confirmation, and His dwelling among them. He reminds them that He broke Egypt’s yoke so they could walk upright.

经文含义 Meaning

祝福的中心是神住在百姓中间

本段从物质供应推进到平安、得胜、生养和神的同住。最高祝福不是粮仓满溢本身，而是“我要在你们中间立我的帐幕”。神折断埃及的轭，显明顺服不是回到奴役，而是在救赎后挺身行走。

The blessings move from provision to peace, victory, fruitfulness, and God’s presence. The highest gift is not full barns alone, but “I will make my dwelling among you.” Obedience is not slavery; it is upright walking after redemption.

灵修应用 Devotional

从被奴役到挺身行走

许多人离开一种辖制后，又被恐惧、比较或旧习惯压弯。神救赎人的目的不只是脱离痛苦，也是恢复尊严和与祂同行的自由。今天可以操练一个具体顺服，作为挺身行走的记号。

Many leave one bondage only to be bent again by fear, comparison, or old habits. God redeems not merely to remove pain but to restore dignity and freedom with Him. Practice one concrete act of obedience today as a sign of upright walking.

26:14-17

若百姓不听从、不遵行诫命，厌弃律例、背弃约，神就使惊惶、疾病和心力衰残临到；他们撒种却被仇敌吃掉，仇敌辖制他们，他们无人追赶也要逃跑。

If Israel refuses to listen and despises God’s statutes, breaking His covenant, terror, wasting sickness, failed labor, enemy rule, and fearful flight will come upon them, even when no one pursues.

经文含义 Meaning

悖逆先破坏内在安稳

咒诅段落先提到惊惶和虚耗，说明背约不只是外在惩罚，也会侵蚀人里面的安稳。拒绝听从神，会使人失去辨认现实的勇气，甚至在无人追赶时也活在逃跑状态。

The curse section begins with terror and wasting, showing that covenant rebellion corrodes inward stability as well as outward circumstances. Refusing God’s voice leaves people fearful, even fleeing when no one pursues.

灵修应用 Devotional

停止用恐惧管理生活

当人远离神，常用控制、逃避或强撑来管理恐惧。经文提醒我们，真正的安稳不能靠更严密的自我保护建立。回转的第一步，是承认恐惧背后的不信，并重新听神的话。

When people drift from God, they often manage fear through control, avoidance, or performance. The text reminds us that security cannot be built by tighter self-protection. Returning begins by naming unbelief beneath fear and listening again.

26:18-26

若百姓仍不听从，神的管教要加重：打碎他们因势力而有的骄傲，使天如铁、地如铜；劳力归于徒然。若继续敌对神，灾祸要加七倍，野兽、刀剑、瘟疫和粮食短缺相继临到。

If Israel still will not listen, discipline intensifies: God will break the pride of their power, make the heavens like iron and the earth like bronze, and make labor fruitless. Continued hostility brings multiplied plagues, beasts, sword, pestilence, and shortage of bread.

经文含义 Meaning

管教递增，为要打碎骄傲

反复出现的“若还不听从”显示这些警告有呼唤性质。神打碎“势力的骄傲”，因为骄傲使人把安全感建在军力、产能或制度上，却拒绝悔改。加重的管教揭露罪会持续扩大破坏。

The repeated “if you still will not listen” gives the warnings a summons-to-return quality. God breaks proud power because pride trusts force, production, and systems while refusing repentance. Escalating discipline exposes sin’s widening damage.

灵修应用 Devotional

让警钟早一点叫醒我们

生活中的阻力不都等于神的管教，但每一次挫折都可以成为省察的入口。成熟不是等到关系、身体、财务全都崩塌才回头，而是在神轻声提醒时就愿意停下、承认和调整。

Not every hardship is divine discipline, yet every disruption can become an occasion for examination. Maturity does not wait until relationships, health, and finances collapse; it listens early, stops, confesses, and adjusts.

26:27-35

若百姓仍敌对神，灾祸会更严厉，城邑荒凉，圣所荒废，地成为荒场；他们要被分散在列邦中。那时地要享受安息，补足从前百姓住在其上却没有守的安息年。

If Israel continues in hostility, severe judgment follows: cities laid waste, sanctuaries desolated, the land made empty, and the people scattered among nations. Then the land will enjoy its Sabbaths, making up for the rest denied while Israel lived on it.

经文含义 Meaning

被忽略的安息仍要被神追讨

这里把背约与土地安息联系起来。人若长期拒绝神为土地和社会设下的界限，最终会失去原本以为可掌控的一切。荒凉不是神喜悦毁坏，而是显明违背圣约秩序的后果。

The text links covenant rebellion with neglected land rest. When people ignore God-given limits for land and society, they eventually lose what they thought they controlled. Desolation reveals the consequence of violating covenant order.

灵修应用 Devotional

尊重神设下的界限

我们也可能长期透支身体、压榨关系、占用安息，直到生活被迫停摆。神设界限不是减少生命，而是保护生命。今天需要问：哪些领域我一直不肯让它休息？

We too can overdraw our bodies, strain relationships, and consume rest until life is forced to stop. God’s limits do not diminish life; they protect it. Ask today: where have I refused to let something rest?

26:36-39

余剩的人在仇敌之地心中衰弱，叶子被风吹的声音也要使他们逃跑；他们要彼此跌倒，无法站立在仇敌面前，并因自己和祖先的罪孽衰败。

Those who remain in enemy lands will have weakened hearts; the sound of a windblown leaf will make them flee. They will stumble over one another and waste away because of their own iniquity and that of their fathers.

经文含义 Meaning

罪使人失去站立能力

经文用风吹叶子的声音描绘极深的恐惧。被掳不只是地理上的远离，也包括心灵的衰弱和群体的互相绊倒。罪的后果会跨代累积，但经文随后也开启认罪与回转之路。

The windblown leaf pictures profound fear. Exile is not only geographical distance; it includes weakened hearts and communal stumbling. Sin’s effects accumulate across generations, yet the next verses open a path of confession and return.

灵修应用 Devotional

面对累积的破碎

有些惧怕来自长期未处理的罪、伤害或家庭模式。属灵成长不是否认这些重量，而是在神面前诚实承认，并寻求真理、群体和必要帮助，让生命重新学习站立。

Some fear grows from long-unaddressed sin, wounds, or family patterns. Spiritual growth does not deny this weight; it honestly brings it before God and seeks truth, community, and needed help so life can learn to stand again.

26:40-46

若他们承认自己和祖先的罪，承认他们敌对神，心未受割礼而谦卑，甘愿接受罪罚，神就要记念祂与雅各、以撒、亚伯拉罕所立的约，也要记念这地。即使他们在仇敌之地，神也不厌弃他们到尽头，因祂是耶和华他们的神。

If they confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers, acknowledge their hostility, humble their uncircumcised hearts, and accept discipline, God will remember His covenant with Jacob, Isaac, and Abraham, and remember the land. Even in enemy territory He will not utterly reject them, for He is the LORD their God.

经文含义 Meaning

最后的话不是咒诅，而是记念

本章严厉，却不以绝望收尾。悔改包括承认罪、谦卑心、接受神公义的管教。盼望来自神主动记念约：祂不会把百姓完全弃绝，因为祂的身份和应许比人的失败更深。

The chapter is severe, but it does not end in despair. Repentance includes confession, humbled hearts, and acceptance of righteous discipline. Hope rests in God’s covenant remembrance: His identity and promise are deeper than human failure.

灵修应用 Devotional

回转仍有路

当人面对失败，最危险的谎言是“已经太迟了”。利未记 26 章说，悔改不是自我惩罚，而是诚实回到守约的神面前。即使后果仍需承担，关系的恢复和盼望仍然真实。

After failure, one dangerous lie says, “It is too late.” Leviticus 26 says repentance is not self-punishment but honest return to the covenant-keeping God. Even when consequences remain, restored relationship and hope are real.

方法论总结 / Methodology

从圣约上下文到今日悔改与盼望

利未记 26 章必须先放在西奈圣约和利未记整体结构中阅读。它不是孤立的奖惩清单，而是圣洁生活说明之后的圣约宣告：神要在百姓中间居住，百姓则以专一敬拜和顺服回应。祝福与咒诅都围绕关系、土地和神同在展开。

Leviticus 26 should first be read within the Sinai covenant and the structure of Leviticus. It is not an isolated reward-and-punishment list, but a covenant declaration after instructions for holy living: God dwells among His people, and they respond with exclusive worship and obedience. Blessing and curse revolve around relationship, land, and divine presence.

正统解经原则 / Contextual Exegesis

第一，观察重复词：“听从”“遵行”“厌弃”“敌对”“记念”。第二，注意文学推进：祝福简洁而完整，咒诅分层加重，最后转向认罪与约。第三，避免把本章直接简化成个人成功公式；它首先是神与以色列圣约群体之间的土地、敬拜与同在条款。

Observe repeated words: listen, walk, reject, hostility, remember. Trace the literary movement from blessing, through escalating discipline, to confession and covenant remembrance. Avoid reducing the chapter to an individual success formula; it first addresses Israel as a covenant people in land, worship, and presence.

灵修应用路径 / Devotional Application

应用时要经过“桥梁原则”：神仍呼召祂的子民专一敬拜、认真听从、尊重界限、在管教中回转，并把盼望建立在祂守约的怜悯上。今天的读者不应把每个困难机械地判定为咒诅，却可以让困难成为省察、认罪和重新信靠的入口。

Application moves through a bridge principle: God still calls His people to exclusive worship, attentive obedience, respect for limits, repentance under discipline, and hope in His covenant mercy. Readers should not mechanically label every hardship a curse, but hardship can become a doorway to examination, confession, and renewed trust.

实际落地 / Practical Use

辨认哪些事物正在夺取神的位置，并重新安排安息、祷告和顺服。 / Identify what competes for God’s place and reorder rest, prayer, and obedience.

在身体、工作、金钱和关系中承认神设下的界限。 / Honor God-given limits in body, work, money, and relationships.
