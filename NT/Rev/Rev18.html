Revelation 18: The Fall of Babylon

巴比伦的倾倒与哀哭

The Fall of Babylon and the Lament of the World

第十八章是一首宏大的挽歌，也是胜利的凯歌。它详细描绘了世界经济与宗教体系（巴比伦）的突然崩溃。地上的君王、客商和船长为这“大城”的毁灭而哀哭，因为他们的财富瞬间化为乌有。然而，天上的声音呼召神的子民“从那城出来”，并为神的公义审判欢喜。

Chapter 18 is both a grand dirge and a victory song. It details the sudden collapse of the world's economic and religious system (Babylon). Earth's kings, merchants, and mariners mourn the destruction of the "Great City" as their wealth vanishes in an hour. Yet, a voice from heaven calls God's people to "come out of her" and rejoice in His righteous judgment.

倾倒的巴比伦 Fallen Babylon

象征抵挡神的世界体系（政治/经济/文化）。它的倾倒被描述为“成了鬼魔的住处”，表明其属灵本质的败坏和彻底的荒凉。

Symbolizes the anti-God world system. Its fall renders it a "dwelling place for demons," indicating spiritual corruption and total desolation.

从那城出来 Come Out of Her

这是给神子民的紧急呼召。不是物理上的迁徙，而是属灵上的分别（Separation）。为了不与她的罪有份，也不受她所受的灾殃。

An urgent call to God's people. Not physical migration, but spiritual separation. To avoid sharing in her sins and receiving her plagues.

一时之间 In One Hour

重复出现的短语，强调审判的突然性和迅速性。世人认为坚不可摧的经济体系，在神的手中瞬间瓦解。

Recurring phrase emphasizing the suddenness and speed of judgment. The world's "invincible" economic system collapses in an instant.

权威释义

Authoritative Exegesis

侧重于与旧约先知书（以赛亚书、耶利米书、以西结书）对古代巴比伦和推罗审判预言的平行对比。
Focusing on parallels with OT prophetic judgments on Babylon and Tyre (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel).

  • 核心原则 Principles
    理解“巴比伦”为经济与文化的集合体，而非单一城市。哀歌反映了世界体系对物质的依赖和神审判的公义。
    Understand "Babylon" as an economic/cultural complex. Laments reflect reliance on materialism and God's just judgment.

灵修默想

Spiritual Devotion

侧重于从世界分别出来的呼召，以及对财富和享乐主义的警惕。
Focusing on the call to separate from the world, and vigilance against wealth and hedonism.

  • 核心原则 Principles
    “从那城出来”是心灵的迁徙。要在世界繁华时看到其必然的倾倒，从而积攒财宝在天上。
    "Come out of her" is a migration of the heart. See the inevitable fall amidst worldly prosperity; store treasures in heaven.

"我的民哪，你们要从那城出来，免得与她一同有罪，受她所受的灾殃。"

"'Come out of her, my people,' so that you will not share in her sins, so that you will not receive any of her plagues."
