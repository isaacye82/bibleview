Revelation 20: The Millennium & The Great White Throne

一千年与白色大宝座

The Thousand Years and The Great White Throne

第二十章揭示了历史终局的三个关键阶段：撒但的捆绑与圣徒的千禧年掌权、撒但的最终释放与毁灭（火湖）、以及白色大宝座前的最终审判。这是正义得到最终伸张、罪恶被彻底清除的时刻。死亡和阴间也被扔进火湖，唯有名字记在生命册上的人得享永生。

Chapter 20 reveals three key stages of the end: the binding of Satan and the Millennial Reign of saints, Satan's final release and destruction (Lake of Fire), and the Final Judgment at the Great White Throne. It is the moment when justice is finally served, evil is utterly purged, and even Death and Hades are cast into the fire. Only those written in the Book of Life inherit eternal life.

Seal of the Abyss

Bound for 1000 Years
捆绑一千年

千禧年 (Millennium) The 1000 Year Reign

象征基督作王、和平与公义的时期。撒但被捆绑，无法迷惑列国。圣徒复活与基督一同作王，这是“头一次的复活”。

Symbolizes Christ's reign of peace and justice. Satan is bound. Saints resurrect and reign; this is the "First Resurrection".

火湖 (Lake of Fire) The Final Destination

也就是“第二次的死”。这是撒但、兽、假先知以及所有名字未记在生命册上之人的最终结局。它是永恒的隔离与刑罚。

The "Second Death". The final destiny of Satan, the Beast, the False Prophet, and all not in the Book of Life. Eternal separation.

白色大宝座 Great White Throne

宇宙终极的法庭。天地都逃避了。所有死人（无论大小）都要站在神面前，根据案卷（行为）和生命册受审判。

The ultimate cosmic court. Earth and sky flee. All dead stand before God, judged by the Books (deeds) and the Book of Life.

解经与应用的方法论

Methodology of Interpretation and Application

权威释义

Authoritative Exegesis

侧重于解析末世论的象征符号：一千年（字面vs象征）、第一次复活（身体vs灵性）、歌革和玛各（以西结书38章的应验）。
Focusing on eschatological symbols: Millennium, First Resurrection, Gog & Magog.

  • 核心原则 Principles
    理解“撒但被捆绑”为对其迷惑列国能力的限制。区分“第一次复活”（信徒）与“死人复活”（最终审判）。
    Understand "Binding" as limiting deception. Distinguish "First Resurrection" (believers) from general resurrection.

灵修默想

Spiritual Devotion

侧重于对神最终得胜的信心、对罪恶结局的警醒，以及对救恩确据的感恩。
Focusing on faith in victory, vigilance against sin, and gratitude for assurance.

  • 核心原则 Principles
    不要被罪恶暂时的释放（迷惑）所动摇。最重要的是确保名字在生命册上，而非仅仅关注末世的时间表。
    Don't be shaken by temporary evil. Prioritize having your name in the Book of Life over knowing timelines.

"若有人名字没记在生命册上，他就被扔在火湖里。"

"Anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire."
